## What this means

On the open chain the frozen band is a single ladder: one mode per balanced pair of rates, carried up rung by rung, and a ceiling that F144 proves by bounding how far a lowest-weight state's ket can agree with its bra. On the ring the ladder breaks, because the wrap bond's sign follows the particle number, and the band's floor survives because the ladder hands its modes back and forth between the ring and the ring threaded by a π flux. This document shows that at odd N the ceiling survives as well, and for a reason that is simpler than the chain's: on a ring the modes are plane waves, a plane wave carries a momentum, and at odd N no two momenta add up to π. That one arithmetic fact removes, for all but exceptional parameters, every way for a state above the bottom rung to freeze on its own, so the whole band is what the corner is, and the corner is one mode per pair of opposite momenta. At even N two momenta do add up to π, and what that resonance turns out to be is the ring's second ladder, the staggered spin ladder of a bipartite ring; it turns the identity's one square into three, the ladder's among them, and raises its top by ℓ, so from N = 8 on the band is again the corner's, generically; and the corner is one mode per pair of balanced rates at every even N as well, generically, on both rings. On the ring threaded by the π flux that last step has a reason of its own: one direction, the inverse of the particle current, is not lifted off the root by the offsets at second order, because at first order they only turn it, and a turn reaches no population; at fourth order the turned current does reach the populations, and the frozen modes could absorb that, class by class, if they could carry the trace, which no frozen mode does.

## 1. Setting

The XY ring of N sites, H = J·Σ_b (σ⁺_bσ⁻_{b+1} + h.c.) over the N bonds of the cycle, with the wrap bond between sites N − 1 and 0 priced by occupation (the spin ring) or by order (the free periodic ring), under Z dephasing at rates γ_l on the R₉₀ locus γ_l + γ_{N−1−l} = 2γ̄ with γ̄ ≠ 0, written γ_l = γ̄ + δ_l with δ_{N−1−l} = −δ_l; the uniform point is δ = 0, uniform rates. Fixing γ̄ costs nothing, since the frozen space depends on J/γ̄ and δ/γ̄ only. At γ̄ = 0 the statement is false: that is F140's zero-mean stratum, where the corner carries N rather than ⌊N/2⌋ ([PROOF_R90_FROZEN_DIVISOR](PROOF_R90_FROZEN_DIVISOR.md) §6) and the vacuum cell sits on the root besides. The joint-popcount block (p, q) holds the cells |A⟩⟨B| with |A| = p and |B| = q, and the rung p is the diagonal block (p, p). Multiplicity means the dimension of ker(L + 4γ̄) on a block, the geometric one; where the algebraic one is meant it is said. On a diagonal block the dissipator is −2Σ_{l∈AΔB}γ_l on the cell, which at uniform rates is −4γ̄·𝒦 with 𝒦 = |A \ B| the disagreement count, and D̂ = |A ∩ B| = p − 𝒦 the double occupancy. Φ(ρ) = Σ_l c_l† ρ c_l and Ψ = Φ† are the η ladder with Jordan-Wigner strings, and LW_ℓ = ker Ψ on the rung ℓ, the lowest-weight vectors there. A **fixed-flux ring** is the free-fermion ring whose single-particle matrix h has wrap entry s = +1 (periodic) or s = −1 (π flux), quantized by order in every sector; its modes are the plane waves φ_k(l) = e^{ikl}/√N with k ∈ (2π/N)·ℤ for s = +1 and k ∈ (π/N)·(2ℤ + 1) for s = −1, at energies 2J·cos k.

## 2. Each rung of the spin ring is a rung of a fixed-flux ring

**Lemma 1.** The spin ring's sector of p particles is the fixed-flux ring's with s = (−1)^(p−1).

*Proof.* The wrap hop σ⁺_{N−1}σ⁻_0 is c†_{N−1}c_0 times the Jordan-Wigner string over the sites 1 to N − 2, which holds the other p − 1 particles. ∎

So the block (p, p) of the spin ring is the block (p, p) of the fixed-flux ring with s = (−1)^(p−1): ket and bra hold the same number of particles, see the same wrap sign, and the rest of L is the same. This is the even-height case of [PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY](PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md) §5 read on one block. The order-priced ring is the fixed-flux ring with s = +1 in every sector. Gate R1 checks the sector matrices entry for entry, N = 3 to 8.

Inside a fixed-flux ring one single-particle matrix serves every sector, so Φ and Ψ commute with L at every rate profile ([PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4](PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4.md) Lemmas 2.1 and 2.2, which hold for any h; Ψ by taking the adjoint of each part, the dissipator and the commutator with H being Hermitian superoperators). The frozen subspace, and the generalized eigenspace of the root as well, is then an sl(2) module along the diagonal, and with n_ℓ the dimension of the frozen subspace inside LW_ℓ on the rung ℓ,

  **frozen dimension on the rung p = Σ_{ℓ=1}^{min(p, N−p)} n_ℓ**

(the argument of Corollary 4.2 there; a multiplet seeded at the rung ℓ spans the rungs ℓ to N − ℓ, and the rung 0 is one cell at rate 0, which sits at −4γ̄ only at γ̄ = 0). Each n_ℓ is the nullity of the stacked matrix [L + 4γ̄; Ψ] on the rung ℓ, whose entries are polynomials in (J, γ̄, δ) over ℤ[i], so n_ℓ takes its smallest value on a dense open set of the locus and can only rise on its complement.

At odd N the two signs are one case: with U = diag((−1)^l), which reverses the sign of every bond except the wrap bond, the wrap joining two even sites, U·h₊·U = −h₋; U is diagonal and so commutes with every rate profile and with Ψ, and conj(L(J)) = L(−J) for real h and real rates, so the two fixed-flux rings carry the same frozen counts on every block. The proofs below are written for both signs anyway, since nothing in them is easier for one.

## 3. The identity on the plane waves

**Lemma 2 (no resonance at odd N).** At odd N, no two allowed momenta of a fixed-flux ring add up to π modulo 2π.

*Proof.* In units of π/N the momenta are even integers for s = +1 and odd integers for s = −1; in both cases two of them add up to an even number, and π is N in those units, odd. ∎

At every even N some pair does, for both signs, 0 + π for s = +1 and π/N + (N − 1)π/N for s = −1 (gate R2 enumerates N = 3 to 41).

**Proposition 3 (the identity).** At odd N, on LW_ℓ ∩ V₀, with V₀ the kernel of the two-copy commutator ad_H on the rung ℓ, and Dmom(ρ) = Σ_k n_k ρ n_k with n_k the occupation of the plane wave k,

  **N·⟨v, D̂ v⟩ = ℓ²·‖v‖² − ⟨v, Dmom v⟩.**

*Proof.* Write n_l = Σ_{k,k'} φ_k(l)* φ_{k'}(l) c†_k c_{k'} and D̂ρ = Σ_l n_l ρ n_l. Summing the four plane waves over l gives δ(k₁ + k₃ ≡ k₂ + k₄)/N for the term c†_{k₁}c_{k₂} ρ c†_{k₃}c_{k₄}, which takes a mode k₂ out of the ket and puts k₁ in, and takes k₃ out of the bra and puts k₄ in. V₀ is spanned by the cells of mode sets with equal Slater energies, and the term stays in V₀ exactly when the two changes of energy agree. Write q = k₁ − k₂ = k₄ − k₃. Then the condition is 2cos(k₂ + q) − 2cos k₂ = 2cos(k₃ + q) − 2cos k₃, that is sin(q/2)·[sin(k₂ + q/2) − sin(k₃ + q/2)] = 0. It holds when q = 0, when k₃ = k₂, or when k₂ + k₃ + q ≡ π, the last ruled out by Lemma 2. The terms with q = 0 sum to N̂ρN̂/N = ℓ²ρ/N. The terms with k₃ = k₂ and q ≠ 0 move one mode k, common to ket and bra, to a mode k' free in both: they are Σ_{k≠k'} Φ_{k'}Ψ_k with Ψ_kρ = c_kρc_k† and Φ_{k'}ρ = c†_{k'}ρc_{k'}, which is ΦΨ − Σ_k Φ_kΨ_k = ΦΨ − Dmom, and ΦΨ vanishes on LW_ℓ. ∎

Gate R3 checks it as an operator identity on LW_ℓ ∩ V₀ at N = 5 and 7, both signs, every rung ℓ ≤ ⌊N/2⌋ (LW_ℓ ∩ V₀ is empty above), and checks that it fails at N = 4 and 6.

**Corollary 4 (the floor).** At odd N, on LW_ℓ ∩ V₀,

  **⟨v, 𝒦 v⟩ ≥ (ℓ(N − ℓ)/N)·‖v‖²,**

with equality exactly on the vectors with no mode common to ket and bra. For N ≥ 5 and 2 ≤ ℓ ≤ ⌊N/2⌋ the bound exceeds 1, the binding case being ℓ = 2, where 2(N − 2) > N reads N > 4.

*Proof.* 𝒦 = ℓ − D̂ on the rung and ⟨v, Dmom v⟩ = Σ_k ‖Ψ_k v‖² ≥ 0. ∎

This is F144's statement with the transform length M = N + 1 of the chain replaced by the ring's N, and with a deficit that is a single square rather than three: the chain's spin-ladder and chiral terms come from the sine modes' reflection a ↦ M − a, whose ring counterpart k ↦ π − k is exactly the resonance Lemma 2 excludes at odd N. Gate R4 reads the minimum as attained.

## 4. Nothing new freezes above the bottom rung

**Proposition 5.** At odd N ≥ 5, n_ℓ = 0 for every 2 ≤ ℓ ≤ ⌊N/2⌋, for both signs, and the root is then absent from L restricted to LW_ℓ on the rung ℓ even counted algebraically: at the uniform point for all but finitely many J, and on the locus for (J, δ) off a proper algebraic subset.

*Proof.* At the uniform point the dissipator on the rung ℓ, shifted by the root, is B = 4γ̄(1 − 𝒦), and L preserves LW_ℓ. With h = J·h₀, H₀ the second quantization of h₀ and s = 1/J, (L + 4γ̄)/J = A + sB with A = −i·ad_{H₀}, the pencil of [PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4](PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4.md) Section 5; restrict it to LW_ℓ. A is anti-Hermitian there, its kernel is LW_ℓ ∩ V₀, and its spectral projectors commute with Ψ. By Corollary 4 the compression of B to LW_ℓ ∩ V₀ has no zero eigenvalue, so by the argument of Proposition 5.1, which counts branches with their algebraic multiplicity, no branch vanishes identically, and by that of Proposition 5.3 the root is absent off a finite set of J. Since n_ℓ, and the determinant of L + 4γ̄ on LW_ℓ, are a nullity and a determinant of matrix polynomials in (J, δ), and the locus is an affine space, the value at one point of it bounds the generic value. ∎

The uniform point serves as the anchor here, where it does not for the corner: above the bottom rung it carries nothing new, while at the corner it carries N − 1 (below). It is an anchor off a finite set of couplings only: at the uniform point at N = 5 the rung 2 does carry one lowest-weight mode at J = (2/√3)·γ̄.

## 5. The corner

On the rung 1 the frozen dimension is n_1, the corner (1,1), a problem in one particle. Its commutant V₀ at odd N is spanned by the N projectors P_k = |φ_k⟩⟨φ_k| and the N − 1 operators O_k = |φ_k⟩⟨φ_{−k}| with k ≢ −k, since 2cos k = 2cos k' only for k' = ±k; one momentum is its own opposite, k = 0 for s = +1 and k = π for s = −1, and the rest come in ⌊N/2⌋ pairs.

**Proposition 6.** At odd N, for both signs, the corner carries exactly ⌊N/2⌋ for (J, δ) off a proper algebraic subset of the locus, and exactly N − 1 at the uniform point for all but finitely many J, in both cases with the algebraic multiplicity equal to the geometric one.

*Proof.* By the argument of Proposition 5.1 there, which needs only A semisimple, the number of branches vanishing identically at large J, counted algebraically, is at most the dimension of the kernel of the compressed rate operator B₁ on V₀, and by that of Proposition 5.3 the count is that number off a finite set of J; so the generic count in J is bounded by dim ker B₁. With Δ = diag(δ_l), Γ = diag(γ_l) and δ̂(q) = (1/N)Σ_l δ_l e^{−iql}, the shifted rate on the corner is X ↦ −2{Δ, X} + 4·diag(Γ·X_diag), and in the coordinates X = Σ_k a_k P_k + Σ_k b_k O_k, with S = Σ_k a_k, its compression reads

  on P_k: −2[δ̂(2k) b_{−k} + δ̂(−2k) b_k] + (4/N)[γ̄ S + Σ_j b_j δ̂(−2j)],
  on O_k: −2δ̂(2k)(a_k + a_{−k}) + (4/N)[γ̄ b_k + S δ̂(2k) + Σ_j b_j δ̂(2k − 2j)],

using δ̂(0) = 0 on the locus and that 2j ≡ 2k only for j = k at odd N. At the uniform point δ = 0 the kernel is {b = 0, S = 0}, of dimension N − 1: the traceless combinations of the P_k, which commute with h and have zero diagonal, and so are frozen at every J. With a profile, the ⌊N/2⌋ antisymmetric combinations a_k = −a_{−k}, b = 0, stay in the kernel for every δ: every term above vanishes on them. For the bound from above, perturb from the uniform point along εδ, small multiples of one profile; B₁ is Hermitian, the compression of a real diagonal operator, and analytic in ε, so the eigenvalues leaving zero do so at the first order that does not vanish on the uniform kernel. The first-order operator vanishes on the uniform kernel, since its image there lies on the O_k, orthogonal to the P_k. The second-order one, through the O_k on which the uniform operator is (4γ̄/N)·1, acts on the uniform kernel as a ↦ −(N/4γ̄)·8|δ̂(2k)|²·(a_k + a_{−k}), projected onto the traceless combinations; on the self-opposite momentum δ̂(2k) = δ̂(0) = 0. Its kernel is the antisymmetric part alone whenever δ̂(2k) ≠ 0 for every pair: the image vanishes only if it is constant across k, hence zero, since its entry at the self-opposite momentum is, so every pair sum a_k + a_{−k} vanishes, and tracelessness then removes the self-opposite coefficient as well. ## 7. Even N: the resonance opens the second ladder

**Proposition 3′ (the identity at even N).** At even N, for both signs, on LW_ℓ ∩ V₀, with Y_{ab} := c_a v c_b†, g_a := Y_{aa} and the staggered ladder 𝔖⁻v := Σ_b c_{b̄} v c_b,

  **N·⟨v, D̂ v⟩ = ℓ(ℓ + 1)·‖v‖² − ‖𝔖⁻v‖² − ½·Σ_a ‖g_a + g_ā‖² − Σ_{a ≠ ā} ‖Y_{aā}‖².**

*Proof.* D̂ = Σ_l n_l · n_l gives ⟨v, D̂ v⟩ = Σ_l ‖c_l v c_l†‖², and expanding c_l in the plane waves, ⟨v, D̂ v⟩ = (1/N)·Σ ⟨Y_{cd}, Y_{ab}⟩ over a − b ≡ c − d: every four-mode overlap is 1/N, where the chain's are the overlaps T of PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4 (7.1). The third set is the involution of the chain's grade lemma (PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4 Lemma 7.1). Sum over the union of the three sets. With A_{bb'} := c†_{b̄}c_{b̄'}, cyclicity of the trace gives Ξ = Σ_{b,b'} Tr(v†A_{bb'}v c_b†c_{b'}) and ‖𝔖⁻v‖² = Σ_{b,b'} Tr(v†A_{bb'}v c_{b'}c_b†), and c_{b'}c_b† = δ_{bb'} − c_b†c_{b'} with Σ_b A_{bb} = N̂ gives ‖𝔖⁻v‖² = ℓ‖v‖² − Ξ. *The turn.* With Λ := ½·h·j⁻¹ = Σ_k cot k·P_k and φ the solution of φ_{l+1} − 2φ_l + φ_{l−1} = δ_l on the ring, which exists since δ̂(0) = 0 and whose constant drops out,

  **{Δ, j⁻¹} = [h, [φ, Λ]].**

In the plane waves, with q := k − k′, the left side has the entry δ̂(q)·(1/sin k + 1/sin k′) = 2δ̂(q)·sin((k + k′)/2)·cos(q/2)/(sin k·sin k′), and the right side φ̂(q)·(ε_k − ε_{k′})·(cot k′ − cot k) = −4φ̂(q)·sin((k + k′)/2)·sin(q/2)·sin q/(sin k·sin k′); the two agree because φ̂(q) = δ̂(q)/(−4sin²(q/2)) at q ≢ 0, and both vanish at q = 0. Since φ is diagonal, i[φ, Λ] is the first-order change of Λ under the site phases e^{iφ}, a turn about the z axis by the angles φ_l, and it has no diagonal; so K₀ acts on it as −iJ[h, ·], and since w has no diagonal either,

  **K₁w = K₀y,  y := −(2i/J)·[φ, Λ]:**

the offset moves the inverse current only by the Hamiltonian's image of a turn about the z axis, which holds no population. y has no part at q = 0 and so none in Z, which lies there, and R₀K₁w = y. This is one level below the remark of [PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY](PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md) at its Zeno and Hamiltonian ends, that a Hamiltonian perturbation of the dephaser's kernel has no dissipative part to give at first order: here the dissipative perturbation's first image on w is itself Hamiltonian, and its dissipative part waits for the next step.

*The cost.* Z is spanned by real matrices, K₀ᵀ = K₀ and K₁ is real and diagonal in the cells, so R₀ᵀ = R₀ and every W_n is a symmetric bilinear form on real vectors. w is i times a real matrix, so with w unnormalized ⟨w, W₄w⟩ = yᵀK₁R₀K₁y and W₃w = P_Z K₁R₀K₁y, and c₄ = −(β(y, y) − β(y, Z′)·β(Z′, Z′)⁻¹·β(Z′, y))/‖w‖², the Schur complement of y in the form β(a, b) := −aᵀK₁R₀K₁b on Z′ ⊕ span(y), Z′ the complement of w in Z, where β(Z′, Z′) is W₂ there. The reflection fixes the class sums and y and reverses the differences t_C, and β is even under it, so the differences drop out and Z′ may be replaced by Z₊, the traceless combinations of class sums. On Z₊ ⊕ span(y) every vector is real and fixed by v ↦ ΣvΣ, which maps K₀ to its complex conjugate and K₁ to itself, so the imaginary part of R₀ couples nothing there, and its real part is ½(R₀ + R₀†) = R₀·4γ̄·diag·R₀†. So on that space β(u, u) = −4γ̄·‖diag(R₀†K₁u)‖², the mirror image of [PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY](PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md) Lemma 3, where the Hermitian part sits on the coherences and the Schur complement is taken onto the populations; here it sits on the populations, and

  **c₄ = (4γ̄/‖w‖²)·min_{z∈Z₊} ‖diag(R₀†K₁(y + z))‖²:**

c₄ has the sign of γ̄, and its size is the populations that the second excursion of the turned current must reach once the kernel has absorbed what it can.

*The resonant sectors.* That minimum is at least its part in the resonant sectors, and there it is explicit. In the frame v the sector q holds the cells |k⟩⟨k − q|, K₀ is A + 4γ̄·ddᵀ on it with d := N^{−1/2}·Σ_k |k⟩⟨k − q|, and the coefficient of diag(x) at the momentum q is dᵀx. At a resonant q the direction ζ̂ of Proposition 7, here the normalized sum of the cells |q/2⟩⟨−q/2| and |π + q/2⟩⟨π − q/2|, is annihilated by A and by A†, so ζ̂ᵀ(A + 4γ̄·ddᵀ)x = 4γ̄η·dᵀx with η = (2/N)^{1/2}, for K₀ and its adjoint alike: the population of the solution is ζ̂ᵀb/(4γ̄η), whatever the source b in U = +1. For z = Σ_k s_k·P_k in Z₊ the two cells receive −2δ̂(q)·(s_{q/2} + s_{−q/2}) and −2δ̂(q)·(s_{π+q/2} + s_{π−q/2}), together −4δ̂(q)·Y_C, with Y_C the class sum of Proposition 7 over the class C of q/2; this holds for the classes of two orbits of two momenta each and for the class {π/2, 3π/2} at q = π, whose two orbits are single momenta and whose cells each carry its whole sum. For y the same plane-wave algebra, pairing p with −p, gives ⟨q/2|{Δ, y}|−q/2⟩ = i·Θ(q)/(J·sin(q/2)), and the same on the second cell, with

  **Θ(q) := Σ_p g(q/2 − p)·g(q/2 + p),  g(u) := δ̂(u)/sin(u/2),  g(0) := 0,**

summed over the allowed p, the arguments taken as real numbers, on which the product does not depend. So the population that R₀†K₁(y + z) holds at the resonant q is −(N^{1/2}/(2γ̄))·δ̂(q)·(Y_C − μ_C) with μ_C := −i·Θ(q)/(J·sin(q/2)·δ̂(q)), and z is traceless: Σ_k s_k = Σ_C |O_C|·Y_C = 0, |O_C| the size of the class's orbits. On the locus e^{−iq/2}·δ̂(q) is imaginary at every q = 2πm/N, so g(u) lies in e^{iu/2}·iℝ, Θ(q) in e^{iq/2}·ℝ, and μ_C is real and the same at q and −q: class by class, Y_C = μ_C cancels y's resonant population exactly.

*The single pair.* At δ₁, g(u) = −(2i/N)·e^{iu/2} at every u ≢ 0, and the two terms p = ±q/2 drop out, so Θ(q) = −(4(N − 2)/N²)·e^{iq/2} and μ_C = −2(N − 2)/(NJ·sin²(q_C/2)). These share the sign of −J, so Σ_C |O_C|·μ_C ≠ 0 and no traceless z meets them all. The resonant part of the cost is a sum over the classes of |Y_C − μ_C|² with positive weights, which does not vanish on the hyperplane Σ_C |O_C|·Y_C = 0, so its minimum there is positive, and c₄ ≠ 0, of the sign of γ̄. ∎

What keeps the inverse current off the root is the trace. Class by class the kernel could absorb what the turned current spends on the populations in the resonant sectors; what it cannot do is absorb all of it at once, because a frozen mode holds no trace: the trace is the left eigenvector of a trace-preserving generator at the eigenvalue 0, so every eigenmode at −4γ̄ is traceless, the general fact behind [PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4](PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4.md) Lemma 3.1 on the chain's corner. In the nonresonant sectors z in Z₊ leaves no population, Proposition 7's X_r reading only the differences t_C, and y spends nothing there either, which is read (gate R11); with that read the minimum is the rank-one remainder of the trace constraint,

  **c₄ = (2/(NJ²γ̄))·|τ|²/Σ_C (|O_C|²/ω_C),  τ := Σ_C |O_C|·Θ(q_C)/(sin(q_C/2)·δ̂(q_C)),**

ω_C := Σ_q |δ̂(q)|² over the resonant q with q/2 ∈ C, read against the definition at N = 4 to 20. It has the sign of γ̄ and, away from the poles where some δ̂(q_C) vanishes, vanishes exactly where τ does; on the locus τ is i times a real function of the profile, so that is a real hypersurface of the locus, on which c₄ is read to drop to rounding, and δ₁ lies off it at every N, its τ = −(2i(N − 2)/N)·Σ_C |O_C|/sin²(q_C/2) summing terms of one sign. Θ is, up to a factor of modulus 4 and the phase of the half step, the transform of the square of the integrated offset, ψ with ψ_{l+½} − ψ_{l−½} = δ_l on the bonds and zero mean, so c₄ is set by how the square of that bond profile meets the resonant momenta.

**On the letter cube.** The turn reads on [the letter cube](../THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) (§7, §8) as two of its moves. A coherence |l⟩⟨m| of the corner carries X or Y at l and m and I or Z everywhere else, so it sits at height k_Z = 2, while the populations |l⟩⟨l|, strings of I and Z alone, lie on the quiet edge k_Z = 0. The transpose, the sign (−1)^{n_Y} on the strings ([F118](../ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f118)'s D), sorts the coherences further: the real symmetric combinations are built from X_lX_m + Y_lY_m, the bond's letters, and are even, the imaginary antisymmetric ones from X_lY_m − Y_lX_m, the current's letters, and are odd. w and Λ are odd, y is even, and the step from Λ to y is a commutator with the Z field φ. Every Hamiltonian step with real letters flips the transpose sign ([F114](../ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f114)), the field's as much as the bond's, and the dissipator keeps it, so both sides of the turn are odd, {Δ, w} = −½K₁w by one dissipative step and [h, [φ, Λ]] by two Hamiltonian ones. What singles out the field step is §8's sentence on a one-site term: it keeps k_Z, moving a letter between X and Y along a face diagonal, and the quiet edge lies in its kernel; the image of a commutator with a Hermitian operator is orthogonal to that kernel, so it has no part on the quiet edge, whatever it acts on. That is why y holds no population; in the plane waves the same fact is the telescoping Σ_k (cot(k − q) − cot k) = 0, a shift by q mapping the allowed momenta onto themselves.

The bond supplies the rest, by the move of §8 through which the watching is felt, the bond turning a one-site population into the spin current: with ψ_{l+½} = φ_{l+1} − φ_l, the integrated offset above, and j_{l+½} := (i/2)·h_{l,l+1}·(|l+1⟩⟨l| − |l⟩⟨l+1|) the current on the bond, so that Σ_l j_{l+½} = j,

  i[φ, h] = 2·Σ_l ψ_{l+½}·j_{l+½},

the one excitation's continuity equation summed against φ, and since [h, Λ] = 0 the Jacobi identity writes the turn as {Δ, j⁻¹} = i[i[φ, h], Λ]. The offset is diagonal on the strings and moves nothing, but with a rate per site its charge on a coherence |l⟩⟨m|, l ≠ m, is −2(δ_l + δ_m), a function of the sites and not of the point, so the cube's coordinates do not see it; it enters site by site, as the divergence of the bond profile ψ, and on the inverse current it acts as the commutator of the ψ-weighted current with Λ. The energy shell, the entries between plane waves of equal energy, k′ = ±k, has two halves. Forward, k′ = k, the turn's left side vanishes because δ̂(0) = 0, the zero mean that lets φ exist; backward, k′ = −k, because 1/sin k + 1/sin(−k) = 0, the inverse current being odd under reversing the direction of travel while the energy is even. A matrix that vanishes on the shell is a commutator with h, so the offset's first image on w had to be Hamiltonian in shape; what the turn adds is a preimage with no population. The phases e^{iφ} are a gauge: conjugation by them commutes with Z dephasing at every rate profile and puts ψ on the bonds, and Σ_l ψ_{l+½} = 0 leaves the π flux untouched, the one bond phase no gauge removes and the one that makes j invertible. Such a conjugation relates two Hamiltonians at the same rates, as do the turns about Z of [§9](../THE_ONE_SQUARE.md#9-the-moves-and-the-two-questions), which carry DM phases away on a tree, and the diagonal phase gauges of [the colouring page](../../experiments/THE_PALINDROME_AS_A_COLOURING.md)'s frame theorem, and never two rate profiles; so none absorbs the offset, and what the identity finds is that on w alone, at first order, the offset does what the bond makes of a turn of Λ. [F168](../ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f168)'s twisted coupling, in [the cat under a turned field](../../experiments/THE_CAT_UNDER_A_TURNED_FIELD.md), meets the same two-site letters, X_aX_b + Y_aY_b and X_aY_b − Y_aX_b, from the other side: there the turns that bring every field onto X leave a residual twist on each bond, and at order 7 the rates see the current it drives through the square of its divergence, a circulation without sources escaping them; here the offset of the rates is itself a divergence. The two are noted together and not claimed as one mechanism.

Gate R11 checks the turn as a matrix identity at N = 4 to 24 on random zero-mean offsets, to the size of a rounding, with the inverse Laplacian dropped as the control that must miss; the same turn on the letter cube, i[φ, h] exactly the bond currents weighted by 2ψ, the Jacobi form and both halves of the shell to rounding, with {Δ, h}, whose backward shell entries do not vanish, as the control, the bond currents summing to j, and, in Pauli strings at N = 4 and 6, w, Λ and y wholly at k_Z = 2 with their whole norm, w and Λ of odd n_Y and y of even n_Y; K₁w = K₀y in the corner, with y exactly free of populations and, to rounding, of Z; the decoupling of y from the differences on the locus, which off the locus fails as the control; c₄ by its definition against the population cost minimized over Z₊ and over Z′, at N = 4 to 20 on random locus profiles and couplings, all three through one pseudo-inverse of K₀; the populations of y and of z in Z₊ at the resonant q against the two formulas above, and μ_C real on the locus; at δ₁, the value by hand against c₄ by its definition and by the closed form at N = 4 to 24, its sign following γ̄, and against the smallest eigenvalue of the corner's U = +1 block over ε⁴ at N = 4 to 12, a route with no pseudo-inverse, whose deviation falls as ε² at three offsets; and, read, the closed form, the nonresonant silence of y, and the zero of c₄ where τ changes sign away from its poles at N = 6, 8 and 10.

**What this gives.** At even N ≥ 8, by the sum formula of Section 2 and Proposition 5′, every diagonal rung p of the spin ring carries n_1 of the fixed-flux ring with s = (−1)^(p−1), and every rung of the order-priced ring carries n_1 of the periodic ring, off a proper algebraic subset of the locus and, at the uniform point, for all but finitely many J. At the uniform point that is Proposition 6′'s pair of numbers, so the spin ring's rungs alternate, 3N/2 − 2 on the odd rungs and 3N/2 − 1 on the even ones; gate R9 reads 10 and 11 on the rungs 1 and 2 at N = 8. On the locus, by the split and Propositions 7 and 8, the corner of either fixed-flux ring carries exactly N/2, so every rung of the order-priced ring and every rung of the spin ring carries exactly N/2 at every even N ≥ 8, geometrically and algebraically, off a proper algebraic subset.

## 8. What is not proved

**Even N.** At even N what stays open lies below N = 8 and in the exceptional set. Gate R6 reads the corner as N/2 exactly at N = 4 to 20 on both rings, on one profile each, a second route to Propositions 7 and 8 at those sizes. Proposition 6's large-J argument does not close at even N on either ring: the large-J kernel exceeds N/2 by one mode per orbit of the group generated by k ↦ −k and k ↦ k + π, whose orbits are Proposition 7's classes, on the momenta that are not their own opposites, read in floats at N = 4 to 14 (gate R9); Propositions 7 and 8 go around it at finite J. Nor does [PROOF_R90_FROZEN_DIVISOR](PROOF_R90_FROZEN_DIVISOR.md) §7 carry over: its cofactor theorem (§6) needs only a real, symmetric, reversal-invariant h and so holds on the ring, but the leading coefficient that §7 computes vanishes there, since for each pair ±k the two standing waves of one energy and opposite reflection parity give a vector of V₋ that K annihilates. Below the bound of Corollary 4′, at N = 4 and 6, the rungs above the corner can carry lowest weight of their own: at the uniform point the periodic ring at N = 6 does at the rung 2 (gate R7, the smaller of two nullities modulo primes, an upper bound, and a float SVD read inside a moat, an independent second route), and on the locus the middle rung at N = 4 carries 4, its extra two read as lowest weight at the rung 2 of the π-flux ring (gate R7), while N = 6 reads ⌊N/2⌋ on every rung ([XY_FROZEN_BAND](../../experiments/XY_FROZEN_BAND.md)). Why N = 4 overfills is open; three facts stand beside it, that at N = 4 the frozen root −4γ̄ and its fold partner (4 − 2N)γ̄ coincide ([XY_FROZEN_BAND](../../experiments/XY_FROZEN_BAND.md), the paragraph on N = 4), that the π-flux ring at N = 4 has the most degenerate one-particle spectrum on record, ±√2 each twice ([PROOF_STRUCTURAL_CEILING](PROOF_STRUCTURAL_CEILING.md) Section 4), and that the same spectrum makes the ring's block (2, 2) the one exception to gap dominance, its two-fermion top √2 + √2 = 2√2·J riding the −2γ floor ([PROOF_RING_GAP_DOMINANCE](PROOF_RING_GAP_DOMINANCE.md), the N = 4 exception). That last one sits at a different root and so in different modes; it shares the block and the spectrum, not a mechanism anyone has shown.

**The exceptional set.** As on the chain, the statements are generic: off a finite set of couplings at each generic profile, and off a proper algebraic subset jointly. That subset is not only a set of couplings. At composite N it holds profiles that keep an excess at every coupling read: an offset carried by the single pair (l, N − 1 − l) gives the corner ⌊N/2⌋ + (gcd(2l + 1, N) − 1)/2, read at N = 9, 15 and 21 for every l, both signs and two couplings (gate R8, nullities modulo primes, upper bounds above the floor); there δ̂(q) = (e^{−iql} − e^{−iq(N−1−l)})/N vanishes on the nonzero q whose order divides gcd(2l + 1, N), so Proposition 6's second-order lift misses those pairs. Their number, (gcd(2l + 1, N) − 1)/2, is F157's Heisenberg blind-seat count at the seat l, and the excess read above the floor matches it; the two meet at the count and at one harmonic, not at the modes. For q = 2πm/N, which is where every 2k lies, the offset's transform is δ̂(q) = −(2i/N)·e^{iq/2}·sin(q(l + ½)), so the pair ±k is missed where the harmonic cos(q(l + ½)) is 1 or −1; F157's modes on the uniform open Heisenberg chain are ψ_m(j) ∝ cos(πm(2j + 1)/(2N)), m = 0..N − 1, with ψ_m(l)² ∝ 1 + cos(q(l + ½)) for the same q, so the mode m has a node at the seat l where that harmonic is −1, the node condition (2l + 1)m ≡ N (mod 2N) of [F157](../ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f157). In a missed pair {m, N − m} with m(2l + 1) = tN the partner carries t' = 2l + 1 − t, of the other parity, which is the complementarity f_{N−m} = −f_m of the chain's one-magnon densities ([PROOF_UNIFORM_LAW](PROOF_UNIFORM_LAW.md) B0, f_m(l) being this harmonic), and t odd is the node condition; so each missed pair holds exactly one blind Heisenberg mode of the seat l and each blind mode lies in one missed pair, and both counts are (gcd(2l + 1, N) − 1)/2. Gate R8 checks in integers to N = 199 that F157's node law picks exactly one member of each missed pair, and reads in floats to N = 31, inside a moat, that the zeros of δ̂ are the missed set and the zeros of the chain's eigenvectors at the seat are the node law's set, which tells the two members of a pair apart at the 14 seats there with gcd(2l + 1, N) > 1. What is joined is the count and the harmonic: F157 counts eigenstates of an open chain that one watched seat cannot see, this section counts momentum pairs of the ring whose second-order lift a two-site offset misses, and the excess above the floor stays a read. The algebraic count has exceptions of its own: on a locus profile at N = 5 the corner's root turns defective, one Jordan block of size two, at a pair of real couplings ±J* while the geometric count stays ⌊N/2⌋, the ring's counterpart of the defective corners of [PROOF_R90_FROZEN_DIVISOR](PROOF_R90_FROZEN_DIVISOR.md) §9; that is read, not gated.

**The off-diagonal band.** The ring's off-diagonal ladders, ⌊N/2⌋ at even N and empty at odd N in the band note's table, are not treated here.
