## Abstract

The girth ladder turned "is this Hamiltonian's palindrome hard, and at which rung?" into moment algebra: the tower t_j(l) = Tr(Z_l H^j) is silent below its first nonzero rung k, and k bounds the hardness moment, m\* ≤ 2k + 1, with a sum-of-squares coefficient. When the bound is tight, and when p_{m\*} is the positive monomial that makes it hard at every γ > 0, is open: k = ℓ suffices and is not needed. This note makes the tower's deg-1 face a *hardware observable*, and the instrument is nothing we add to the device: it is the device's own amplitude damping.

The engine is one line. In the standard noise model (Z-dephasing + amplitude damping), amplitude damping is the unique non-unital piece, and its pump direction is a pure local Z: D[σ⁻_l](I) = +γZ_l (the same (Z_l, I) matrix entry that [F82](PROOF_F82_T1_DISSIPATOR_CORRECTION.md) identified as the dissipator's entire Π²-antisymmetric content), with D[σ⁺_l](I) = −γZ_l, so a thermal device pumps with the **net vacuum rate** Δγ_l = γ↓_l − γ↑_l of [F84](PROOF_F84_AMPLITUDE_DAMPING.md). At the maximally mixed state everything else in the generator annihilates the state, so for any measured observable A,

  **d/dt ⟨A⟩ |_{ρ = I/d} = (1/d) · Σ_l Δγ_l · Tr(A Z_l),**

exactly. Choose A = H^j and the right side is (1/d)·Σ_l Δγ_l·t_j(l): the slope of the j-th energy moment under nothing but the chip's own damping **is the moment tower, read linearly**, rung by rung. The slope is dephasing-blind, evolution-blind, and closes at detailed balance; the first rung is [F113](PROOF_F113_COEFFICIENT_DERIVATION.md) itself, with the exact constant asymmetry = −4^N·slope⟨H⟩; the curvature one order up is *exactly affine* in the generator and fingerprints X/Y-flavored parasites linearly, the complementary lens to F113's Z-drive reader. The certificate is honestly one-sided: the tower's first nonzero rung k proves hardness at some m\* ≤ 2k + 1; a silent deg-1 tower proves nothing about softness (the 4-body pair IIXY+ZXZY is silent at every rung, so it has no k at all, and is hard at m\* = 11 through its deg-5 class).

## §1 The law

Work with L = −i[H,·] + Σ_l γ^φ_l D[Z_l] + Σ_l γ↓_l D[σ⁻_l] + Σ_l γ↑_l D[σ⁺_l], σ⁻ = (X+iY)/2, D[c](ρ) = cρc† − ½{c†c, ρ}, on N qubits (d = 2^N).

**Theorem (pump-slope law).** d/dt Tr(A e^{Lt}(I/d)) at t = 0 equals (1/d)·Σ_l Δγ_l·Tr(A Z_l) with Δγ_l = γ↓_l − γ↑_l, for every observable A and every H.

*Proof.* The slope is Tr(A·L(I))/d. The commutator kills I. Dephasing is unital: D[Z_l](I) = Z_l I Z_l − I = 0. Amplitude damping is the non-unital piece: D[σ⁻](I) = σ⁻σ⁺ − ½{σ⁺σ⁻, I} = |0⟩⟨0| − |1⟩⟨1| = +Z per damped site, and D[σ⁺](I) = −Z likewise. So L(I) = Σ_l Δγ_l Z_l. ∎

With A = H^j: **slope_j = (1/d)·Σ_l Δγ_l·t_j(l)**, the deg-1 tower of the girth ladder, weighted by the per-site net damping. The pump is the same object twice over: F82's Π²-antisymmetric entry (the only place the standard dissipator breaks the palindrome's antisymmetric lens) and the generator of this channel. The palindrome-breaking direction and the tower-reading direction are one operator.

## §2 The three blindnesses

All exact, all one-line consequences of the proof above:

1. **Dephasing-blind.** γ^φ_l never enters the slope (unital). A device with any dephasing profile reads the same tower.
2. **Evolution-blind.** The slope does not contain the generator's Hamiltonian at all; only the *measured polynomial* A = H_p^j enters. Changing the evolution H, even entirely, leaves the slope fixed. (This is what pushes the chip's actual Hamiltonian to the curvature, §5, and it is a feature: the slope is a clean baseline that cannot be contaminated.)
3. **Detailed-balance closure.** At γ↓ = γ↑ the pump vanishes and every rung reads zero: the channel is powered exactly by [F84](PROOF_F84_AMPLITUDE_DAMPING.md)'s temperature-independent vacuum component. Read that the way F84 states it, and not as a temperature knob. For a **bosonic** bath γ↓ = γ₀(n̄+1) and γ↑ = γ₀n̄, so Δγ = γ₀ at **every** temperature: thermal photons enter both rates symmetrically and cancel, and γ↓ = γ↑ is reached at no temperature at all, the difference staying γ₀ while only the ratio γ↑/γ↓ climbs toward 1. So against a photon bath an equilibrated device does not lose the channel and a cold one does not gain it. That premise is F84's and it is not every bath: for a two-level-system or quasiparticle bath the two rates approach each other as the bath saturates and Δγ does go to zero, which is why §7 keeps the null as an instrumentation question rather than a temperature one.

## §3 Rung one is F113

Within [F113](PROOF_F113_COEFFICIENT_DERIVATION.md)'s scope (H = Σ_l (ω_l/2)Z_l, amplitude damping γ_T1 and pumping γ_pump), two quantities derived in different worlds coincide up to a dimension factor. F113's static Frobenius polarity asymmetry, ‖M₊‖² − ‖M₋‖² = (4^N/2)·Σ_l ω_l(γ_pump,l − γ_T1,l), and this channel's dynamic slope of the energy, slope⟨H⟩ = (1/d)·Σ_l Δγ_l·(ω_l/2)·d = ½·Σ_l Δγ_l ω_l. Comparing:

  **asymmetry = −4^N · slope⟨H⟩**  (the three routes agree to 5e-16 at N = 2, 3, which is what the anchor prints). The MAGNITUDE is the bridge; the minus is not physics but the pipeline's stacking pairing, the same fence F113 carries, since slope⟨H⟩ is a rate while the asymmetry's sign follows the pairing convention. Under the matched pairing the same run returns +4^N·slope⟨H⟩.

The bridge is worth pausing on. F113's left side is a property of the generator's matrix in operator space, a Frobenius norm imbalance between Π-eigenspaces, with no time in it anywhere. The right side is the initial rate of change of a measured energy. That a static spectral-coordinate imbalance *is* a measurable pump rate (times −4^N) is the channel's claim to the name "F113-analogue" made literal: **the moment-tower channel at rung 1 is F113**, and rungs j ≥ 2 are its ladder, which is exactly what the [connection hunt](PROOF_PI_FACTORS_AS_R_TIMES_D.md) ordered.

One reading the bridge does **not** license: that either side is a frame-free property of the drive. Both are lab-frame quantities, and a change of rotating frame shifts every single-site Z amplitude, hence the asymmetry. The numbers in §4 are stated in the frame in which the site energies are defined.

## §4 The certificate, honestly one-sided

Scan j = 1, 2, 3, …. Two rungs are in play and they are not the same one: the tower's first nonzero rung, which is a property of H alone, and the first rung whose measured **slope** fires, which also depends on Δγ and can be strictly later (the second fence below gives t₁ ≠ 0 whose slope reads exactly zero). Write k for the tower's first nonzero rung. Since t_j = 0 for every j < k, the binomial supertrace sum collapses to its middle term and p_{2k+1} carries the sum-of-squares coefficient P_{2k+1,1} = (2k+1)·C(2k,k)·Σ_l t_k² > 0. So a firing rung certifies **hardness**, and it bounds the hardness moment:

  **m\* ≤ 2k + 1.**

**It does not give m\*, and k is not the girth.** The [girth dichotomy](../../simulations/f87_girth_dichotomy.py)'s ℓ is 1 for a nonzero diagonal and otherwise the shortest odd cycle of the hopping graph (with 0 the sentinel for bipartite), so apart from that sentinel it is odd; k is under no such constraint. The bound is sometimes tight and sometimes not, and what decides it is open. k = ℓ is enough, since below the girth every class is dead, but it is not needed: the flight's own witness H_p = X₀ + X₀Z₁ + 0.7·X₁X₂ has no diagonal and a bipartite hopping graph, so ℓ = 0 while k = 2, and p₅ = 7680·γ is a positive monomial, m\* = 5 = 2k + 1 exactly. What can happen at k > ℓ is that a higher-degree class fires below 2k + 1, and sometimes does. H = Y₂ + Z₀Z₁Y₂ + Z₀Z₁Z₂ at N = 3 has t₁ = t₂ = 0 and t₃ = (0, 0, 16), so k = 3 and the bound reads 7, but H has a nonzero diagonal, so ℓ = 1 and p₅ = 7680·γ³ already fires: m\* = 5. The **hard at every γ > 0** clause travels with the same condition, since what carries it is p_{m\*} being a positive monomial; the dichotomy's monomial argument thresholds on ℓ, but H_p above shows the monomial can appear without k = ℓ, so this too is a sufficient reading and not a criterion. Here p₇ = 35840·γ − 322560·γ³ + 161280·γ⁵ is not monomial and changes sign between γ = 0.25 and γ = 0.4; the moment that is positive throughout is p₅, not the certificate's own. So read a firing rung as: hard, at a moment no later than 2k + 1, exactly 2k + 1 when the girth rung is the one that fired.

Three honesty fences:

- **Silence is not softness.** The channel reads only the d = 1 face of [F117](../ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md)'s class decomposition. Hardness can live entirely in higher-leg classes: the 4-body pair IIXY+ZXZY has t_j(l) = 0 for every j, so no first nonzero rung at all, and every site, yet is hard at m\* = 11 through its deg-5 class (p₁₁ = 86507520·γ⁵). A silent deg-1 tower through j_max certifies only that the deg-1 face is dead through m = 2j_max + 1. (The weighted ceiling family behaves the same way: the off-line witness (1,2,1) fires at m\* = 11 in the deg-3 class with its deg-1 tower silent.)
- **Site sums can cancel.** The raw slope reads Σ_l Δγ_l t_k(l), which can vanish accidentally even when t_k ≠ 0 sitewise. Only the second half of this closes the gap: a distinct Δγ_l profile still yields one weighted combination (the next fence gives a non-uniform profile that returns exactly zero), so what resolves t_k(l) individually is deliberately damping one qubit at a time, a weak partial reset on site l. The certificate in §6 uses the resolved form.
- **A vanishing total moment is not a silent channel.** The mirror of the fence above. The slope reads the inner product of the two profiles, (1/d)·Σ_l Δγ_l t_k(l), which is proportional to the bare total Σ_l t_k(l) only at uniform rates; the firing condition is Σ_l Δγ_l t_k(l) ≠ 0. Neither a nonzero total moment nor a non-uniform rate profile is that condition, and each fails in both directions: a zero-total moment fires against a rate profile it is not orthogonal to, a nonzero one is silenced by a moment profile of mixed sign, uniform rates fire on a nonzero total, and a non-uniform profile orthogonal to the moments gives exactly zero. The device being cold does not rescue the total: the signs at issue are the moments', which are a property of H, so all-positive Δγ_l still leaves the two profiles free to be orthogonal (at N = 2, ω = (2, −1) against Δγ = (1, 2) reads exactly zero at a total moment of +2). Rung 1 exhibits the two zero-total legs: a C₄ XX+YY ring with site energies Σ_l α_l n_l, n_l = (I − Z_l)/2, α = (+0.1, −0.1, +0.1, −0.1), so ω_l = −α_l and Σ_l Tr(Z_l H) = 0 exactly, at γ_pump = 0 reads asymmetry 0.000000 for γ_T1 = (1, 1, 1, 1), −25.600000 for (1, 2, 1, 2), and 0.000000 again for the equally non-uniform but orthogonal (1, 1, 2, 2). That is BLOCK I of the verifier named in §7; it pins each row's value, so a silent channel fails it. Which is also why a uniform-rate reading is not the safe default: the [Kingston flight](../../experiments/F120_MOMENT_TOWER_KINGSTON.md) measured per-qubit pump slopes spanning 2.19·10⁻³ to 5.79·10⁻³ per μs across its three qubits and two arms, and its own T1 landscape moved on every qubit within the day, by 1.2 to 1.4× comparing in-situ with in-situ, so the pairing has to be read where and when it is measured. The [Absorption Theorem](PROOF_ABSORPTION_THEOREM.md)'s vector form is the same move on the spectral side, where site-dependent rates likewise replace a scalar total by a profile-weighted share.

## §5 The curvature fingerprint, and the complementarity

One order up, the second derivative at the mixed state is

  d²/dt² ⟨A⟩ |_{I/d} = (1/d)·Σ_l Δγ_l·Tr(A·L(Z_l)),

and because the pump itself is generator-independent and L appears exactly once, the curvature is **exactly affine in the generator**, no small-δ expansion needed. For a chip Hamiltonian H_c = H_p + δV against the programmed measurement polynomial A = H_p^j, the dephasing and damping contributions cancel in the difference and

  Δcurvature = (δ/d)·Σ_l Δγ_l·(−i)·Tr(V·[Z_l, A]):

the parasite V is read **linearly against the commutator probes [Z_l, H_p^j]**. The probe set has a flavor: commutators of Z with X/Y-letter strings. So X/Y-flavored parasites with overlap on the probe set are visible at first order (verified: Y₀, Y₀Z₁, Y₁X₂ read at +0.0090, +0.0090 and +0.0126, each matching the probe law to 1.7e-18, 1.7e-18 and 6.9e-18), while **Z-flavored parasites are exactly invisible** ([Z_l, Z-string] = 0; verified at 0.00e+00). That blind spot is not a defect, it is a division of labor: Z-drives are exactly what F113's balance channel reads linearly, and X/Y-drives are exactly what F113 cannot see (its structural origin: only [Z, σ⁻] ∝ σ⁻ feeds the asymmetry; [X, σ⁻] = −Z and [Y, σ⁻] = −i·Z leave the σ⁻ direction and stay balanced). **The two channels never read the same parasite**: F113's balance reads the Z-flavor, the pump curvature reads X/Y-flavor with overlap on the probe set, and both read linearly with closed-form coefficients. Disjoint is all that is claimed, and it is worth saying what is not: they do not between them cover the single-site algebra. Visibility on the curvature side is a condition on the programmed H_p, not a flavor rule, so both channels can miss the same parasite: at H_p = Z₀Z₁ the parasite Y₀ is invisible to each, since [Z_l, H_p^j] = 0 kills the probe and Tr(Z_l Y₀) = 0 kills the balance. In F113's own scope, H_p a pure Z-drive, the curvature side reads nothing at all.

## §6 The hardware protocol

Everything measurable here is standard hardware vocabulary; nothing needs process tomography.

1. **Preparation.** I/d is prepared in distribution: sample computational basis states |x⟩ uniformly (or twirl), one per shot group. (Our existing hardware arms have used only pure initial states; the basis-average is the one new protocol element, and it is preparation-free per shot.)
2. **Evolution.** Free evolution under the device for a short ramp of times t ∈ {0, δt, 2δt, …} inside the early window (the curvature, dominated by the damping of the pumped Z's, sets the window; the verifier's finite-time block recovers the law from exactly this fit).
3. **Measurement.** ⟨H_p^j⟩ as a Pauli polynomial: for a local programmed H and fixed j the string count is polynomial, and the j = 1, 2 rungs at small N are a handful of settings per time point.
4. **Readout.** The fitted initial slope per rung j, against the prediction (1/d)Σ_l Δγ_l t_j(l) with Δγ_l from calibration (γ↑ ≈ 0 on a cold device, so Δγ_l ≈ 1/T1_l). Site resolution by per-site partial damping where the summed certificate is ambiguous.

The prediction is parameter-free once T1 calibration is read: the slope values, the rung at which they first fire, the detailed-balance null, and the curvature shifts under a deliberately injected X/Y-drive are all closed-form. The natural first target is the rung-2 witness pair (slope⟨H⟩ = 0 exactly, slope⟨H²⟩ = Σ Δγ_l t₂(l)/d ≠ 0): a one-qubit-observable null next to a firing rung on the same device, the same discrimination pattern our [Confirmations](../../simulations/framework/confirmations.py) entries already use.

## §7 Scope, and what is ours

The slope law itself is elementary open-system algebra (non-unital channels pump; unital ones do not), and the d = 1 moments it reads are classical traces of the *programmed* Hamiltonian. What this note banks is the assembly, none of whose pieces needed to be invented but whose composition was unbuilt: the pump direction is F82's antisymmetric entry, its weight is F84's vacuum rate, its first rung is F113's asymmetry with the exact bridge constant −4^N, its tower is the girth ladder's deg-1 face, its silence is bounded honestly by F117's class decomposition, and its curvature reads a parasite class disjoint from F113's, linearly. The first energy-moment polynomial that responds to the device's own damping is rung k, and it bounds the hardness moment m\* ≤ 2k + 1, until now a statement about power sums of a recentred generator, from a measurement.

Fences: deg-1 face only (higher-leg readers, the d = 3 response chain, are named follow-up, not claimed); of §6 the flight ran the basis-state averaging, the τ ramp and rungs j = 1, 2, and it resolved sites by permuting the middle qubit across two arms rather than by the per-site partial damping §6 prescribes, so the resolved form of §4's certificate is still derived and not flown; the detailed-balance null is not something a chip's temperature reaches (§2.3: Δγ is the vacuum rate at every T), so testing it needs an engineered σ⁺ at a tuned rate, e.g. §4's partial reset conjugated by an X, which this flight did not carry; the curvature arm (an injected X/Y-drive shifting the pump curvature where a Z-drive cannot) is named [Still open](../../experiments/F120_MOMENT_TOWER_KINGSTON.md) by the flight itself; and the rates-from-calibration route of §6 step 4 is the one layer the chip declined, replaced in-situ by the prep-conditioned Γ; the curvature fingerprint sees X/Y-flavored parasites only (by design, §5); all statements are for the standard noise model (Z-dephasing + amplitude damping; other exotic non-unital channels would add their own pump directions to L(I) and are out of scope).
