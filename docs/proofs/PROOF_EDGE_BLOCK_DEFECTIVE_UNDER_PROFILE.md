## (b) Lemma A: on a path, M is non-derogatory

**Lemma A.** Let the graph be a path, vertices in their natural order, with every bond coupling `J_b ≠ 0`. Then `rank(M − λI) ≥ N − 1` for every λ ∈ ℂ, in either Δ book. Equivalently, the geometric multiplicity of every λ is at most one, hence exactly one at every eigenvalue.

This is the classical non-derogatory property of an unreduced tridiagonal matrix; it is stated and proved here because the repo does not hold it, and because the proof is two lines. *Proof.* On a path, M is tridiagonal, and its off-diagonal entries are `M[b, b+1] = M[b+1, b] = 2i·J_b` in both books, the Δ term being diagonal. Delete row 0 and column N−1 from `M − λI`. The remaining (N−1) × (N−1) matrix is triangular, with those off-diagonal entries on its diagonal, so its determinant is `∏_b (2i·J_b)`: free of λ, and nonzero whenever every coupling is nonzero. A matrix with a nonvanishing (N−1) × (N−1) minor has rank at least N−1. Rank is invariant under relabelling, so the natural order is a convenience and not a hypothesis. ∎

Gate **G2** carries this symbolically for both Δ and N = 2 to 6, comparing exactly, with no tolerance anywhere in it.

## (c) The corollary, and why no EP instrument appears here

**Corollary.** On a path edge block with all `J_b ≠ 0`, **any** repeated eigenvalue is defective, and an eigenvalue of algebraic multiplicity m heads a single Jordan block of size m.

Geometric multiplicity one with algebraic multiplicity m ≥ 2 leaves room for nothing else. There is no diabolic alternative to exclude, which is why neither this proof nor its gate invokes `EpCharacter`, a Petermann factor, a phase rigidity, or any other defective-versus-diabolic discriminator: Lemma A closes by an exact rank argument what those instruments would have to decide numerically. The repo's history is why that matters, the real-axis Petermann magnitudes having been retracted as grid artifacts on 2026-06-21. Gate **G3** measures the rank statement where it can bite, at the EPs themselves, and prints the minimum eigenvalue gap over random draws to show why a random λ cannot test it.

## (d) N = 2 in closed form

With `M = [[−2γ₀, 2iJ], [2iJ, −2γ₁]]` the discriminant of the characteristic polynomial is

  **disc = 4·(γ₁ − γ₀)² − 16·J²,**

so the eigenvalues coalesce exactly on `|γ₁ − γ₀| = 2J`. On the branch `γ₁ = γ₀ + 2J` the doubled eigenvalue is `tr M / 2`, the nilpotent `K = M − (tr M/2)·I` equals `[[2J, 2iJ], [2iJ, −2J]]`, which is nonzero, and `K² = 0`. A genuine 2 × 2 Jordan block at real positive rates and a real coupling. The two Δ books differ at N = 2 by the scalar `−2iJ·I`, so the condition is the same in both. ## (e) Existence, and the codimension that makes it cheap

**On the XY chain the discriminant is real.** A path is bipartite, so with `S = diag((−1)^k)` one has `S·conj(M)·S⁻¹ = M` **exactly** at Δ = 0, since `S·A_J·S⁻¹ = −A_J` while S leaves the rate diagonal alone. Two hypotheses are doing work and both are named here rather than left to §(f): the rate diagonal is REAL, and the bond profile is real as well as symmetric, so that `conj(A_J) = A_J`. What breaks the identity is a complex SYMMETRIC off-diagonal, which makes `A_J` non-Hermitian; a physically complex hop, `J_b` against `conj(J_b)`, leaves `A_J` Hermitian, and then the characteristic polynomial and the discriminant stay real and this section's conclusions stand. The REASON needs care, and an earlier version of this sentence gave one that proves the counterexample too. It is not that the alternating-sign similarity gives a spectrum symmetric about zero: `S·A_J·S = −A_J` depends only on the bipartite sign pattern and holds for the complex SYMMETRIC placement as well, where the polynomial is not real. What happens at a Hermitian hop is that the identity CHANGES ITS RIGHT-HAND SIDE, `S·conj(M)·S⁻¹ = Mᵀ`, and `M` and `Mᵀ` are cospectral, so the spectrum is closed under conjugation and the polynomial is real. Equivalently and more simply: a path is a tree, so the hop phases carry no flux and a diagonal unitary removes them, which is why nothing moves. That identity is **not this document's**: it is `ChiralKClaim`'s `K·H·K = −H`, Tier 1, same K, same basis, any bond profile, combined with a rate diagonal that is real; `INHERITED_RULES_AND_THE_OWN.md:84-85` records the same pairing and shelves it in the Δ = 1 book, where the degree diagonal does break it. What follows from here is the new part. M is therefore similar to its own conjugate, its characteristic polynomial has real coefficients, and the discriminant is real. Gate **G5** asserts the similarity against literal zero, and asserts that the same similarity **fails** at Δ = 1, where the degree diagonal breaks it (`S·D_J·S⁻¹ = D_J`, not `−D_J`).

**So the real-λ EP set has codimension one.** `disc = 0` is one real equation in the rate space `ℝ^N` rather than two, so its solution set is contained in a hypersurface, of dimension AT MOST N − 1 rather than the N − 2 that two equations would leave. "At most" is the honest form: the real zero set of a real polynomial can be lower-dimensional or empty, and what is established pointwise is local hypersurface structure at each SIMPLE sign-change root, which is what the witnesses exhibit. The scope is worth stating flatly, and an earlier version of this sentence overstated it (corrected 2026-08-20). It said that a real discriminant makes its VANISHING a coalescence on the real axis. That is false: `(λ²+1)²` has real coefficients and zero discriminant with both double roots at ±i, and the next paragraph of this section concedes as much when it keeps the conjugate-pair EPs at codimension two. What is true is the statement the certificate actually uses. For a real polynomial with SIMPLE roots and `s` conjugate pairs, `sign(disc) = (−1)^s`; the simplicity is the hypothesis, since a repeated root sends the discriminant to zero, whose sign is neither `+1` nor `−1`, and `(λ²+1)²` is that case. It is applied only at the bracket ENDS, where the discriminant is an exact nonzero rational, so it holds where it is used. A **sign change** then forces the parity of `s` to flip, so an ODD NUMBER of pairs arrives on or leaves the real axis somewhere inside, and real roots must have collided there; the merge is put on the axis by the sign CHANGE and never by the vanishing. Neither the degree's parity nor the leading coefficient enters: each pair contributes `(2bi)² < 0`, cross terms between distinct pairs come in conjugate quadruples with positive product, and `a_n` appears as `a_n^(2n−2)`, an even power. Every EP this route produces therefore has Im λ = 0, and the route sees only those.

**What is gated about that reality, and what is not.** The gated quantity is the coalescing pair's **mean**, which is a trace over an invariant 2-space whose nearest neighbour sits 0.1653 to 0.6441 away, so it is stable and its computed imaginary part is a cancellation residual bounded by eps·‖M‖₂·n. **G6** asserts that bound and the isolation it rests on; **G6c** asserts it again at every witness across four decades of joint (γ, J) scaling, twenty-one readings whose worst is 0.46, with the alternatives printed beside them as a reading rather than asserted. Three assertions about those alternatives stood here across two earlier versions and all three were withdrawn: one was an arithmetic identity that passes on any input, and two turned on where the largest of twenty-one cancellation residuals happened to land, which a relabelling that changes no spectrum already moves. So the **shape** of the size factor is gated nowhere in this file, G1 included, and these witnesses span only N = 4, 5, 6, which could not separate it in any case. Two things this bound is **not**. It is not a bound on the *split*, which at a defective point is the unstable quantity and runs like √(eps·‖M‖₂); as a ratio of the two models that is 8.5·10⁶ to 2.3·10⁷ across the seven, and against the measured splits a wider 3.1·10⁶ to 3.5·10⁷, neither of them gated. And no raw magnitude is quoted, because a cancellation residual moves with the units: an earlier version of this sentence said 2.0e-16, which at a scale of 10⁴ becomes 1.4e-12 for the same physical EP. 

**Back to the codimension.** EPs between eigenvalues that are not each other's conjugates still occur as conjugate PAIRS of EPs and remain codimension two; the reality of the discriminant does not reduce those. What is codimension one is the real-λ stratum, and the witnesses below are all of that type.

**Existence, and what certifies it.** Existence then needs nothing more than a **sign change**: a real continuous function taking opposite signs at the ends of an interval has a zero between them. No topological degree, no radius, no point count, and no way for a near-coalescence to masquerade as a zero. ## (f) What falls, and what survives

**The Edge lemma's conclusion is false under a rate profile.** §(e) contradicts it at N = 4, 5 and 6, on the operator it is about. Its premise fails first, `A = −2γ·I` being scalar only at uniform γ, but a failed premise merely removes a guarantee; §(e) removes the conclusion.

**The window-edge lemma survives, and it does the confining.** What it needs is not that the Hermitian part be Hermitian, which is true of every matrix and therefore not a hypothesis at all: it is that the Hermitian part EQUALS `−2·diag(γ)`, which holds because `2i·A_J` is exactly anti-Hermitian for a real symmetric bond profile AT REAL J. That survives any RATE profile, which is the point here, and it is what fails if the hopping is made non-Hermitian; a complex hop with conjugate placement is gauge and changes nothing. Under that hypothesis the lemma forbids a defective eigenvalue only **at an edge** of the rate window `[−2γ_max, −2γ_min]`. Bendixson together with that lemma give, as a theorem and with no measurement:

> A defective eigenvalue on a path edge block **must** sit strictly inside `[−2γ_max, −2γ_min]`.

"Must", not "anywhere": the witnesses are seven points, and nothing here shows the interior is exhausted. Gate **G7** corroborates the containment; it does not establish it. At uniform γ the interval collapses to a point, there is no interior, and the old conclusion returns as the zero-width case. `PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md` says "the Edge lemma is the zero-width case" of a window too, and the two statements must not be merged: that window is in **n_diff** units, and on an edge block n_diff ≡ 1 at every γ profile, so its zero width is the constancy of n_diff and not the uniformity of γ. Read literally, that sentence claimed a normal pencil on the edge block under any profile, which was one of the sites §(e) refutes rather than prior agreement with §(f). The fencing pass of 2026-08-20 separated the two windows at the source, so the sentence now carries the distinction itself and this paragraph records what it used to say rather than a live defect. The bridge to use is the one `OpenArcsRegistry.cs` already wrote: "the gamma-profile bracket is that window with gamma in place of n_diff". The window statement is not new either: `hypotheses/INHERITED_RULES_AND_THE_OWN.md:88-89` states it for this block, Tier 3 labelled. ## (g) Why uniform γ never showed this

Lemma A applies to the Hermitian Jacobi matrix `A_J` as well, so a path's hopping spectrum is **simple whenever `A_J` is Hermitian and every `J_b ≠ 0`**. Both hypotheses are load-bearing and neither may be dropped: at a vanishing bond the chain is cut and the pieces can repeat each other, and a non-Hermitian `A_J` has nothing forcing its spectrum to be simple or even semisimple. Hence at uniform γ the block's spectrum is simple: there is no repeated eigenvalue at all, and the Edge lemma's conclusion holds there for a reason that has nothing to do with normality. Gate **G9** measures 2700 random J profiles at uniform γ and never finds a degeneracy. It separately confirms, at uniform J, that M is normal at uniform γ, with a commutator that is exactly zero; that half holds for any real symmetric A_J and is not a reading of the 2700.

An earlier draft argued this from `2J(1 − cos(mπ/N))`, which is F2's closed form up to a factor of two (F2 is `4J(1 − cos(πk/N))`) and misses the case twice over: it holds only at **uniform J**, and it is the **Laplacian** spectrum, Δ = 1, while §(g) is about the Δ = 0 adjacency block, whose spectrum is `4J·cos(mπ/(N+1))`. The Jacobi argument is both correct and free, being Lemma A again.

This gives the statement its final shape:

> On a path edge block AT UNIFORM γ, no bond profile with `A_J` Hermitian and every `J_b ≠ 0` can create a degeneracy, so the per-site RATE profile is what breaks the uniform case; and Lemma A then leaves any degeneracy, however reached, no form but a Jordan block.

The first clause needs BOTH words, and two successive versions of this box dropped one each. Drop nonvanishing: at uniform γ = 0.05 with bonds `(0.3, 0, 0.3)` the N = 4 block has the doubled eigenvalues `−0.1 ± 0.6i` with algebraic 2 and geometric 2, so a bond profile DOES produce a degeneracy there, and it is SEMISIMPLE. Drop Hermiticity: at uniform γ = 0.05 with N = 3 and bonds `(1, i)` placed SYMMETRICALLY, as §(b)'s builder places them, both nonzero and the path unbroken, the block has the single eigenvalue `−0.1` with algebraic multiplicity 3 and geometric multiplicity 1, a Jordan block of size three at uniform γ. The word is Hermiticity and not reality, and the difference is not pedantry: a physically complex HOP, placed as `J_b` against `conj(J_b)`, keeps `A_J` Hermitian, and then the characteristic polynomial and the discriminant stay real and nothing in §(e) moves, the phases being gauge. What breaks the spectrum is a complex SYMMETRIC off-diagonal, which is not a Hermitian Hamiltonian at all. Neither counterexample touches Lemma A, which §(b) states for a path with every `J_b ≠ 0` and which needs no Hermiticity: the first cuts the path into two dimers, so it is outside the hypothesis, and the second is a genuine Lemma A instance, defective exactly as Lemma A says a repeated eigenvalue must be. What they refute is the sentence about the HOPPING spectrum. Gate G9 sees neither, drawing its couplings real and from `[0.05, 2.0]`.

A bond profile cannot do it AT UNIFORM γ with `A_J` Hermitian and every `J_b ≠ 0`, and every one of those words belongs in this sentence rather than in the section's title, which is where the first of them used to live. Once the rates are non-uniform the coupling becomes a knob like any other: at N = 4 with γ = (0.05, 0.15, 0.07, 0.09) held fixed and the other two bonds at 0.1, turning bond 0 gives an exact real coalescence at J* = ±0.0495110382, Re λ = −0.16980, geometric multiplicity 1 against algebraic 2, hence a Jordan block by Lemma A; the discriminant has degree 10 in that bond and its roots come in ± pairs because the characteristic polynomial sees a coupling only through J². Gate **G9b** certifies the parts of that a gate can, and takes no eigenvalue anywhere: the isolated positive real root of the discriminant over ℚ locates `J*` inside a rational bracket, and the exact sign change at that bracket's ends, by `sign(disc) = (−1)^s` for simple roots, puts the coalescence on the real axis; Lemma A's λ-free minor gives geometric multiplicity exactly 1. What it does NOT certify, and the earlier wording claimed it did: the value `Re λ = −0.16980`, which no gate here computes, and the ALGEBRAIC multiplicity, for which a discriminant gives only `≥ 2`. Both are right, checked independently at forty digits with the other pair distinct, but they are read from the algebra rather than from the gate. The contrast that carries the meaning, that the same bond at uniform rates reaches nothing, is deliberately NOT gated: at uniform γ the block's discriminant is `(−4)^(N(N−1)/2)` times `disc(A_J)`, and it is `disc(A_J)` that is strictly positive for any real nonvanishing bond profile, being the discriminant of a real symmetric unreduced Jacobi matrix, whose spectrum is simple and real. The block's own discriminant is therefore SIGN-CONSTANT rather than positive, and its sign runs with period four in N: negative at N ≡ 2, 3 and positive at N ≡ 0, 1 (mod 4), the `−16J²` of §(d) being the N = 2 instance. Either way there is no sign change to find and a gate on it could not fail. That is Lemma A a third time. The arc `site_resolved_vacuum_block` records the same doubling under FIRST-A item (6), and records that a first reading of that run wrongly said bonds carry no cusps at all. What is proven here is the uniform-γ statement and nothing wider: a rate profile is what opens the door, not what monopolises it.

## (h) Scope, and three neighbours this is not
