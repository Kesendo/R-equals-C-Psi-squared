**What this is.** One page listing the discrete symmetries of the open XY chain under per-site Z-dephasing, for a reader who needs to know which maps conjugate the Liouvillian to itself or to its reflection, what each one does to the spectrum or to the blocks, and where its proof and its typed Claim live. Each row is one map, one sentence on what it does, its tier, and its anchors. The polarity-cube refinement family (F102 to F113) has its own page, [the polarity cube family](POLARITY_CUBE_FAMILY.md), and the N = 10 computation that uses these symmetries has its own, [the N = 10 block-spectrum push](../experiments/N10_BLOCK_SPECTRUM_PUSH.md).

**Scope.** The generator is

    L(ρ) = −i[H, ρ] + Σ_l γ_l (Z_l ρ Z_l − ρ),    H = Σ_b (J_b/2)(X_b X_{b+1} + Y_b Y_{b+1}) + Σ_l h_l Z_l,

sites l = 0..N−1, bonds b = 0..N−2, open ends, rates γ_l > 0, and h = 0 unless a row says otherwise. Operator side: linear or antilinear maps of Liouville space that commute with L or carry it to −L − c. Parameter side: maps of the profiles (γ, J, h) that leave a stated object invariant. Other dephasing letters, other jumps and other topologies are listed at the end as adjacent, not as rows.

**Key.**

- A sector is a joint-popcount block (p_c, p_r) = (bra popcount, ket popcount) of the matrix units |a⟩⟨b|, p_c = popcount(b); the order is the one `JointPopcountSectors` uses.
- Π is the palindromizer, Π(ρ) = ρᵀ·X^⊗N, per site I → X, X → I, Y → iZ, Z → iY ([glossary](GLOSSARY.md)). Π² = Ad_{X⊗N} is written 𝓕 or Π²_Z; Ad_{Z⊗N} is written Π²_X. bit_b(σ) = (n_Y + n_Z) mod 2 and bit_a(σ) = (n_X + n_Y) mod 2 are the exponents of their eigenvalues (−1)^bit on a Pauli string σ ([polarity cube](GLOSSARY.md)). Π_X and Π_Y are the palindromizers of X and Y dephasing, Π_Y = Π_Z⁻¹; ā is the bitwise complement of a.
- R is the right multiplication ρ ↦ ρ·X^⊗N, which flips the index b of \|a⟩⟨b\|; F118 calls it the ket reflection, naming the column index of a coherence the ket, the opposite of this key's naming ([mirror group](GLOSSARY.md)); D is the transpose, throughout this page, and the dissipator is written out in words; Π_Z = R·D.
- F71 names the chain's site reflection l ↔ N−1−l, after the c₁ mirror [F71](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md); R₉₀ names the parameter reshuffle x ↦ 2·x_avg − x_mirror, the mirror of site l being N−1−l and of bond b being N−2−b.
- σ = Σ_l γ_l; the palindrome is the closure of the spectrum multiset under λ ↦ −λ − 2σ.
- A Pauli string is written P where σ would collide with Σγ; q is the coupling of a block pencil L(q) = A + q·C; the ladder rows give their sector steps in the key's (p_c, p_r).
- Tiers are the typed layer's: Tier 1 derived, Tier 1 candidate, Tier 2 empirical.

## The substrate: U(1) × U(1)

Per-side popcount conservation, continuous and not a row: H conserves the excitation number and Z_l ρ Z_l keeps both popcounts, so L is block-diagonal in the (N+1)² sectors of size C(N, p_c)·C(N, p_r). Every row below refines these blocks, pairs them, or reflects their spectra. Tier 1 derived; `compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/BlockSpectrum/JointPopcountSectors.cs`.
