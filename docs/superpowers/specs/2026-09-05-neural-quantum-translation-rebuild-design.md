# Neural Quantum-Translation Rebuild Design

**Date:** 2026-09-05

## Goal

Rebuild the Neural documentation as a from-below translation of the quantum
objects already owned by this repository. Each proposed neural counterpart must
be derived from the neural generator, survive a falsifiable gate, and retain the
scope of that gate. Familiar neuroscience language and training-data priors are
not evidence; an unfamiliar result remains live when the repository computation
supports it.

The result is not a polished analogy between two fields. It is a traceable road
whose branches end in one of three honest places: an exact transfer, a scoped
computational sighting, or a failed/open translation.

## Translation discipline

Every bridge is recorded as a four-part object:

1. **Quantum owner:** the exact operator, theorem, formula, or live witness in
   the repository.
2. **Neural candidate:** a named matrix, mode, involution, parameter, or
   observable in the neural dynamics, defined without metaphor.
3. **Transfer derivation:** the algebra that would carry the quantum structure
   to that candidate, including every extra hypothesis.
4. **Gate:** an independently computable property that fails when the proposed
   transfer is false.

Only a passing derivation and gate permit an exact-transfer sentence. A numerical
gate permits only the regime, ensemble, seed policy, tolerance, and observable it
actually tested. A failed gate is a result and may open a new object; it must not
be softened into an analogy or hidden because established vocabulary expected a
different answer.

## Current defects the rebuild must remove

- The front door says the symmetry appears in biological brains although the
  only tested connectome does not satisfy the condition.
- Synthetic Wilson-Cowan-like drive sweeps are presented as biological,
  metabolic, thermal, or life-wide findings.
- Exact palindromic spectra are said to be real, silent, or necessarily stable.
- The mediator sweep is explained as breaking a symmetry the odd-seat coupled
  construction never possessed.
- Residual growth is treated as the cause of oscillation although exact
  palindromic networks can oscillate and broken examples can remain silent.
- A greedy pairing of real parts on random Dale networks is presented as a
  theorem-level E/I character swap.
- Trace/centre conservation is inflated into topology blindness or full spectral
  pairing.
- F36 is scoped to Dale's Law although support symmetry and scaled magnitudes are
  independent requirements.
- A neural fold is identified with Hopf or gain one without a gate owning that
  statement.
- Producer help text and summaries continue to print withdrawn causal readings.

## Record boundaries

Experiments, committed result dumps, Confirmations, `CAUGHT_ERRORS.md`,
`reflections/`, and `recovered/` retain the event they record. Their present-day
headers and links may clarify authority, but historical observations are not
rewritten into a cleaner past. Living guides, proofs, formula entries, claims,
and producer descriptions carry only current truth; superseded prose disappears
because git already owns its history.

## Verification

The implementation must provide fresh evidence for:

- theorem equivalence at the matrix-entry level, including `Q^2 = I` and the
  zero-gain edge case;
- multiset pairing with multiplicity and complex eigenvalues;
- adversarial rejection by the scalar-`s` residual where the fitted residual
  reads zero;
- exact reproduction of the 200-seed counts;
- basis-invariant partner-subspace transport under `Q`, including a degenerate
  case;
- drive fixed-point convergence and threshold-consistent frequency counts;
- local Markdown links and documented PowerShell/POSIX run commands;
- targeted C# tests, Neural Python gates, and a residual phrase sweep.

No expensive census is run merely to reconfirm a deductive corollary. Where an
exact owner already proves the result, the derivation and a separating small
gate are the evidence.

## Non-goals

- Do not claim a result for brains, life, metabolism, temperature, cognition, or
  C. elegans unless a declared biological object and dataset pass the gate.
- Do not import graph-Laplacian results from quantum single-excitation blocks
  into arbitrary neural Jacobians.
- Do not force Neural dynamics to reproduce the quantum V-effect, the quarter
  boundary, a Hopf bifurcation, or a dimensionless `Q` by analogy.
- Do not discard a reproducible unfamiliar result because it lacks an existing
  textbook label or resembles no training-data pattern.
- Do not invent a replacement mechanism for the coupling or drive sweeps.
- Do not alter unrelated user changes in the dirty working tree.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every cross-domain statement exposes its quantum owner, neural candidate,
  extra hypotheses, and gate, directly or through a precise owning link.
- The five Neural documents agree on the theorem, connectome null, surviving
  measurements, and open mechanisms.
- No living sibling repeats the retired silence, symmetry-breaking, biological
  thermal-window, 96-percent, centre-as-proof, or generic Hopf claims.
- F36/F37 wording and any typed representation agree with the final theorem.
- The new gates fail on minimal counterexamples to the old instruments and pass
  on exact constructed networks.
- All affected cheap producers and targeted tests pass from fresh runs; links
  resolve; the final tell-tale phrase scan is empty except for explicit event or
  caught-error records.
