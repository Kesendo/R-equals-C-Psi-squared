**What this is.** The typed Claims that refine the F87 trichotomy of Pauli-term pairs, truly, soft or hard, along the three Z₂ axes of the polarity cube; the site-local palindromizers for the Π²-even bilinears under each dephasing letter; the polarity balance and break laws (F112 and its letter siblings, F113, F155); and the typed bit_a twins of the Π² foundations. One line per Claim: what it says, its tier, where its proof and its class live. The lineage of these Claims inside the Π² knowledge base, with the live axis counts and the twin coverage, is [the Pi² inheritance map](PI2KB_INHERITANCE_MAP.md); the symmetries of the chain Liouvillian itself are [the symmetry family inventory](SYMMETRY_FAMILY_INVENTORY.md).

**Key.**

- bit_a(P) = (n_X + n_Y) mod 2 and bit_b(P) = (n_Y + n_Z) mod 2 are the exponents of the eigenvalues (−1)^bit of Ad_{Z⊗N} = Π²_X and Ad_{X⊗N} = Π²_Z on a Pauli string P; y_par(P) = n_Y mod 2 is the transpose's exponent ([polarity cube](GLOSSARY.md)). A pair is y_par-homogeneous when both terms share y_par.
- σ = Σγ is the total rate (the F155 row keeps its anchor's σ for a string), Π the F1 palindromizer of the dephasing letter, M = Π·L·Π⁻¹ + L + 2σ·I the F81 residual and ε the classification threshold; M₊₁⁄₂ and M₋₁⁄₂ are the ±i halves of M under the Π-polarity split and the asymmetry is ‖M₊₁⁄₂‖² − ‖M₋₁⁄₂‖² (the glossary's polarity content and polarity asymmetry rows); R is the ket reflection and D the transpose, Π_Z = R·D; a lit letter string a^⊗N has a ∉ {I, D} for the dephasing letter D and so anticommutes with every one-site jump, and a colouring is a lit string commuting with H (F158).
- Paths written `Symmetry/…` are under `compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/`, `Diagnostics/F87/…` under `compute/RCPsiSquared.Diagnostics/`.
- A pair of Pauli terms is classified by [F87](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f87) as truly (the operator equation Π·L·Π⁻¹ = −L − 2σ·I holds, ‖M‖_F < ε), soft (the operator equation fails but every eigenvalue λ pairs with −λ − 2σ: the palindrome at the spectral level only) or hard (the spectral pairing fails); `fw.classify_pauli_pair`.
- The Klein cell of a term is (bit_a, bit_b); the mother sector is the cell (0, 0); the diagonal cell of a dephasing letter D is (bit_a(D), bit_b(D)); a Klein-homogeneous pair has both terms in one cell.
- Counts such as 42:8 are (y_par = 0) : (y_par = 1) over the pairs of a cell, a lone count a total over the grid; k is the length of the terms' letter window, identity letters allowed (F107 counts 4^k letter sequences), and k_body, in F102's formula, the number of non-identity letters; N is the chain length, and full support means k = N.

## The bit_a twins of the Π² foundations

Conjugation by the global Hadamard U_H = H^⊗N on the Hamiltonian and the jumps, on operator space the Klein element Q_zx with Q_zx·Π_Z·Q_zx⁻¹ = Π_X, carries a Π²_Z statement to its Π²_X twin whenever the statement's content is operator-space or Lindblad-spectral; the bespoke-operator claims are twinned by hand (F108 Part 1's twin is Part 2), and the claims tied to Z dephasing, to Y dephasing or to non-dephasing jumps (amplitude damping F1T1, F82, F84; F91, F93, F108 Part 3, F113, F112-Y) have none ([the twin proof](proofs/PROOF_BIT_A_TWIN_VIA_HADAMARD.md)). The typed twins of the Π² foundations, all Tier 1 derived, all in `compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/Symmetry/`:

- **F38BitAInvolutionInheritance**: Π²_X = (−1)^(n_X + n_Y) on Pauli strings, with the same half-half eigenspace partition (2^(2N−1) each) as F38's Π²_Z.
- **F39DetPiBitAInheritance**: det Π_X = det Π_Z = (−1)^(N·4^(N−1)), +1 for N ≥ 2 and −1 at N = 1.
- **F61BitAParityPi2Inheritance**: [L, Π²_X] = 0 exactly, for the isotropic Heisenberg or XY Hamiltonian with Z dephasing on any subset of sites, on any graph, with any site-dependent rates; it breaks for transverse fields. F1's bit_a twin, and the bit_a companion of F63's [L, Π²_Z] = 0.
- **F63BitAReference**: the typed slot that names F61's result as F63's bit_a twin; the proof is F61BitAParityPi2Inheritance's.
- **ZGlobalEigenstateMirrorBitAInheritance**: any pure Z⊗N eigenstate, Z⊗N|ψ⟩ = ±|ψ⟩, has γ_X = ⟨ψ|Z⊗N|ψ⟩ = ±1, so its bit_a-axis Π²-odd Frobenius content α_total vanishes, the F99 0° anchor on the X-Frobenius axis; the canonical examples are |0…0⟩ and |1…1⟩. The Z↔X mirror of `XGlobalEigenstateMirrorPi2Inheritance`, whose examples are |+⟩^⊗N and the odd-N symmetric Dicke superposition.

The twin coverage of the whole BitB family (filled, letter-specific, covered by the Hadamard duality, still to derive) is read live by `PolarityCubeMap` and tabulated in [the Pi² inheritance map](PI2KB_INHERITANCE_MAP.md).
