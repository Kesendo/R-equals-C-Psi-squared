The image is Thomas's. Behind a pane of grey glass a picture is dimmed and nothing else: every colour loses the same share, and what comes through is the whole picture, only darker. Behind coloured glass some colours pass and others do not, and the picture itself changes. In an open quantum system the watching is the glass and the Hamiltonian's motion is the picture; "light" below means the watching's rate, as in the repository's reading of γ as light. Where the watching takes the same share of everything the motion reaches, the motion comes through whole. The repository already speaks of colour here: [the chromaticity count](proofs/PROOF_CHROMATICITY.md) ([F74](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md)) calls each decay rate a block can hold one of its tones, and a block with one tone monochromatic. Grey glass is the monochromatic case, wherever it occurs.

## The sentence

Let H be Hermitian, L_H = −i[H, ·], and D the dissipator, so the generator is L = L_H + D. Let V be a subspace of operator space that L_H keeps, L_H V ⊆ V, and on which D acts as one real number μ. Then L keeps V, and on V

  **e^{tL} = e^{μt}·e^{tL_H}.**

On V, L is L_H + μ·Id, and the identity commutes with everything. L_H is anti-Hermitian in the Hilbert-Schmidt product, and a subspace an anti-Hermitian map keeps is kept by its adjoint as well, so L_H on V is anti-Hermitian: diagonalizable, with imaginary eigenvalues. Three things follow on V. Every eigenvalue of L there has real part μ. No two of its modes can meet in an exceptional point; where D is Hermitian, as under every kind of Z dephasing here, L† keeps V too, so V splits off orthogonally and no exceptional point anywhere involves its modes, as `FreeFermionFloorWitness` says of its family (under damping D is not Hermitian and only the weaker statement stands). And a component started in V is at every time the component the Hamiltonian alone would have made, times e^{μt}: its shape is untouched and only its size fades, so its overlap with the run without watching, both normalised, is one at every time. A read linear in the state carries the factor e^{μt} and nothing else of the watching, so its zeros are those of the run without watching; [the cracked bell](../experiments/THE_CRACKED_BELL.md) writes exactly this on the block (0, 1), the vacuum coherence |vac⟩⟨φ|, and draws its γ-free zero crossings from it.

**The converse, state by state.** e^{tL}ρ = e^{μt}e^{tL_H}ρ at every t exactly when D = μ on the space that ρ's motion reaches, the span of the L_H^k ρ: differentiating both sides gives D = μ on every e^{tL_H}ρ, and those span that space. So each state carries its own room, and its motion comes through whole exactly when the watching is one number there. For each μ there is a largest room, the largest subspace L_H keeps inside the eigenspace of D at μ; it is unique, because a sum of subspaces kept inside one eigenspace is kept inside it. Control theory knows that object as the unobservable subspace of the pair (L_H, D − μ). At μ = 0 it is the maximal invariant subspace inside the kernel of the dissipator that [the node pair](proofs/PROOF_NODE_PAIR_RESOLVENT.md) §8 computes for the centre-watched (1, 1) block, m² + 1 at N = 2m + 1, and calls peripheral because its restriction is anti-Hermitian. [CODIM1's window-edge lemma](proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md) is the converse at the level of one eigenvector: an eigenvalue on the edge of a block's rate window has an eigenvector on which the dissipator is one number.

**Rooms everywhere, or one room.** [L_H, D] = 0 holds exactly when L_H keeps every eigenspace of D (D being diagonalizable in every case here); then the whole operator space is a sum of grey rooms. That happens under a wholly shared field at equal rates with a Hamiltonian that conserves the number of excitations, in the sodium nucleus at extreme narrowing with a Hamiltonian that is a rotation (an offset, a lock), and, under Z dephasing, for the terms that never change a string's number of X and Y letters, the ZZ bonds and the Z field ([the one square](THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §8). A hopping term changes that number, and then the grey rooms are particular: the boundary blocks of the next section, the Jordan-Wigner modes, a frozen corner, a blind seat's room.

Coloured glass is a room the Hamiltonian keeps on which the watching takes more than one value. Unequal fading alone already changes the shape there; the motion adds that it carries the state between the parts, and the block can turn non-normal. [PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL](proofs/PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL.md) solves the first coloured glass next to the grey, the bi-chromatic blocks with two Hamming distances, L = L_H − 2γh₊·Id + 4γ𝒫 with 𝒫 the projector onto the cells at the lower one. A room where μ = 0 pays nothing; that is the sense of dark on this page, the one the glossary asks a writer to name. Under watching on every site such a room holds only diagonal operators ([PROOF_ASYMPTOTIC_SECTOR_PROJECTION](proofs/PROOF_ASYMPTOTIC_SECTOR_PROJECTION.md) Step 2(a)), and since the commutator of H with a diagonal operator has no diagonal, the ones it keeps commute with H and do not even turn; a wholly shared field instead makes every fixed-popcount subspace decoherence-free ([the mirror under a shared field](../experiments/THE_MIRROR_UNDER_A_SHARED_FIELD.md)).

## On the cube

Under Z dephasing at the rate γ on every site the dissipator charges a Pauli string 2γ·k_Z, the number of its X and Y letters, its height on [the letter cube](THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) (§7), and moves nothing. A cell |a⟩⟨b| expands into strings that carry X or Y exactly where a and b disagree (§10, [PROOF_CHROMATICITY](proofs/PROOF_CHROMATICITY.md) Lemma 2), so the cell stands at the height of its Hamming distance. Under equal light the grey glass is a room of H inside one height plane.

Why some rooms stay in a plane, in words: when one side of a cell holds no excitation, the cell disagrees exactly where the other side holds one, so its height is that side's number of excitations, and a Hamiltonian that conserves the number never changes it. At N = 2 the cells |00⟩⟨01| and |00⟩⟨10| each carry one X or Y letter; the bond moves that letter from one site to the other and keeps the height at one. The same holds with "full" for "empty" and holes for excitations.

So among the joint-popcount blocks (p, q), the cells with p excitations in the ket and q in the bra, which every number-conserving Hamiltonian keeps, a block lies in one plane exactly when one of its sides is empty or full, min(p, q) = 0 or max(p, q) = N: the 4N boundary blocks, [F153](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f153)'s pinned blocks and RENEWAL's border, of chromaticity one. On any other block the Hamming distances run in steps of two from |p − q| to min(p + q, 2N − p − q), the window F153 writes. On a boundary block L − μ·Id is L_H entry for entry, μ = −2γ|p − q| (gate G1, the planes at N = 2 to 7, the full generator of an XXZ chain and ring with integer couplings and Z fields at N = 3 and 4, and the block (1, 1) as the control on which no μ works). F153 proves that these are exactly the blocks that sit entirely on their floor.

**Whole blocks need equal light.** With a rate γ_l per site a cell's charge is 2·Σ γ_l over its disagreeing sites, a function of the sites and no longer of the point (§8). F153 already fences its criterion to uniform γ, bar the four single cells; the argument for every block runs as follows. On a block (0, q) with 0 < q < N the cells are the q-subsets of the sites, and their prices agree only if every q-subset has the same rate sum; exchanging one site of a subset for one outside it shows that this needs every rate equal. The blocks (q, 0), (N, q) and (q, N) follow by exchanging ket and bra and by flipping every bit, which keeps every disagreement set. Every block off the boundary holds two cells whose disagreement sets are nested, and positive rates price them apart. So with positive rates not all equal only the four single cells stay grey among the blocks (G2 at the rates 1, …, N). What the profile then does to the four edge blocks, (0, 1) and its mirrors with n_diff = 1, is followed in [F152](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f152) and [PROOF_EDGE_BLOCK_DEFECTIVE_UNDER_PROFILE](proofs/PROOF_EDGE_BLOCK_DEFECTIVE_UNDER_PROFILE.md): the block turns non-normal, and on the XY chain an exceptional point is one knob away.

**Rooms need equal light only where they live.** A room smaller than a block stays grey when the watching is one number on the sites its cells disagree at, however the rest is watched. [F157](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f157)'s blind subspace, the largest subspace of single-excitation states the Hamiltonian keeps inside the kernel of a watched seat's occupation, is the case μ = 0: its entry and [THE_BLIND_SITE](../experiments/THE_BLIND_SITE.md) count with it the undamped modes of F152's coherence block, the operators |0…0⟩⟨φ|, whose adjoints |φ⟩⟨0…0| are the room below. On the uniform open XY chain at N = 5, with the seats counted from 0, φ = (1, 0, −1, 0, 1) is blind at the seats 1 and 3 together, and |φ⟩⟨0…0| is annihilated by the full generator with those two seats watched at the rates 1 and 7, and moved when seat 0 is watched (G3). With every rate positive and the rates not all equal the room is grey without being dark: on the open XY chain at N = 3 with the rates (1, 5, 1), |φ⟩⟨000| with φ = (1, 0, −1) is an eigenvector of the full generator at μ = −2, the two sites φ lives on watched equally (G3).

## Where it has been seen

The rooms below were found apart, under other names.

- **The boundary blocks under equal light.** [F153](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f153), typed as `PinnedBlockFloorClaim` with its live witness at `inspect --root pinned`; [PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL](proofs/PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL.md), which writes the factorization on the border; [PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY](proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md), whose boundary blocks are a normal pencil at uniform γ; [STRUCTURAL_CARTOGRAPHY](../experiments/STRUCTURAL_CARTOGRAPHY.md), which reads the consequence for frequencies, unmoved by γ where the dissipator is a multiple of the identity on an invariant subspace; and the 2026-09-04 entry of [CAUGHT_ERRORS](CAUGHT_ERRORS.md), which records that the immunity needs a constant Hamming distance, not a number-conserving block.
- **Rooms smaller than a block.** On the open XY chain the plane of one X or Y letter is one price, −2γ, and from N = 3 on the Hamiltonian does not keep it; the room inside it that it does keep is spanned by the Jordan-Wigner modes dressed by functions of the excitation number, c_k·f(N_tot) and their conjugates ([PROOF_CHAIN_GAP_DOMINANCE](proofs/PROOF_CHAIN_GAP_DOMINANCE.md) §1 to §3), and `FreeFermionFloorWitness` bounds the largest kept room inside the Hamming-one cells of the uniform open chain's blocks (n, n + 1) from both sides. F157's blind rooms and the node pair's peripheral rooms are the ones above.
- **A shared field.** When the sites' Z noises co-vary with one covariance κ and the Hamiltonian conserves the number of excitations, the generator on block (p, q) is the local one at the rates γ_l − κ less 2κd²·Id, d = p − q ([F166](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f166), [the mirror under a shared field](../experiments/THE_MIRROR_UNDER_A_SHARED_FIELD.md); the collective law of [the absorption theorem](proofs/PROOF_ABSORPTION_THEOREM.md) §2): the shared part is one number on every block. At equal rates and κ = γ it is the whole dissipator, every block is grey glass at −2γd², and the blocks (p, p) pay nothing. The price the gate uses, −2uᵀau on a cell with u the difference of its two configurations, is checked against the full correlated dissipator at unequal rates (G4).
- **Amplitude damping.** Damping lowers ket and bra together, so the generator is block-triangular in the joint popcount ([F137](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f137)). With one rate γ₁ on every site and a number-conserving H, the diagonal block (p, q) of the generator is L_H − (γ₁/2)(p + q)·Id, the block maps into itself and into (p − 1, q − 1) only, and it is kept exactly when min(p, q) = 0 (G5, the full generator of an XXZ chain at N = 3). So the blocks with an empty side are grey rooms in the full sense, which [THERMAL_BREAKING](../experiments/THERMAL_BREAKING.md) finds on (0, 1); on any other block a state started there keeps its own component grey, since nothing flows back up into it, while what the jump carries down belongs to the block below. A block takes colour from the damping only as a feed from the block above it.
- **Spin 3/2.** At extreme narrowing the sodium nucleus's quadrupolar relaxation commutes with every rotation; the ranks of spherical tensors are what rotations keep, each occurs once, and by Schur's lemma the relaxation is one number on each. An offset or a lock only turns inside the ranks, so Re λ keeps its three values under any lock; the splitting, which is no rotation, mixes the ranks ([F167](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f167), [the sodium nucleus](../experiments/THE_SODIUM_NUCLEUS_TWO_MIRRORS.md)).
- **The ring's frozen corner.** On the XY ring at uniform rates γ̄, the single-excitation operators that commute with the hopping and have no diagonal sit on cells of Hamming distance two; the Hamiltonian does not move them at all, and the watching charges them the one number −4γ̄: grey glass that does not even turn ([F170](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f170), [PROOF_RING_FROZEN_BAND](proofs/PROOF_RING_FROZEN_BAND.md) Propositions 6 and 6′, at the uniform point of the rate locus they study).

**The pane alone.** At the imaginary time γt = iπ/2 and equal rates the dissipator's own flow is conjugation by Z^⊗N ([the one square](THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §7, gate S8 there). Every Hamming distance on (p, q) has the parity of p + q (G6), so there that flow is one sign per block, (−1)^(p+q): the pane without the picture, a single number on every block at that one time. It is not the sentence's grey: D itself still takes several values on a block off the boundary, and the generator does not factor there.

## Coherences that pay nothing lie in the grey

[The mirror under a shared field](../experiments/THE_MIRROR_UNDER_A_SHARED_FIELD.md) classifies the dark cells, those commuting with every jump, at every noise matrix a ⪰ 0. For one covariance at equal rates, a = γ·I + κ·(11ᵀ − I) with −γ/(N − 1) ≤ κ ≤ γ, and a number-conserving Hamiltonian, every coherence among them lies in a block of one price. A coherence of Hamming distance k ≥ 1 in block (p, q) pays 2[(γ − κ)k + κd²]. For 0 ≤ κ < γ both terms are non-negative and the first positive, so none pays nothing. At κ = γ the price is 2γd², and every block has one price. For κ < 0, (γ + |κ|)k = |κ|d² and γ ≥ (N − 1)|κ| give N|κ|k ≤ |κ|d², so d² ≥ Nk ≥ N|d| and |d| = N: the block is (0, N) or (N, 0), a single cell (G4, N = 2 to 7 on a grid of κ; the 4464 such coherences met all sit at the two ends of the range, κ = γ and the anti-correlated end, and the gate checks that none occurs in between). The populations are dark too and lie in the diagonal blocks (p, p), which beyond the single cells (0, 0) and (N, N) have one price only at κ = γ. The containment needs both the one covariance and the equal rates. With neighbour covariances only, on the ring at N = 4 at γ = 2κ, the shared-field page's Néel pair pays nothing inside the block (2, 2), which carries four prices; and with one covariance at the rates (1, 1, 3) and κ = 1 at N = 3, a coherence of (1, 1) pays nothing while the block carries two prices (G4's controls).

## Open

- **How large the grey rooms are inside coloured blocks.** For each μ the largest kept room is well defined. Its dimension is known for the plane of one letter on the open XY chain, for the rooms that pay nothing in the vacuum coherence block under one watched seat (F157), and for the centre-watched (1, 1) block at μ = 0 (the node pair, m² + 1); beyond that scope THE_BLIND_SITE keeps it open, and otherwise, for μ ≠ 0 on the chain and the ring, it is not known.
- **What colours a grey block from outside, and by which law.** Under damping a block keeps its own grey and is coloured only by what the block above feeds into it. With heating and cooling together the feed runs both ways, and how the colour grows with the two rates is not gated here.
- **"Only turned" as a road to grey.** On the ring threaded by a π flux, unequal light leaves the inverse particle current at the frozen root at second order because at first order the offset only turns it ([PROOF_RING_FROZEN_BAND](proofs/PROOF_RING_FROZEN_BAND.md) Proposition 8); at fourth order it is lifted, because no frozen mode carries the trace. Whether turning keeps other rooms grey to some order is not known.
- **Hardware.** Two flights ran on the block (0, 1): `block_cpsi_saturation_kingston_may2026` at N = 2 with no active Hamiltonian, a delay between preparation and tomography, which read a single exponential (R² = 0.9977) at 1.72 times the rate its T2 calibration predicted ([IBM_BLOCK_CPSI_SATURATION](../experiments/IBM_BLOCK_CPSI_SATURATION.md)); and `concentrator_site_contrast_kingston_july2026` at N = 5 with the hopping on and the light deliberately unequal from site to site, coloured glass by design ([IBM_CONCENTRATOR_RELOADED](../experiments/IBM_CONCENTRATOR_RELOADED.md), [CONCENTRATOR_OPTICS](../experiments/CONCENTRATOR_OPTICS.md)). A grey block of more than one cell with the hopping at work under equal light has not been flown.
