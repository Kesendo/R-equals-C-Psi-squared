The image is Thomas's. Behind a pane of grey glass a picture is dimmed and nothing else: every colour loses the same share, and what comes through is the whole picture, only darker. Behind coloured glass some colours pass and others do not, and the picture itself changes. In an open quantum system the watching is the glass and the Hamiltonian's motion is the picture; "light" below means the watching's rate, as in the repository's reading of γ as light. Where the watching takes the same share of everything the motion reaches, the motion comes through whole. The repository already speaks of colour here: [the chromaticity count](proofs/PROOF_CHROMATICITY.md) ([F74](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md)) calls each decay rate a block can hold one of its tones, and a block with one tone monochromatic. Grey glass is the monochromatic case, wherever it occurs.

## The sentence

Let H be Hermitian, L_H = −i[H, ·], and D the dissipator, so the generator is L = L_H + D. Let V be a subspace of operator space that L_H keeps, L_H V ⊆ V, and on which D acts as one real number μ. Then L keeps V, and on V

  **e^{tL} = e^{μt}·e^{tL_H}.**

On V, L is L_H + μ·Id, and the identity commutes with everything. L_H is anti-Hermitian in the Hilbert-Schmidt product, and a subspace an anti-Hermitian map keeps is kept by its adjoint as well, so L_H on V is anti-Hermitian: diagonalizable, with imaginary eigenvalues. Three things follow on V. Every eigenvalue of L there has real part μ. No two of its modes can meet in an exceptional point; where D is Hermitian, as under every kind of Z dephasing here, L† keeps V too, so V splits off orthogonally and no exceptional point anywhere involves its modes, as `FreeFermionFloorWitness` says of its family (under damping D is not Hermitian and only the weaker statement stands). And a component started in V is at every time the component the Hamiltonian alone would have made, times e^{μt}: its shape is untouched and only its size fades, so its overlap with the run without watching, both normalised, is one at every time. A read linear in the state carries the factor e^{μt} and nothing else of the watching, so its zeros are those of the run without watching; [the cracked bell](../experiments/THE_CRACKED_BELL.md) writes exactly this on the block (0, 1), the vacuum coherence |vac⟩⟨φ|, and draws its γ-free zero crossings from it.

**The converse, state by state.** e^{tL}ρ = e^{μt}e^{tL_H}ρ at every t exactly when D = μ on the space that ρ's motion reaches, the span of the L_H^k ρ: differentiating both sides gives D = μ on every e^{tL_H}ρ, and those span that space. So each state carries its own room, and its motion comes through whole exactly when the watching is one number there. For each μ there is a largest room, the largest subspace L_H keeps inside the eigenspace of D at μ; it is unique, because a sum of subspaces kept inside one eigenspace is kept inside it. Control theory knows that object as the unobservable subspace of the pair (L_H, D − μ). At μ = 0 it is the maximal invariant subspace inside the kernel of the dissipator that [the node pair](proofs/PROOF_NODE_PAIR_RESOLVENT.md) §8 computes for the centre-watched (1, 1) block, m² + 1 at N = 2m + 1, and calls peripheral because its restriction is anti-Hermitian. [CODIM1's window-edge lemma](proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md) is the converse at the level of one eigenvector: an eigenvalue on the edge of a block's rate window has an eigenvector on which the dissipator is one number.

**Rooms everywhere, or one room.** [L_H, D] = 0 holds exactly when L_H keeps every eigenspace of D (D being diagonalizable in every case here); then the whole operator space is a sum of grey rooms. That happens under a wholly shared field at equal rates with a Hamiltonian that conserves the number of excitations, in the sodium nucleus at extreme narrowing with a Hamiltonian that is a rotation (an offset, a lock), and, under Z dephasing, for the terms that never change a string's number of X and Y letters, the ZZ bonds and the Z field ([the one square](THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §8). A hopping term changes that number, and then the grey rooms are particular: the boundary blocks of the next section, the Jordan-Wigner modes, a frozen corner, a blind seat's room.

Coloured glass is a room the Hamiltonian keeps on which the watching takes more than one value. Unequal fading alone already changes the shape there; the motion adds that it carries the state between the parts, and the block can turn non-normal. [PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL](proofs/PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL.md) solves the first coloured glass next to the grey, the bi-chromatic blocks with two Hamming distances, L = L_H − 2γh₊·Id + 4γ𝒫 with 𝒫 the projector onto the cells at the lower one. A room where μ = 0 pays nothing; that is the sense of dark on this page, the one the glossary asks a writer to name. Under watching on every site such a room holds only diagonal operators ([PROOF_ASYMPTOTIC_SECTOR_PROJECTION](proofs/PROOF_ASYMPTOTIC_SECTOR_PROJECTION.md) Step 2(a)), and since the commutator of H with a diagonal operator has no diagonal, the ones it keeps commute with H and do not even turn; a wholly shared field instead makes every fixed-popcount subspace decoherence-free ([the mirror under a shared field](../experiments/THE_MIRROR_UNDER_A_SHARED_FIELD.md)).

## On the cube

Under Z dephasing at the rate γ on every site the dissipator charges a Pauli string 2γ·k_Z, the number of its X and Y letters, its height on [the letter cube](THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) (§7), and moves nothing. A cell |a⟩⟨b| expands into strings that carry X or Y exactly where a and b disagree (§10, [PROOF_CHROMATICITY](proofs/PROOF_CHROMATICITY.md) Lemma 2), so the cell stands at the height of its Hamming distance. Under equal light the grey glass is a room of H inside one height plane.

Why some rooms stay in a plane, in words: when one side of a cell holds no excitation, the cell disagrees exactly where the other side holds one, so its height is that side's number of excitations, and a Hamiltonian that conserves the number never changes it. At N = 2 the cells |00⟩⟨01| and |00⟩⟨10| each carry one X or Y letter; the bond moves that letter from one site to the other and keeps the height at one. The same holds with "full" for "empty" and holes for excitations.

So among the joint-popcount blocks (p, q), the cells with p excitations in the ket and q in the bra, which every number-conserving Hamiltonian keeps, a block lies in one plane exactly when one of its sides is empty or full, min(p, q) = 0 or max(p, q) = N: the 4N boundary blocks, [F153](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f153)'s pinned blocks and RENEWAL's border, of chromaticity one. On any other block the Hamming distances run in steps of two from |p − q| to min(p + q, 2N − p − q), the window F153 writes. On a boundary block L − μ·Id is L_H entry for entry, μ = −2γ|p − q| (gate G1, the planes at N = 2 to 7, the full generator of an XXZ chain and ring with integer couplings and Z fields at N = 3 and 4, and the block (1, 1) as the control on which no μ works). F153 proves that these are exactly the blocks that sit entirely on their floor.

**Whole blocks need equal light.** With a rate γ_l per site a cell's charge is 2·Σ γ_l over its disagreeing sites, a function of the sites and no longer of the point (§8). F153 already fences its criterion to uniform γ, bar the four single cells; the argument for every block runs as follows. On a block (0, q) with 0 < q < N the cells are the q-subsets of the sites, and their prices agree only if every q-subset has the same rate sum; exchanging one site of a subset for one outside it shows that this needs every rate equal. The blocks (q, 0), (N, q) and (q, N) follow by exchanging ket and bra and by flipping every bit, which keeps every disagreement set. Every block off the boundary holds two cells whose disagreement sets are nested, and positive rates price them apart. So with positive rates not all equal only the four single cells stay grey among the blocks (G2 at the rates 1, …, N). What the profile then does to the four edge blocks, (0, 1) and its mirrors with n_diff = 1, is followed in [F152](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f152) and [PROOF_EDGE_BLOCK_DEFECTIVE_UNDER_PROFILE](proofs/PROOF_EDGE_BLOCK_DEFECTIVE_UNDER_PROFILE.md): the block turns non-normal, and on the XY chain an exceptional point is one knob away.

**Rooms need equal light only where they live.** A room smaller than a block stays grey when the watching is one number on the sites its cells disagree at, however the rest is watched. [F157](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f157)'s blind subspace, the largest subspace of single-excitation states the Hamiltonian keeps inside the kernel of a watched seat's occupation, is the case μ = 0: its entry and [THE_BLIND_SITE](../experiments/THE_BLIND_SITE.md) count with it the undamped modes of F152's coherence block, the operators |0…0⟩⟨φ|, whose adjoints |φ⟩⟨0…0| are the room below. On the uniform open XY chain at N = 5, with the seats counted from 0, φ = (1, 0, −1, 0, 1) is blind at the seats 1 and 3 together, and |φ⟩⟨0…0| is annihilated by the full generator with those two seats watched at the rates 1 and 7, and moved when seat 0 is watched (G3). With every rate positive and the rates not all equal the room is grey without being dark: on the open XY chain at N = 3 with the rates (1, 5, 1), |φ⟩⟨000| with φ = (1, 0, −1) is an eigenvector of the full generator at μ = −2, the two sites φ lives on watched equally (G3).

## Coherences that pay nothing lie in the grey

[The mirror under a shared field](../experiments/THE_MIRROR_UNDER_A_SHARED_FIELD.md) classifies the dark cells, those commuting with every jump, at every noise matrix a ⪰ 0. ## Grey rooms inside coloured blocks: the two ladders

[PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4](proofs/PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4.md) §1 reads a cell |A⟩⟨B| as two species of fermions, the ket's excitations one and the bra's the other. Under equal light the watching then counts the sites that hold exactly one of the two, −2γ|AΔB| = −2γ(p + q) + 4γ·|A ∩ B|: a number per block plus a price on every doubly occupied site, the contact term of the Hubbard model at imaginary interaction that [the literature review](LITERATURE_REVIEW.md) credits to Medvedyeva, Essler and Prosen. In that picture a grey room is one along whose whole motion the number of doubly occupied sites stays fixed. The same document's §2 holds two ladders: the η ladder Φ(ρ) = Σ_l d†_l ρ d_l, which takes (p, q) to (p + 1, q + 1), and the spin ladder S⁺(ρ) = Σ_l (−1)^l d†_l ρ d†_l, which takes (p, q) to (p + 1, q − 1), with d_l the chain's Jordan-Wigner fermions. Neither moves a disagreement set, at any profile (Lemma 2.1); Φ commutes with the Hamiltonian part for every single-particle hopping matrix h (Lemma 2.2), the XY chain's at any couplings, and S⁺ for a real symmetric h exactly when Σ h Σ = −h, Σ = diag((−1)^l) (Lemma 2.3), which the XY chain meets at any real couplings. A map that commutes with L_H and with D carries a grey room onto a grey room at the same μ, so each ladder does. Under equal light a grey room is spanned by joint eigenvectors of the two parts, so its eigenvectors of L do not move with γ: they are the eigenstates of [XY_FROZEN_BAND](../experiments/XY_FROZEN_BAND.md)'s Hubbard operator, the two-copy hopping plus U times the double occupancy with U = 4γ, that hold at every U.

**Every coloured block holds the ladder images of the boundary blocks.** A boundary block (k, 0) is annihilated by S⁺ and by Φ's adjoint, since both would empty the bra below zero, so it is a highest weight of the spin ladder, spin k/2, and a lowest weight of the η ladder, η-spin (N − k)/2. The two ladders commute (they are the two SU(2)s of the Hubbard model), so S⁻^s Φ^r, with S⁻ the adjoint of S⁺ and the order of the two immaterial, is one-to-one on (k, 0) for s ≤ k and r ≤ N − k. With s = (k − p + q)/2 steps down the spin ladder and r = q − s up the η ladder it reaches the block (p, q), p ≥ q, and the two bounds hold exactly when k lies in the block's Hamming window, |p − q| ≤ k ≤ min(p + q, 2N − p − q) in steps of two (the blocks with p < q follow by the transpose). So on the XY chain under equal light **every block (p, q) holds, for each Hamming distance k of its window, a grey room of dimension at least C(N, k) at −2γk, the ladder image of the boundary block (k, 0)** (G8 on the uniform chain, N = 3 to 6: every step intertwines the Hamiltonian part exactly, and the images sit at the one distance k with full rank). The row k = 1 is already certified: `FreeFermionFloorWitness` reads the family Wⁿ(P₀·c_j) in the blocks (n, n + 1), with W the spectator map of [PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY](proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md), which is Φ, applied to the boundary block (0, 1), N modes at exactly −2γ per block, and `OneSidedSectorClosedForm` lists the ladder images of the one-sided sectors among the pieces of the two-sided sectors that close without the imaginary-interaction Bethe ansatz. What the window adds is every k and the spin ladder beside the η one. The fold by X^⊗N on one side, which carries (p, q) to (p, N − q) and every Hamming distance k to N − k ([PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL](proofs/PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL.md), the moves of the cube that carry blocks to blocks), commutes with L_H on the XY chain and sends D to −2γN − D, so it carries the grey room of (p, q) at distance k onto that of (p, N − q) at N − k.

**The block (1, 1).** Its window is k ∈ {0, 2}. At 0 the ladder image is Φ of the vacuum cell, the identity on one excitation. At 2 it is S⁻ of the block (2, 0), whose states are antisymmetric in the two sites: the spin triplet in the doubled picture, whose two excitations never share a site, so the double occupancy stays zero along the motion. [PROOF_R90_FROZEN_DIVISOR](proofs/PROOF_R90_FROZEN_DIVISOR.md) §9.1 owns this half as the odd half of the involution U v_(a,b) = (−1)^(a+b)·v_(b,a), with v_(a,b) the cell of the sites a and b, the transpose composed with Σ: for a real symmetric h, U commutes with L_H exactly when h hops only between sites of opposite parity and has a constant diagonal. U fixes every population, so its odd half holds none; §9.2 shows that S⁺ is one-to-one on it and zero on the even half, and identifies it as the block (2, 0) recentred, and [PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL](proofs/PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL.md) reads it as living "on the envelope alone, as a block of chromaticity one does". On the cube, in words: the transpose turns the sign of every Y letter, Σ turns X and Y on every other site, and neither changes a height. The transpose reverses the order in a commutator and keeps the bond's letters XX + YY; Σ keeps the order and reverses the bond; so the commutator with the bond passes through U unchanged. On the quiet edge, the letters I and Z, both act as the identity, so an operator odd under U has no weight there, and in (1, 1) the only other height is 2.

**On the uniform open XY chain nothing more is grey in (1, 1).** The largest room at −4γ there is the set of operators whose whole motion under ad_h has no diagonal. ad_h is diagonalizable, so that room is the sum, over the frequencies ω, of the kernel of the diagonal map on the eigenspace E_ω. U commutes with ad_h and keeps every diagonal entry, so it keeps each E_ω, the diagonal map kills the odd part of E_ω, and what is left to show is that it is injective on the even part. With θ = π/(N + 1) the modes of h are ψ_a(l) ∝ sin(alθ), a = 1, …, N, at energies proportional to cos(aθ), and Σψ_a = ±ψ_ā with ā = N + 1 − a (`ChiralKClaim`'s spectrum inversion), so U sends |ψ_a⟩⟨ψ_b| to ±|ψ_b̄⟩⟨ψ_ā|, and the even part of E_ω has at most one basis vector per orbit {(a, b), (b̄, ā)} at the frequency ω. Put x = |a − b| and y the smaller of a + b and 2N + 2 − (a + b), so 0 ≤ x < y ≤ N + 1. The orbit's frequency is a multiple of s_x·s_y, s_m = sin(mθ/2), with the sign of a − b, and its diagonal is a multiple of c_x − c_y, c_m(l) = cos(mlθ), by the product formula for two sines. Draw each orbit of E_ω as an edge between x and y. No two orbits share an edge: x, y and the sign of a − b fix a − b and leave a + b to be y or 2N + 2 − y, and those two solutions are (a, b) and (b̄, ā), one orbit; the other sign gives the opposite frequency, and at x = 0 there is no sign. The graph has no cycle: at ω = 0 every edge ends at 0; at ω ≠ 0 none does, and s_m rises strictly on 0, …, N + 1, so two edges {u, v} and {v, w} of one frequency force u = w. The N + 2 vectors c_0, …, c_{N+1} on the sites 1, …, N have rank N, since the cosine transform of type I on N + 2 points is invertible, and their two relations carry the coefficients ε_m and (−1)^m·ε_m, with ε_m = 1/2 at m = 0 and m = N + 1 and 1 between. A relation among the edges' diagonals gives each vertex m the net weight v_m of its edges, and v must be a combination of the two relations. At ω ≠ 0 the vertex 0 is absent, which leaves a multiple of ε on the odd m; at ω = 0 the vertices are 0 and even, which leaves a multiple of ε on the even m. Either way v has one sign wherever it is not zero, while the net weights over each connected piece of the graph sum to zero, so v = 0, and on a forest zero net weights make every edge weight zero. The room at 0 is the identity alone: it holds only diagonal operators the hopping keeps (the sentence on dark rooms above), which commute with h, and on the connected chain that leaves the multiples of the identity. ∎

So in (1, 1) the operators that stay on one height are the two ladder images and nothing else, C(N, 2) + 1 eigenmodes, 11 at N = 5 (G7 counts the room again, at N = 3 to 12). On the other blocks the room meets the ladder bound C(N, k) at N = 3 to 6 everywhere but at k = N/2 where that distance lies strictly inside the block's window, |p − q| < N/2 < min(p + q, 2N − p − q): the block (2, 2) at N = 4 holds 8 against 6, and the blocks (3, 2) and (4, 3) at N = 6, with their transposes, hold 22 against 20 (G8, exact ranks over the integers; (3, 2) and (4, 3) are each other's fold up to the transpose). **The chain's reflection builds the operators more.** The reflection is F71's spatial mirror, site l against site N + 1 − l, written R as a matrix on one excitation (not the cube's R, X^⊗N on one side); at even N with couplings it keeps, the sites fall into the mirror pairs of F75 and F76. **The right half is the bra.** On the open XY chain at even N, read site l ≤ M = N/2 (not PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4's M = N + 1) as the ket of site l of the half chain and its mirror site N + 1 − l as the bra of the same site. **The antipodal map: opposite sites, no flip.** Number the bonds J_l between the sites l and l + 1, with J_N = J_w. No bond lies inside an antipodal pair, so there is no flip, and every hop moves a particle into another pair: it moves out of both the configurations with one particle in every pair and those with one pair full and the rest empty, and an eigenstate on either sits at 0, whatever the couplings. **Every division into pairs, and the rings to N = 12.** The paragraph above finds the sum of full pairs for a division into pairs that the couplings keep exactly when its pairs form a bipartite graph; with positive couplings the converse holds too, and one relation, for couplings of either sign, says why. Write ι for the involution that exchanges the two sites of every pair, J(x, y) for the coupling of the sites x and y, 0 where they share no bond, and s_[x] for the coefficient of the full pair that holds x. For a bond (a, b) between two pairs, the configuration holding ιa and b is reached only from the full pair of a, by the hop a → b, and from the full pair of b, by the hop ιb → ιa, and the configuration holding a and ιb likewise, so J(a, b) s_[a] + J(ιa, ιb) s_[b] = 0 and J(ιa, ιb) s_[a] + J(a, b) s_[b] = 0; a nonzero coefficient needs |J(a, b)| = |J(ιa, ιb)|, and a zero coefficient spreads to every pair bonded to it. Read with h_s, the matrix of H's hopping amplitudes in spin language (J_w on the wrap bond, where the Jordan-Wigner h of the ladders carries −J_w at an even number of particles), a state of two excitations is a symmetric matrix ψ with zero diagonal, on which H acts as h_sψ + ψh_s with the diagonal struck out. - **How large the grey rooms are inside coloured blocks.** For each μ the largest kept room is well defined. Its dimension is known for the plane of one letter on the open XY chain, for the rooms that pay nothing in the vacuum coherence block under one watched seat (F157), for the centre-watched (1, 1) block at μ = 0 (the node pair, m² + 1), and for the block (1, 1) of the uniform open XY chain under equal light (C(N, 2) at −4γ, the identity at 0); beyond that scope THE_BLIND_SITE keeps it open. On the other blocks of the XY chain the ladders give C(N, k) from below at every N; that this is all is measured at N = 3 to 6 only, and at k = N/2 strictly inside the window it is not all: two operators more at N = 4 and at N = 6 and in the block (4, 2) at N = 8, at least two in (3, 3) and at least four in (4, 4) there, built by the chain's reflection from the filled mirror orbitals and the staggered mirror pairs; that nothing beyond them and the ladders is grey is measured at N = 4, 6 and in (4, 2) at N = 8, and open elsewhere. On the even ring the room at k = N/2 in (N/2, 2) is, at the rings whose rooms G10, G11 and [`simulations/grey_glass_ring_rooms.py`](../simulations/grey_glass_ring_rooms.py) measure, the ladder images with the products of the bond axes and, where the ring keeps it, of the antipodal map at N = 4, 8 and 12, and the bond axes' products alone at N = 6 and 10; the divisions into pairs that carry a sum of full pairs are known exactly, but that nothing else is grey is not proved, and beyond those rings it is open. The ladder images of (k, 0) on the even ring are kept at every even k; that at odd k, with the wrap bond nonzero, they are kept only where they fill their block is proved at N = 4, 6 and 8 and open beyond. On the odd ring the images that do not reach their block from a corner by the ladder their parity of k allows are not kept at N = 3, 5 and 7, measured and not proved.
- **What colours a grey block from outside, and by which law.** Under damping a block keeps its own grey and is coloured only by what the block above feeds into it. With heating and cooling together the feed runs both ways, and how the colour grows with the two rates is not gated here.
- **"Only turned" as a road to grey.** On the ring threaded by a π flux, unequal light leaves the inverse particle current at the frozen root at second order because at first order the offset only turns it ([PROOF_RING_FROZEN_BAND](proofs/PROOF_RING_FROZEN_BAND.md) Proposition 8); at fourth order it is lifted, because no frozen mode carries the trace. Whether turning keeps other rooms grey to some order is not known.
