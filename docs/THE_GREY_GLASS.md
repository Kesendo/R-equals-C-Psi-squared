## What this is about

A picture behind a pane of grey glass comes through darker and otherwise whole: every colour loses the same share. Behind coloured glass some colours pass and others do not, and the picture itself changes. In this repository a chain of quantum bits [stands in the light](quantum/DEPHASING_TRANSLATED.md) of its surroundings; light is the repository's word, a figure, for what the surroundings send into the chain. That light dims the parts of the chain's state in which the chain is still two ways at once, shimmering against each other, and leaves alone the plain facts of how much is where. In the image, the glass is that dimming, and the picture behind it is the motion that the chain's own couplings give the state. The page asks one question: where does the motion come through whole?

The answer is one sentence. Wherever the light takes the same share of everything a part of the state can reach as it moves, that part moves exactly as it would without the light and only shrinks, and where the share is nothing it does not even shrink; its shape is untouched either way. The page calls such a part a grey room: a place the motion never leaves, in which the light is one shade. The repository draws a state as a square of cells, its rows and columns the ways the chain could be, so that each cell joins two of those ways; it sorts the cells into blocks by how many excitations, raised bits, each of a cell's two ways holds, and it already had a word for the glass's colour, coined for one family of blocks and read here on every block: each of the rates at which the light alone dims the cells of a block is [one of that block's tones](proofs/PROOF_CHROMATICITY.md), and a block with one tone is monochromatic. Grey glass is the one-tone case, met on a whole block or on a part of one, either way one the motion never leaves.

The page proves the sentence and its converse, that nothing else comes through whole, and gathers rooms the repository had already found one at a time under other names, among them the blocks in which one of every cell's two ways is empty or full, no bit raised or every bit raised, where every site is lit alike; [the patterns of a ring, a chain closed on itself, that fade without turning](proofs/PROOF_RING_FROZEN_BAND.md); [the rooms built on the states that light on a single site leaves untouched](../experiments/THE_BLIND_SITE.md); and three more settings in which such rooms were met, [a shared light that all sites stand in together](../experiments/THE_MIRROR_UNDER_A_SHARED_FIELD.md), [a loss of excitations](../experiments/THERMAL_BREAKING.md), and [the sodium nucleus](../experiments/THE_SODIUM_NUCLEUS_TWO_MIRRORS.md).

Then it goes looking for grey rooms inside the blocks that are coloured as a whole, on the chain whose couplings only pass an excitation from site to site, every site lit alike. ## Abstract

Let the generator be L = L_H + D with L_H = −i[H, ·] and D the dissipator. On a subspace V that L_H keeps and on which D acts as one real number μ, e^{tL} = e^{μt}·e^{tL_H}: the motion is the Hamiltonian's and only the size fades. The converse holds state by state: a state's motion comes through whole exactly when D is one number on the span of its motion, and for each μ there is a largest grey room, the largest L_H-invariant subspace inside the eigenspace of D at μ. ## The sentence

Let H be Hermitian, L_H = −i[H, ·], and D the dissipator, so the generator is L = L_H + D. Let V be a subspace of operator space that L_H keeps, L_H V ⊆ V, and on which D acts as one real number μ. Then L keeps V, and on V

  **e^{tL} = e^{μt}·e^{tL_H}.**

On V, L is L_H + μ·Id, and the identity commutes with everything. L_H is anti-Hermitian in the Hilbert-Schmidt product, and a subspace an anti-Hermitian map keeps is kept by its adjoint as well, so L_H on V is anti-Hermitian: diagonalizable, with imaginary eigenvalues. Three things follow on V. Every eigenvalue of L there has real part μ. No two of its modes can meet in an exceptional point; where D is Hermitian, as under every kind of Z dephasing here, L† keeps V too, so V splits off orthogonally and no exceptional point anywhere involves its modes, as `FreeFermionFloorWitness` says of its family (under damping D is not Hermitian and only the weaker statement stands). And a component started in V is at every time the component the Hamiltonian alone would have made, times e^{μt}: its shape is untouched and only its size fades, so its overlap with the run without light, both normalised, is one at every time. A read linear in the state carries the factor e^{μt} and nothing else of the light, so its zeros are those of the run without light; [the cracked bell](../experiments/THE_CRACKED_BELL.md) writes exactly this on the block (0, 1), the vacuum coherence |vac⟩⟨φ|, and draws its γ-free zero crossings from it.

**The converse, state by state.** e^{tL}ρ = e^{μt}e^{tL_H}ρ at every t exactly when D = μ on the space that ρ's motion reaches, the span of the L_H^k ρ: differentiating both sides gives D = μ on every e^{tL_H}ρ, and those span that space. So each state carries its own room, and its motion comes through whole exactly when the light is one number there. For each μ there is a largest room, the largest subspace L_H keeps inside the eigenspace of D at μ; it is unique, because a sum of subspaces kept inside one eigenspace is kept inside it. Control theory knows that object as the unobservable subspace of the pair (L_H, D − μ). At μ = 0 it is the maximal invariant subspace inside the kernel of the dissipator that [the node pair](proofs/PROOF_NODE_PAIR_RESOLVENT.md) §8 computes for the centre-watched (1, 1) block, m² + 1 at N = 2m + 1, and calls peripheral because its restriction is anti-Hermitian. [CODIM1's window-edge lemma](proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md) is the converse at the level of one eigenvector: an eigenvalue on the edge of a block's rate window has an eigenvector on which the dissipator is one number.

**Rooms everywhere, or one room.** [L_H, D] = 0 holds exactly when L_H keeps every eigenspace of D (D being diagonalizable in every case here); then the whole operator space is a sum of grey rooms. That happens under a wholly shared field at equal rates with a Hamiltonian that conserves the number of excitations, in the sodium nucleus at extreme narrowing with a Hamiltonian that is a rotation (an offset, a lock), and, under Z dephasing, for the terms that never change a string's number of X and Y letters, the ZZ bonds and the Z field ([the one square](THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §8). A hopping term changes that number, and then the grey rooms are particular: the boundary blocks of the next section, the Jordan-Wigner modes, a frozen corner, a blind seat's room.

Coloured glass is a room the Hamiltonian keeps on which the light takes more than one value. Unequal fading alone already changes the shape there; the motion adds that it carries the state between the parts, and the block can turn non-normal. [PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL](proofs/PROOF_DEPHASING_FRONT_RENEWAL.md) solves the first coloured glass next to the grey, the bi-chromatic blocks with two Hamming distances, L = L_H − 2γh₊·Id + 4γ𝒫 with 𝒫 the projector onto the cells at the lower one. A room where μ = 0 pays nothing; that is the sense of dark on this page, the one the glossary asks a writer to name. Under light on every site such a room holds only diagonal operators ([PROOF_ASYMPTOTIC_SECTOR_PROJECTION](proofs/PROOF_ASYMPTOTIC_SECTOR_PROJECTION.md) Step 2(a)), and since the commutator of H with a diagonal operator has no diagonal, the ones it keeps commute with H and do not even turn; a wholly shared field instead makes every fixed-popcount subspace decoherence-free ([the mirror under a shared field](../experiments/THE_MIRROR_UNDER_A_SHARED_FIELD.md)).

## On the cube

Under Z dephasing at the rate γ on every site the dissipator charges a Pauli string 2γ·k_Z, the number of its X and Y letters, its height on [the letter cube](THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) (§7), and moves nothing. A cell |a⟩⟨b| expands into strings that carry X or Y exactly where a and b disagree (§10, [PROOF_CHROMATICITY](proofs/PROOF_CHROMATICITY.md) Lemma 2), so the cell stands at the height of its Hamming distance. Under equal light the grey glass is a room of H inside one height plane.

Why some rooms stay in a plane, in words: when one side of a cell holds no excitation, the cell disagrees exactly where the other side holds one, so its height is that side's number of excitations, and a Hamiltonian that conserves the number never changes it. At N = 2 the cells |00⟩⟨01| and |00⟩⟨10| each carry one X or Y letter; the bond moves that letter from one site to the other and keeps the height at one. The same holds with "full" for "empty" and holes for excitations.

So among the joint-popcount blocks (p, q), the cells with p excitations in the ket and q in the bra, which every number-conserving Hamiltonian keeps, a block lies in one plane exactly when one of its sides is empty or full, min(p, q) = 0 or max(p, q) = N: the 4N boundary blocks, [F153](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f153)'s pinned blocks and RENEWAL's border, of chromaticity one ([F74](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f74), whose count this page reads on every block). On any other block the Hamming distances run in steps of two from |p − q| to min(p + q, 2N − p − q), the window F153 writes. On a boundary block L − μ·Id is L_H entry for entry, μ = −2γ|p − q| (gate G1, the planes at N = 2 to 7, the full generator of an XXZ chain and ring with integer couplings and Z fields at N = 3 and 4, and the block (1, 1) as the control on which no μ works). F153 proves that these are exactly the blocks that sit entirely on their floor.

**Whole blocks need equal light.** With a rate γ_l per site a cell's charge is 2·Σ γ_l over its disagreeing sites, a function of the sites and no longer of the point (§8). F153 already fences its criterion to uniform γ, bar the four single cells; the argument for every block runs as follows. On a block (0, q) with 0 < q < N the cells are the q-subsets of the sites, and their prices agree only if every q-subset has the same rate sum; exchanging one site of a subset for one outside it shows that this needs every rate equal. The blocks (q, 0), (N, q) and (q, N) follow by exchanging ket and bra and by flipping every bit, which keeps every disagreement set. Every block off the boundary holds two cells whose disagreement sets are nested, and positive rates price them apart. So with positive rates not all equal only the four single cells stay grey among the blocks (G2 at the rates 1, …, N). What the profile then does to the four edge blocks, (0, 1) and its mirrors with n_diff = 1, is followed in [F152](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f152) and [PROOF_EDGE_BLOCK_DEFECTIVE_UNDER_PROFILE](proofs/PROOF_EDGE_BLOCK_DEFECTIVE_UNDER_PROFILE.md): the block turns non-normal, and on the XY chain an exceptional point is one knob away.

**Rooms need equal light only where they live.** A room smaller than a block stays grey when the light is one number on the sites its cells disagree at, however the rest is lit. [F157](ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f157)'s blind subspace, the largest subspace of single-excitation states the Hamiltonian keeps inside the kernel of a watched seat's occupation, is the case μ = 0: its entry and [THE_BLIND_SITE](../experiments/THE_BLIND_SITE.md) count with it the undamped modes of F152's coherence block, the operators |0…0⟩⟨φ|, whose adjoints |φ⟩⟨0…0| are the room below.
