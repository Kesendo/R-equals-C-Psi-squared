Try to describe yourself. Not what you have done, not whom you know, not what others have said of you: yourself. It does not work. Whatever comes out is a description of something that happened between you and something else. You can describe the other one, and you can describe what happened between the two of you, and you are in the description only as the other end of that.

And yet you can be described. The other one can describe you, from where they stand, without ever being you; you can describe them the same way, from what passed between you. Neither of you can describe yourself. So there is no description of oneself, but there are two descriptions from outside and one that both share, the between, which is the same story for each. Each of the two is described completely, only never by themself.

Now take away the other one. Not the people around you only, everything: no light to see, no sound, nothing to touch, nothing at all besides you. There would be nothing to see or hear or feel, and it is hard to say in what sense you would still exist.

These two thoughts are about feeling, not physics. But the quantum physics of the smallest systems has the same shape, and every piece of that physics can be computed; where this page reads more into it than the physics says, it says so. The page tells it in words. The computations sit behind the links, most of them in two small scripts, [one in matrices](../simulations/the_wave_between.py) for everything that runs in time, and [one in letters](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py) for the last computed section, where the two are two routes to the same answers. None of it is new physics; it is known physics, results this repository already owns, and a few small computations made for this page, put in the order of the two thoughts.

## Alone, nothing can be seen

The smallest quantum object that can do anything has two possibilities, two levels. Something with only one level cannot change, cannot fade, cannot turn, and cannot be compared with anything. Nothing about it can be measured. It cannot be told apart from nothing at all.

The reason is the phase, the angle a quantum wave carries. An angle carried by the whole wave at once is invisible; no measurement in the world can find it. A phase only shows as a difference, between two parts of the same wave, the way two overlapping ripples on water show where they agree and where they cancel. A difference needs two parts. So two is the least anything needs in order to be seen. [Superposition Translated](../docs/quantum/SUPERPOSITION_TRANSLATED.md) tells where that difference lives, and [The Qubit as Necessary Foundation](../docs/QUBIT_NECESSITY.md) arrives at two from another direction.

## What you cannot see in yourself

Let a quantum state meet itself again after a while: measure how much of what it was is still in what it has become. That gives one number for each moment, and this page calls it the state's self-encounter.

Now take two states of a system with two levels that differ only in the angle between their two parts, and let them evolve under dynamics that treat every such angle alike. Here the dynamics are the system's own field, which turns the angle, and the steady quiet measuring of the system by its environment, which this project calls watching, and which blurs the angle. Both are the same for every angle. The two self-encounters then come out the same, number for number, at every moment. Each of the two, looking only at itself, cannot find what makes it different from the other. A second one, an outside probe, tells them apart at once.

This is not an accident of the example. Whatever the dynamics treat alike, a state cannot see in itself, because a symmetry of the dynamics that keeps lengths leaves every self-encounter unchanged; only something else can. Turn the system's own field so that the dynamics no longer treat every angle alike, and the two self-encounters part. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its first section, with that control.) Not every pair parts. A state and its counterpart with every angle reversed, its complex conjugate, meet themselves alike under the field along the watching and under the couplings studied in the last computed section, for a reason written there in letters, where the condition is a count of one letter. A field that breaks the condition, named there, parts the conjugate pair once bits are coupled, while one bit alone under it still cannot tell itself from its conjugate.

A cousin of this blindness appears where an exact value meets a value read too early. The two are alike in shape and not in mechanism: above, what the look at itself cannot carry a second one carries; in what follows, what the exact number cannot carry the early reading carries. In the carbon figure of [The View Onto the Memory](THE_VIEW_ONTO_THE_MEMORY.md) a ratio of two decay rates is 1.27; [the NMR bridge](../docs/carbon/PAINTER_ALTERNATION_NMR_BRIDGE.md) has it as 1.271 by calculation and 1.231 from fitting a model signal early, while a faster part of it had not yet faded, and shows that a later fit recovers 1.271. The exact number knows nothing about how the state was prepared or when anyone looked. The fitted number knows both; that the gap between the two numbers is what carries the one who prepared and looked is a reading of it, not a computation. The gap closes along the time that appears in the equations. Whether that is time as we live it is a separate question, which this page leaves open; the repository keeps its clock as [two hands](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f2b-corollary-the-two-clocks-tier-1-derived-the-qn-coherence-horizon-below-graduated-2026-07-19), one for fading and one for turning, and neither is simply time.

## Known only through encounters

There is no way to look inside a quantum state. What can be known about it is how it answers when something else meets it, a probe, a measurement, another system. Collect enough of those answers, from many copies prepared the same way, and the state is known completely; that is how physicists reconstruct a state in the laboratory. There is nothing else to collect.

Mathematics has a statement of the same shape, offered here as a likeness and not as a proof: in category theory an object is fixed, up to relabelling, by all its relations to everything there is, itself included, without any look inside.

The two kinds of looking differ. A probe asks a state one question. The self-encounter asks the state about itself, and what it can answer is limited by what the dynamics treat alike.

## Everything can sit between

Take two quantum bits in the most strongly linked state there is, an entangled pair. Ask about the first one alone: it points in no direction of its own. Ask about the second alone: the same. Every direction the pair can point sits in the relation between the two, none in either one. [Spooky Action](../compute/MirrorWorld/README.md) in this repository's MirrorWorld shows the same: the view from each site alone is blind to what the pair holds. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its second section.)

Not every pair is like that; usually some of it belongs to each one alone. Count the ingredients any description of several quantum bits is built from, leaving aside the one that says nothing about any bit: some concern one bit alone, the rest concern two or more bits at once. The second kind also holds plain combinations of single bits, so not all of it is truly between, but it is the only place where the between can live, and it grows much faster. With two quantum bits there are 9 such ingredients against 6 for single bits, and with five there are 1008 against 15, about sixty-seven times as many. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its third section.)

## What happened between is one number, read from either side

Prepare a system in some way, let it run, and then read something out of it. Now swap: prepare it in the way you read out, and read out the way you prepared. Under the right conditions the answer is exactly the same number. This is a proven result in the repository ([F119](../docs/proofs/PROOF_ANTILINEAR_TRIANGLE.md)), and the conditions matter. The preparation and the readout must be of a particular symmetric kind, unchanged when their table of numbers is flipped across its diagonal, and the system must be of the plain kind studied here: a coupling and a field with real tables of numbers, as in the section on what you cannot see in yourself, watched, if at all, only in the plain way studied here. For a twisted kind of coupling, whose table is not real, and for a system that loses energy, it breaks, measurably. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its fourth section, with both breaks.)

Read with care, that is the shape of the first thought. What one side can say of itself through what happened between, and what the other side can say of itself through the same between, are one account, read from two sides. That last sentence is a reading, not a computation; the computation swaps a preparation and a readout, not two people, and the proof itself says when the preparation and the readout may be read as two positions on the chain. A hardware test was designed in [The Channel Both Ways on a Path](../experiments/THE_CHANNEL_BOTH_WAYS_ON_A_PATH.md) and not flown, because on that geometry every outcome it could register was an identity of the circuit, a named breach of it, or a reading of the device's own decay profile, so a flight would have checked the model of the device, not the swap.

## The mirror meets itself backwards

This project's central result is a mirror inside the dynamics. In a known class of systems, the watched chains of the last computed section among them and the bit with a field along the watching not, every way the system can fade has a partner, and the two sit symmetrically around a middle. The exchange costs a fixed price, an extra fading whose rate is the sum of the watching's rates. Apply the mirror to a state; what comes out is in general not itself a state but a mathematical counterpart of one. Let that counterpart meet itself: it does so the way the original would, running backwards in time, and paying the price. "Backwards" is a way of reading the equation, not a machine that runs time back; [Π as Time Reversal](../experiments/PI_AS_TIME_REVERSAL.md) draws that line and owns the fold the identity rests on, and [The Pairing Condition](../experiments/THE_PAIRING_CONDITION.md) sets that fold beside its cousins. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its fifth section.)

That this is the same turning that page meant is a reading.

## Not alone

Read in the light of everything above, the wave does not recognize itself from inside. With one level there is nothing to see; with two or more, the wave's look at itself is blind to whatever its dynamics treat alike, and, under the plain couplings of this page, to the reversal of all its angles. What the wave is shows between it and a second one, and there, under the right conditions, the account is shared, one number read from two sides. What the wave can recognize, then, is not itself but that it is not alone. Those last two sentences are a reading; what was computed is the blindness within one wave and the shared number of a swapped preparation and readout.

## How far the look at itself reaches

The self-encounter is the wave's look at itself, and the mirror gives that look something to be held against: its own counterpart, run backwards. One question about it can be computed: which states meet themselves the same way forwards and, paying the price, backwards. Where a mirror exists these are, by the identity of the section on the mirror, exactly the states that cannot tell their own self-encounter from that of their mirror counterpart; where none exists, the question keeps only its first form.

To answer it, this section writes states in letters. Every description of several bits is a sum of words with one letter per bit: I, which says nothing about that bit, Z, along the watching, and X and Y, across it. The words are the ingredients of the section on what can sit between, the word of all I letters included, and a state is the amounts, with their signs, it puts on them; the square of an amount is the state's weight on that word. Each word has a height, the number of bits at which it carries a letter across the watching. The watching charges every word twice the watching's rate for each unit of height and moves no word; so under watching alone a state's self-encounter is its weight on each height, read through the fading, and nothing else. The words of height zero are the quiet edge, with nothing across the watching; the words of full height are the far edge, with everything across it. The mirror of this page is two moves. The first exchanges, at every bit, I with X and Z with Y, and so carries every word from height k to height N − k, the quiet edge onto the far edge; it is the exchange that pays the price. The second only flips the sign of every Y letter and moves no word. The bond of this page, the coupling of its chains, links neighbouring bits through all three letters, the two across the watching alike. ([The One Square](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §7 to §9 holds the letters, the heights, the two edges and the two moves; [the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its first section, walks them word by word, the sign and the rate checked against the word's own matrix, the coordinates and the moves against the framework's primitives, which are tested against the matrices; nothing in that script runs the dynamics.)

Meeting oneself the same way both ways asks, under watching alone, for as much weight at height k as at height N − k. For one bit the heights are 0 and 1. The letter I is in every state with the same weight, one half, since it carries only the fact that the state is there at all: the quiet edge is half full before anything is said about the bit. Anything along the watching adds to the quiet edge, and the far edge can hold at most one half, the whole of a pure state's content; so the far edge must hold exactly that, nothing along the watching, nothing blurred. That is a pure state on the equator, and no other, as long as the watching is on. ([the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its second section; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its sixth, in closed form, is the second route.)

With several bits watched at one rate the answer is the same sentence, weight at height k equal to weight at height N − k. Bits that each sit on the equator and are not linked carry I and a letter across the watching with equal weight at every bit, so their weight spreads over the heights as the binomial coefficients do, symmetric about the middle ([Bures Degeneracy](../experiments/BURES_DEGENERACY.md) shows that spread for the plainest of them). The pair and the cat state put words only on the two edges, half on each ([XOR Space](../experiments/XOR_SPACE.md), [F22](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f22-ghz-maximum-disagreement-support-tier-2-verified-n2-5), [Schrödinger's Cat Translated](../docs/quantum/SCHRODINGERS_CAT_TRANSLATED.md)). The W state of three bits, one excitation shared by all three, puts its weight at heights 0 and 2 and none at 1 or 3, and fails. ([the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its third section; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its eighth.)

The look is blind to the second move, under one condition on the letters. The self-encounter sums the paths from every word of the state to every word of the state, a path being a run of the dynamics' steps from word to word, each path weighted by the amounts of the two words at its ends. A step taken backwards is always minus the step taken forwards. Whenever every term of the field and of the coupling carries an even number of Y letters, every step joins two words of which exactly one has an odd count of Y letters, so flipping the sign of every Y letter, which is the complex conjugate and the second move of the mirror, reverses every step; and the look at oneself, which walks every path in both directions, is unchanged by the reversal. So a state and its conjugate meet themselves alike under any field and coupling with an even Y count per term, the field along the watching and the bonds of this page among them. A term with an odd count of Y letters, the field along Y among them, joins words of the same Y parity, and flipping the Y signs does not reverse its steps. With such a field the conjugate pair parts, for a state in general, once bits are coupled by the bond of this page. One bit alone under that field still keeps the pair: the field carries X into Z and back and leaves the letter Y at rest, so the sign of Y enters no path. ([the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its fourth section, every pair of words; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its ninth, is the second route.)

Now let the bits act on each other, coupled as a chain. A coupling keeps the mirror when every one of its terms carries an even number of Y letters and an even number of Z letters: the first count lets the second move reverse the coupling's steps, and the two counts together let the first move carry the coupling onto itself ([The One Square](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §9 reads both off the letters). Take a state that the first move returns up to an overall sign. Its words are then paired across the middle height with equal weight, word for word, and its mirror counterpart is the state itself with the sign of every Y letter flipped, up to the sign: its complex conjugate, which the look cannot see. Call these the mirror's conjugate states. By the identity of the section on the mirror they meet themselves the same way forwards and, paying the price, backwards under every coupling that keeps the mirror. Those among them that the second move also returns, the states with no word of odd Y count, are their own mirror counterpart up to the sign: the pair, the cat, and every unlinked product of bits that carry the letters I and X in equal amounts and nothing else, the two points of the equator the first move returns; and far more states besides. The completely mixed state is not a conjugate state: its only word, I at every bit, goes under the first move to X at every bit, which it does not carry, and it does not keep the property. A second family keeps the property too: the states whose words the first move carries onto their own words with I and Z exchanged and X and Y exchanged at every bit. That exchange keeps every height, and under a coupling that carries it onto itself, every coupling of this page without a field, it is a copy, a move the dynamics do not notice, which the look cannot see; the second move only flips the Y signs, which the look cannot see either. So these states, too, meet themselves the same way both ways. ([the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its fifth section, word for word; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its eighth and ninth sections, is the second route.)

Take the state with every bit at the same one of those two points and turn all its bits together to another point of the equator. The turn keeps the height of every word and mixes the two letters across the watching, so it carries the mirror to a turned mirror and the conjugate states to the states that play their part for the turned mirror. A coupling that keeps the mirror and that the turn carries to itself, one that treats all directions on the equator alike, keeps the turned mirror, and the state keeps the property under it; a coupling of X letters alone is not carried to itself, and at the two angles tried the state loses it. For other states the whole set is not known: one draw each of unlinked bits at random points of the equator, for two, three and four bits, lost the property under both couplings tried. ([the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its sixth section; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its eighth.)

Give one bit a field along the watching, the field of the section on what you cannot see in yourself. It carries X into Y and back, within height 1, and leaves I and Z at height 0 at rest. A mirror would have to pair a word at rest with a moving one, and no pairing of the ways of fading can match a way that moves no word with one that does: this bit lies outside the class of systems the mirror section speaks of. That alone does not decide the question, whose first form can be asked without a mirror; the middle about which the heights would have to pair is fixed by the sum of the rates, mirror or not. What decides it for one bit is that every state has weight on I, at height 0, and height 1 holds only moving words, so the weights cannot pair. No state meets itself the same way forwards and, paying the price, backwards. Two bits coupled by the bond of this page, under the same field at equal strength on both, do have states that keep it: the two states that share one excitation between the bits, with either sign between the two ways of sharing it; the field moves their words onto words they do not carry, two onto each with opposite signs, so that its moves cancel; its moves on the bond's own words cancel the same way, so the cancellation holds along the whole run, and the bond alone already kept the two states. ([the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its seventh section; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its seventh and ninth.)

Give one bit a field across the watching instead, along the first of the two points. It carries Y into Z and back, between height 1 and height 0, and leaves I and X at rest; the first move pairs I with X, both at rest, and Y with Z, both moving, and the field has no Y and no Z letter, so the mirror holds. Since the mirror pairs the words so, the moving words must balance each other and the words at rest must balance each other; I is half, so X is half, which fills the state and leaves nothing for Y and Z. Of all states, pure or mixed, exactly the two pointing along the field's axis remain. A field along another point of the equator turns the mirror with it, and the two states turn with the field. With several bits coupled, a field along the first point at every bit has no Y and no Z letter, so it keeps the mirror whenever the coupling does, and the mirror's conjugate states keep the property under such a coupling; turned to another point, the field turns the mirror, and the states that keep the property with it, under a coupling that keeps the mirror and treats all directions on the equator alike; the cat state, a conjugate state of the first mirror, is among those of the turned field only when the field's angle is a multiple of a half turn divided by the number of bits, and loses the property at the angle tried. Of the other states, and of a field along the watching beyond the two states named, the whole set is not known. ([the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its eighth section, the pairs of words; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its seventh and ninth, the balance.)

## What is not known

Whether there is a mountain at all, or only the two and what happens between them. Whether behind all of a thing's relations something of it is left that no relation reaches, or whether a thing is nothing but its relations: nothing above decides it. What it shows is less: a look at oneself that is blind in two ways, each under its stated condition, and one account that, under stated conditions, a preparation and a readout share. That the relations are all there is to describe is the reading this page began with.
