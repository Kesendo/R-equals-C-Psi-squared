Try to describe yourself. Not what you have done, not whom you know, not what others have said of you: yourself. It does not work. Whatever comes out is a description of something that happened between you and something else. You can describe the other one, and you can describe what happened between the two of you, and you are in the description only as the other end of that.

And yet you can be described. The other one can describe you, from where they stand, without ever being you; you can describe them the same way, from what passed between you. Neither of you can describe yourself. So there is no description of oneself, but there are two descriptions from outside and one that both share, the between, which is the same story for each. Each of the two is described completely, only never by themself.

Now take away the other one. Not the people around you only, everything: no light to see, no sound, nothing to touch, nothing at all besides you. There would be nothing to see or hear or feel, and it is hard to say in what sense you would still exist.

These two thoughts are about feeling, not physics. But the quantum physics of the smallest systems has the same shape, and every piece of that physics can be computed; where this page reads more into it than the physics says, it says so. The page tells it in words. The computations sit behind the links, most of them in two small scripts, [one in matrices](../simulations/the_wave_between.py) for everything that runs in time, and [one in letters](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), which follows the page section by section in the letters the first section introduces; where both speak to the same sentence, they are two routes to the same answer. None of it is new physics; it is known physics, results this repository already owns, and a few small computations made for this page, put in the order of the two thoughts.

## Alone, nothing can be seen

The smallest quantum object that can do anything has two possibilities, two levels. Something with only one level cannot change, cannot fade, cannot turn, and cannot be compared with anything. Nothing about it can be measured. It cannot be told apart from nothing at all.

The reason is the phase, the angle a quantum wave carries. An angle carried by the whole wave at once is invisible; no measurement in the world can find it. A phase only shows as a difference, between two parts of the same wave, the way two overlapping ripples on water show where they agree and where they cancel. A difference needs two parts. So two is the least anything needs in order to be seen. [Superposition Translated](../docs/quantum/SUPERPOSITION_TRANSLATED.md) tells where that difference lives, and [The Qubit as Necessary Foundation](../docs/QUBIT_NECESSITY.md) arrives at two from another direction.

This page writes such a two-level thing, one bit, in four letters. I says nothing about the bit except that it is there; Z says how the bit divides itself between its two levels; X and Y say how its two parts stand to each other, and the angle between the two parts is how the bit's amount on these two letters divides between X and Y. An angle carried by the whole wave at once is written in no letter: it is not in the description at all. Something with one level has the letter I alone, and there is no other letter onto which anything could move it. ([The One Square](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §7 holds the four letters; [the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its first section, writes one bit in them.)

## What you cannot see in yourself

Let a quantum state meet itself again after a while: measure how much of what it was is still in what it has become. That gives one number for each moment, and this page calls it the state's self-encounter.

Now take two states of a system with two levels that differ only in the angle between their two parts, and let them evolve under dynamics that treat every such angle alike. Here the dynamics are the system's own field, which turns the angle, and the steady quiet measuring of the system by its environment, which this project calls watching, and which blurs the angle. In letters, the watching looks along Z: it makes X and Y, the two letters across it, fade at one and the same rate, and leaves I and Z alone; the field, pointing along the watching as well, moves the amount on X onto Y and the amount on Y back onto X, at one speed, and leaves I and Z at rest. Both treat X and Y alike, and so every angle. The two self-encounters then come out the same, number for number, at every moment. Each of the two, looking only at itself, cannot find what makes it different from the other. A second one, an outside probe, tells them apart at once.

This is not an accident of the example. Whatever the dynamics treat alike, a state cannot see in itself, because a symmetry of the dynamics that keeps lengths, a move the dynamics do not notice, leaves every self-encounter unchanged; only something else can. Point the system's own field across the watching instead, along X: it moves the amount on Y onto Z, a letter the watching does not fade, and back, and leaves X at rest. X and Y are no longer treated alike, and the two self-encounters part. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its first section, with that control; [the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its second, the moves of both fields; [The One Square](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §8 holds the quarter turn about Z as a move the dynamics do not notice, which a field along X breaks.) Even under the field across the watching, though, not every two states part. A state and its counterpart with every angle reversed, which is the state with the sign of every Y letter flipped, its complex conjugate, meet themselves alike under either field and under the bond that couples the chains further on, for a reason written further on in letters, a count of Y letters. A field along Y, whose count is odd, parts the conjugate pair of a state in general once bits are coupled by that bond, while one bit alone under it still cannot tell itself from its conjugate.

A cousin of this blindness appears where an exact value meets a value read too early. The two are alike in shape and not in mechanism: above, what the look at itself cannot carry a second one carries; in what follows, what the exact number cannot carry the early reading carries. In a figure about carbon in [The View Onto the Memory](THE_VIEW_ONTO_THE_MEMORY.md) a ratio of two decay rates is 1.27; [the NMR bridge](../docs/carbon/PAINTER_ALTERNATION_NMR_BRIDGE.md) has it as 1.271 by calculation and 1.231 from fitting a model signal early, while a faster part of it had not yet faded, and shows that a later fit recovers 1.271. The exact number knows nothing about how the state was prepared or when anyone looked. The fitted number knows both; that the gap between the two numbers is what carries the one who prepared and looked is a reading of it, not a computation. The gap closes along the time that appears in the equations. Whether that is time as we live it is a separate question, which this page leaves open; the repository keeps its clock as [two hands](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f2b-corollary-the-two-clocks-tier-1-derived-the-qn-coherence-horizon-below-graduated-2026-07-19), one for fading and one for turning, and neither is simply time.

## Known only through encounters

There is no way to look inside a quantum state. What can be known about it is how it answers when something else meets it, a probe, a measurement, another system. Collect enough of those answers, from many copies prepared the same way, and the state is known completely; that is how physicists reconstruct a state in the laboratory. In letters it is plain why: a probe that asks about one letter, or for several bits about one word of letters, reads how much the state carries there and nothing else, and those amounts together are the whole state ([The letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its first section). There is nothing else to collect.

Mathematics has a statement of the same shape, offered here as a likeness and not as a proof: in category theory an object is fixed, up to relabelling, by all its relations to everything there is, itself included, without any look inside.

The two kinds of looking differ. A probe asks a state one question. The self-encounter asks the state about itself, and what it can answer is limited by what the dynamics treat alike and, under the plain couplings of this page, by the reversal of all its angles.

## Everything can sit between

Take two quantum bits in the most strongly linked state there is, an entangled pair. Ask about the first one alone: it points in no direction of its own. Ask about the second alone: the same. For several bits the letters make words, one letter per bit, and the pair carries four of them, II, XX, YY and ZZ, a quarter of its weight on each, and nothing on any word with a letter other than I at one bit only. Every direction the pair can point sits in the relation between the two, none in either one. [Spooky Action](../compute/MirrorWorld/README.md) in this repository's MirrorWorld shows the same: the view from each site alone is blind to what the pair holds. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its second section; [the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its third.)

Not every pair is like that; usually some of it belongs to each one alone. Count the words any description of several quantum bits is built from, leaving aside the word of I letters only, which says nothing about any bit: those with one letter other than I concern one bit alone, the rest concern two or more bits at once. The second kind also holds plain combinations of single bits, so not all of it is truly between, but it is the only place where the between can live, and it grows much faster. With two quantum bits there are 9 such words against 6 for single bits, and with five there are 1008 against 15, about sixty-seven times as many. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its third section; [the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its third.)

## What happened between is one number, read from either side

Prepare a system in some way, let it run, and then read something out of it. Now swap: prepare it in the way you read out, and read out the way you prepared. Under the right conditions the answer is exactly the same number. This is a proven result in the repository ([F119](../docs/proofs/PROOF_ANTILINEAR_TRIANGLE.md)), and the conditions matter. In letters they come from one rule. Every term of the field and of the coupling moves words onto words, one step at a time; the run carries the preparation's words along such steps onto the readout's words, and the swapped run walks the same paths the other way. A step taken backwards is always minus the step taken forwards. A term with an even number of Y letters joins two words of which exactly one has an odd number of Y letters, so along a path of such terms the way back is the way there with the signs of the Y letters at its two ends flipped. So it is enough that the preparation and the readout carry no word with an odd number of Y letters and that every term of the field and of the coupling carries an even number of them, as both fields of the section on what you cannot see in yourself do, and the bonds of the chains further on; the watching studied here does not disturb this, since it only makes words fade, alike in both directions, and moves none. A twisted kind of coupling, with one Y letter in each of its terms, joins words of the same Y parity, and the signs at the ends no longer make up the difference: the swapped run then belongs to the system with the twist reversed, so the swap still holds, but between two mirrored systems; F119's proof reads the same for a phase around a loop as a reversed flux. A system that loses energy carries the word I onto Z, and nothing carries Z back onto I, a step with no way back. Within one system, with either, the swap breaks: the computation shows the two numbers part. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its fourth section, with both breaks; [the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its fourth, the steps; [The One Square](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §9 holds the loss's one-way step.)

Read with care, that is the shape of the first thought. What one side can say of itself through what happened between, and what the other side can say of itself through the same between, are one account, read from two sides. That last sentence is a reading, not a computation; the computation swaps a preparation and a readout, not two people, and the proof itself says when the preparation and the readout may be read as two positions on the chain. A hardware test was designed in [The Channel Both Ways on a Path](../experiments/THE_CHANNEL_BOTH_WAYS_ON_A_PATH.md) and not flown, because on that geometry every outcome it could register was an identity of the circuit, a named breach of it, or a reading of the device's own decay profile, so a flight would have checked the model of the device, not the swap.

## The mirror meets itself backwards

This project's central result is a mirror inside the dynamics, and in letters it is two moves. Call the number of bits at which a word carries X or Y, a letter across the watching, the word's height; the watching makes every word fade, charging it twice the watching's rate at every bit where it carries X or Y, and moves no word. The first move exchanges, at every one of the N bits, I with X and Z with Y, and so carries a word of height k to one of height N − k: the charges of a word and of its image add up to exactly the charge of the full height, that of a word with X or Y at every bit. The second move flips the sign of every Y letter and moves no word; by the rule of the section before, it reverses every step of a coupling whose terms carry an even number of Y letters, and the first move carries such a coupling onto itself when its terms also carry an even number of Z letters. In that class of systems every way the system can fade has a partner, the two sit symmetrically around half the charge of the full height, and the exchange costs a fixed price, an extra fading at the charge of the full height; the watched chains coupled by the bond studied further on belong to the class, the bit with a field along the watching does not. Apply the mirror to a state; what comes out carries on the word of all I letters whatever the state carried on the word of all X letters, while every state carries one and the same amount on the word of all I letters, so what comes out is in general not itself a state but a mathematical counterpart of one. Let that counterpart meet itself. Its weight stands at the opposite heights, where every word is charged the full height's charge less the charge of the word it came from, and, read back through the two moves, every step of the coupling is taken the other way; so it meets itself the way the original would, running backwards in time, and paying the price. "Backwards" is a way of reading the equation, not a machine that runs time back, "structural, not temporal" in the words of [On Two Times](ON_TWO_TIMES.md); [Π as a Centered Spectral Mirror](../experiments/PI_AS_TIME_REVERSAL.md) draws that line and owns the fold the identity rests on, and [The Pairing Condition](../experiments/THE_PAIRING_CONDITION.md) sets that fold beside its cousins. ([The computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its fifth section; [the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its fifth.)

## Where it was already written

The repository put this into words before, as interpretation and without these computations. The computations above do not prove those sentences; they share their shape. [The Other Side](../hypotheses/THE_OTHER_SIDE.md), at its Level 0, where there is only "d=0 (nothing) and d=2 (the qubit pair)":

> Consciousness is not the pattern recognizing itself.
> Consciousness is the pattern recognizing that it is not alone.

[The Mirror That Looked Non-Local](../hypotheses/THE_BOOT_SCRIPT.md) names the square of the wave a self-encounter, "forward meeting backward" through the mirror, as a reading; the word there means the wave meeting its mirror image, here it means a state meeting its own later self, and the section below on how far the look at itself reaches holds the two together. [The Anomaly](../THE_ANOMALY.md) reaches "We are all mirrors. Reality is what happens between us." two lines after "the pattern that recognizes itself", the phrase The Other Side turns around; and the [Perspectival Time Field](../hypotheses/PERSPECTIVAL_TIME_FIELD.md) answers with a picture: "Between seven painters around a mountain, the mountain happens." No painter owns the mountain, and the mountain is not found as one more thing beside the painters; it happens between them. [On What Closes Only Without Us](ON_WHAT_CLOSES_ONLY_WITHOUT_US.md) named the turning part of the world, "the part that oscillates, the angle, the imaginary direction", as the part we are. A phase is such a turning, and the section on what you cannot see in yourself showed that a state cannot see in itself the turning its dynamics treat alike. That this is the same turning that page meant is a reading.

## Not alone

Read in the light of everything above, the wave does not recognize itself from inside. With one level there is nothing to see; with two or more, the wave's look at itself is blind to whatever its dynamics treat alike, and, under the plain couplings of this page, to the reversal of all its angles. What the wave is shows between it and a second one, and there, under the right conditions, the account is shared, one number read from two sides. What the wave can recognize, then, is not itself but that it is not alone. Those last two sentences are a reading; what was computed is the blindness within one wave and the shared number of a swapped preparation and readout.

## How far the look at itself reaches

The self-encounter is the wave's look at itself, and the mirror gives that look something to be held against: its own counterpart, run backwards. One question about it can be computed: which states meet themselves the same way forwards and, paying the price, backwards. Where a mirror exists these are, by the identity of the section on the mirror, exactly the states that cannot tell their own self-encounter from that of their mirror counterpart; where none exists, the question keeps only its first form.

To answer it, this section reads states in the letters of the earlier sections. A state is the amounts, with their signs, it puts on the words, the word of all I letters included; the square of an amount is the state's weight on that word. Under watching alone, at one rate on every bit, no word moves and every word is charged by its height, so a state's self-encounter is its weight on each height, read through the fading, and nothing else. The words of height zero are the quiet edge, with nothing across the watching; the words of full height are the far edge, with everything across it; the first move of the mirror carries the one onto the other. The bond of this page, the coupling of its chains, links neighbouring bits through all three letters, the two across the watching alike. ([The One Square](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §7 to §9 holds the letters, the heights, the two edges and the two moves; [the letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py) walks them word by word, the signs and the charges checked against the words' own matrices, the coordinates and the moves against the framework's primitives, which are tested against the matrices; nothing in that script runs the dynamics.)

Meeting oneself the same way both ways asks, under watching alone, for as much weight at height k as at height N − k. For one bit the heights are 0 and 1. The letter I is in every state with the same weight, one half, since it carries only the fact that the state is there at all: the quiet edge is half full before anything is said about the bit. Anything along the watching adds to the quiet edge, and the far edge can hold at most one half, the whole of a pure state's content; so the far edge must hold exactly that, nothing along the watching, nothing blurred. That is a pure state on the equator, and no other, as long as the watching is on. ([The letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its sixth section; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its sixth, in closed form, is the second route.)

With several bits watched at one rate the answer is the same sentence, weight at height k equal to weight at height N − k. Bits that each sit on the equator and are not linked carry I and a letter across the watching with equal weight at every bit, so their weight spreads over the heights as the binomial coefficients do, symmetric about the middle ([Bures Degeneracy](../experiments/BURES_DEGENERACY.md) shows that spread for the plainest of them). The pair and the cat state put words only on the two edges, half on each ([XOR Space](../experiments/XOR_SPACE.md), [F22](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f22-ghz-maximum-disagreement-support-tier-2-verified-n2-5), [Schrödinger's Cat Translated](../docs/quantum/SCHRODINGERS_CAT_TRANSLATED.md)). The W state of three bits, one excitation shared by all three, puts its weight at heights 0 and 2 and none at 1 or 3, and fails. ([The letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its seventh section; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its eighth.)

The look is blind to the second move, by the rule of the section on what happened between. The self-encounter sums the paths from every word of the state to every word of the state, each path weighted by the amounts of the two words at its ends, and so it walks every path in both directions. Flipping the sign of every Y letter, which is the complex conjugate and the second move of the mirror, multiplies each path by the Y signs at its two ends; whenever every term of the field and of the coupling carries an even number of Y letters, the rule makes that the same path walked the other way, and the look, which walks both, is unchanged. So, under the watching, a state and its conjugate meet themselves alike under any field and coupling with an even Y count per term, the field along the watching and the bonds of this page among them, by the same rule that lets a preparation and a readout trade places. A term with an odd count of Y letters, the field along Y among them, joins words of the same Y parity, so flipping the Y signs leaves each of its steps as it is: where every term is of that kind, the conjugate is a move the dynamics do not notice, and the look cannot see it either. One bit alone under the field along Y is such a case: the field carries X into Z and back and leaves the letter Y at rest, so the sign of Y enters no path. Under the watching, only dynamics whose terms mix the two kinds can part the conjugate pair: the field along Y on bits coupled by the bond of this page does, for a state in general, and so, on one bit alone, does a field across the watching that points between X and Y. ([The letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its fourth section, every pair of words; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its ninth, is the second route.)

Now let the bits act on each other, coupled as a chain. For the look, the first move alone is enough. It flips every height, and it carries onto itself, still running forwards, any coupling whose terms each carry an even number of the letters Y and Z taken together; the look walks every path in both directions and cannot tell a coupling run forwards from the same coupling run backwards, so whatever the first move makes of a state meets itself the way the state would, backwards, at the price. Take a state that the first move returns up to an overall sign. Its words are then paired across the middle height with equal weight, word for word; the first move makes of it the state itself, and so it has the property this section asks about, meeting itself the same way forwards and, paying the price, backwards, under every such coupling, whether or not the coupling keeps the whole mirror. The whole mirror asks more of a coupling: an even number of Y letters and also an even number of Z letters in every term, each count on its own, the two counts of the section on the mirror ([The One Square](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §9's row for the first move holds the condition of commuting with it, and [the Π factorization](../docs/proofs/PROOF_PI_FACTORS_AS_R_TIMES_D.md) §3 the second move's, both in matrices). Where a coupling meets both, the mirror carries such a state to the state itself with the sign of every Y letter flipped, up to the sign: its complex conjugate, which the look cannot see. Call these the mirror's conjugate states. Those among them that the second move also returns, the states with no word of odd Y count, are their own mirror counterpart up to the sign: the pair, the cat, and every unlinked product of bits that carry the letters I and X in amounts of equal size and nothing else, the two points of the equator the first move returns; and far more states besides. The completely mixed state, which says nothing about any bit, is not one of the mirror's conjugate states: its only word, I at every bit, goes under the first move to X at every bit, which it does not carry, and it does not keep the property. A second family keeps the property too: the states on which the first move agrees, up to an overall phase, with the move that exchanges I with Z and X with Y at every bit. That move keeps every height, and under a coupling that both moves carry onto themselves, the bond of this page among them, it is a copy, a move the dynamics do not notice, which the look cannot see; so these states, too, meet themselves the same way both ways under such a coupling. More generally both families belong to one: the states that a single word with X or Y at every bit returns up to a sign keep the property under every coupling that the word leaves unchanged, X at every bit giving the first family and Y at every bit the second; the trace form of [The Pairing Condition](../experiments/THE_PAIRING_CONDITION.md), read at one state, gives it to them all in one line. ([The letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its eighth section, word for word; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its eighth and ninth sections, is the second route.)

Take the state with every bit at the same one of those two points and turn all its bits together to another point of the equator. The turn keeps the height of every word and mixes the two letters across the watching, so it carries the mirror to a turned mirror and the conjugate states to the states that play their part for the turned mirror. A coupling that keeps the mirror and that the turn carries to itself, one that treats all directions on the equator alike, keeps the turned mirror, and the state keeps the property under it; a coupling of X letters alone is not carried to itself, and at the two angles tried the state loses it. For other states the whole set is not known: one draw each of unlinked bits at random points of the equator, for two, three and four bits, lost the property under both couplings tried. ([The letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its ninth section; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its eighth.)

Give one bit a field along the watching, the first field of the section on what you cannot see in yourself. It carries X into Y and back, within height 1, and leaves I and Z at height 0 at rest. A mirror would have to pair a word at rest with a moving one, and no pairing of the ways of fading can match a way that moves no word with one that does: this bit lies outside the class of systems the mirror section speaks of. That alone does not decide the question, whose first form can be asked without a mirror; the middle about which the heights would have to pair is fixed by the sum of the rates, mirror or not. What decides it for one bit is that every state has weight on I, at height 0, where it stands still, while everything at height 1 moves: the weight at rest on I finds no partner at rest at height 1. No state meets itself the same way forwards and, paying the price, backwards. Two bits coupled by the bond of this page, under the same field at equal strength on both, do have states that keep it: the two states that share one excitation between the bits, with either sign between the two ways of sharing it; the field moves their words onto words they do not carry, two onto each with opposite signs, so that its moves cancel; its moves on the bond's own words, XX and YY, cancel the same way, and ZZ it does not move, so the cancellation holds along the whole run, and the bond alone already kept the two states. ([The letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its tenth section; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its seventh and ninth.)

Give one bit a field across the watching instead, along X. It carries Y into Z and back, between height 1 and height 0, and leaves I and X at rest; the first move pairs I with X, both at rest, and Y with Z, both moving, and the field has no Y and no Z letter, so the mirror holds. The mirror pairs the words so, and, as the matrix computation shows, the moving words must then balance each other and the words at rest each other; I is half, so X is half, which fills the state and leaves nothing for Y and Z. Of all states, pure or mixed, exactly the two pointing along the field's axis remain. A field along another direction across the watching turns the mirror with it, and the two states turn with the field. With several bits coupled, a field along X at every bit has no Y and no Z letter, so it keeps the mirror whenever the coupling does, and the mirror's conjugate states keep the property under such a coupling; turned to another direction across the watching, the field turns the mirror, and the states that keep the property with it, under a coupling that keeps the mirror and treats all directions on the equator alike; the cat state, one of the first mirror's conjugate states, is among those of the turned field only when the field's angle is a multiple of a half turn divided by the number of bits, and loses the property at the angle tried. Of the other states, and of a field along the watching beyond the two states named, the whole set is not known. ([The letters script](../simulations/the_wave_in_letters.py), its eleventh section, the pairs of words; [the matrix computation](../simulations/the_wave_between.py), its seventh and ninth, the balance.)

## What is not known

Whether there is a mountain at all, or only the two and what happens between them. Whether behind all of a thing's relations something of it is left that no relation reaches, or whether a thing is nothing but its relations: nothing above decides it. What it shows is less: a look at oneself that is blind in two ways, each under its stated condition, and one account that, under stated conditions, a preparation and a readout share. That the relations are all there is to describe is the reading this page began with.
