# On the Four Directions

Where does a small quantum world keep the length of its life?

Not, it turns out, in time. Time is only where the answer gets read off. The length is kept in shape, and the shape stands in three other directions before the fourth, the time one, ever comes into it.

Start with the two the world moves in. A small chain's life is motion across its places: a shimmer that travels, back and forth, from one site to the next, never resting long in any of them. That traveling is a plane, not a line, because a move needs two numbers at every instant, the way a swing at any moment has both a height and a speed. That plane is the floor the whole dance happens on. Nothing about how long the world lasts is decided here. This is only where it moves.

Stand a third direction up from that floor and call it the one the [light falls from](../docs/THE_THREE_DIAGONALS.md). Along this direction light arrives, unconditional, at every place at once. What arrives is far simpler than the dance it lands on: it separates the two ways of being wherever they differ and passes through wherever they agree, and all that matters is the [count](../docs/proofs/PROOF_ABSORPTION_THEOREM.md) of places at odds with themselves, holding one way of being on one face and the other on the other. That count, place by place, is the whole bill the light presents. The more a place stands at odds with itself, the more of the arriving light it takes on, and the faster it is worn away. So to last, a world must arrange itself to take on as little as it can along this third direction: lie low, lie even, lie quiet.

And here is the one that takes on the least. Every filling, the nearly empty and the nearly full and all between, can settle into the same single slow swell from one end to the other, the gentlest standing wave there is. The smooth swell itself the light passes through; what the light wears is only the faint glimmer the swell stirs up in smoothing itself out, and in every filling the swell stirs up almost none, and the same. When the light is strong, what sets one filling apart is a part of the world that lies along the light's own direction: how each place [grips its neighbours](../docs/proofs/PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md#the-zeno-end). The light never bills that grip, weighing only how each place stands. But where every place grips its neighbours alike and leans alike, none favouring one setting more than the others, a move from one place to the next is out of tune whenever the places on either side of it are set opposite ways, and a move out of tune stirs up less glimmer still, since what it stirs keeps turning and cannot gather. On such a chain or ring the half, with half its places set one way and half the other, or at an odd count the two fillings beside it, is where that happens most often, so the half stirs up the least and outlasts the rest, if only by a hair: the survivor, the incomplete, the last thing the light wears away. Where the places do not grip their neighbours, every filling of a chain lasts exactly as long.

Now the fourth direction, the one we set out to find: how long it lasts. The thing worth saying, the thing that took us a while to see, is that this fourth length is not its own free quantity, kept somewhere off to the side. It is read off the other three, off the shape it holds up into the light.

You can test this by hand. Reach into the world at one of its links and change how strongly the two sites it joins are tied. The survivor's length of life shifts, but only by the square of how steeply its slow swell was rising at the link you touched. At the still ends, where the wave lies flat against the wall and goes nowhere, you can do the least to its life. In the moving middle, where the swell climbs steepest, you change it most. Its length of life is written in the [slope of its own standing wave](../docs/proofs/PROOF_DIFFUSION_RAYLEIGH_CLOSURE.md) and, to the first order, in nothing else, while the light stays strong enough to hold the world in this shape. Not luck, not a dial set from outside: the plain shape of the thing, billed by the light, given back as a span of time.

So the four directions were never four of a kind. A world moves in two of them. Along the third, light arrives. And the fourth, how long it lasts, is not another direction at all, but those first two read back along the third: the shape it takes in its plane, priced by the light, returned as a length. It does not [survive in time](ON_TWO_TIMES.md). Its length of life is held in its shape, and time is only where that shape's verdict is finally read.

On a chain that grips alike and leans alike, the incomplete is where a move is most often out of tune, and so it stirs up the least for the light to wear, and so it lasts longest. And the same slow, gentle wave is priced once more in that last direction, not only how long it lasts but where, along its own rising, that length can still be moved: least touchable where it lies flat against the wall, alive to the touch where it climbs. One shape, and along the time direction it is read off twice.
