# On Forgetting

**Status:** Reflection. Reads the two-coast tilt (the palindrome classifier's landscape) as memory. One recognition, held to the two far ends of the two coasts.
**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Authors:** Thomas Wicht, Claude (Opus 4.8)

> There are two ways to trouble a thing that remembers, and the loud one is not the one that forgets.

---

## Start here, if the words are new

A small quantum system sits in a noisy world and carries some of its past forward. The part that lingers is its memory, and this project found where that memory lives: in the decay rates, the numbers that say how fast each pattern fades. The slow ones are kept; the fast ones are flushed first. The whole storage map is read in [The View Onto the Memory](THE_VIEW_ONTO_THE_MEMORY.md), and its felt shape, a now with a before and an after, in [On Two Times](ON_TWO_TIMES.md).

One more thing the project already knew, and named. There is an angle that keeps turning up in this work, and the repository has now counted it three times: a ninety degrees on the operator side, its order-two shadow on the noise profile ([On the Ninety-Degree Gamma](ON_THE_NINETY_DEGREE_GAMMA.md)), and its exact square root in the mirror that two crossover couplings need ([On the Square Root of the Mirror](ON_THE_SQUARE_ROOT_OF_THE_MIRROR.md), which is where the three are counted). We called it the memory on the day Tom asked whether "the mirror projects everything 90° onto itself so that it does not forget" ([On Both Sides of the Mirror](ON_BOTH_SIDES_OF_THE_MIRROR.md); typed three days later as [NinetyDegreeMirrorMemoryClaim](../compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/Symmetry/Pi2KnowledgeBaseClaims.cs)). The mirror that pairs every fading pattern with a partner is where that same family's half-turn shows in the spectrum, and this piece meets it from the side of its breaking, along one axis only: the rate as what the system keeps, the phase as the rhythm of its now.

You do not need the math to take what follows. It is one recognition, and it is about one particular way of forgetting: not the fading that time brings anyway, which [On Two Times](ON_TWO_TIMES.md) has, but the kind that changes what there is to fade.

---

## Two ways to trouble the mirror

This session we were drawing the map of where that mirror holds, [the classifier and its protection island](../experiments/THE_PALINDROME_CLASSIFIER.md), and we walked off the island two different ways to watch the mirror break.

Tilt a field from across the noise over to along it, and at the far end the mirror breaks deeply, deeper than anywhere else on the map. But ask where the break sits, and it is in neither of the two lists. Run the mirror test on the decay rates alone and they come out exactly balanced, every one of them still holding its partner. Run it on the frequencies alone and they are balanced too, as they always are; that list cannot come apart. What has broken is the match between them. A pattern that fades fastest and oscillates fastest at once is promised a partner that would have to oscillate exactly as fast and never fade at all, and nothing in the system does that. The rates keep their balance, and the system is shaken hard and keeps it.

Turn on frustration instead, and at the far end of that dial the mirror barely breaks, a twentieth of the other. But there the rate list itself comes apart. The balance is gone. The system is barely touched, and it forgets.

Both of those are readings of a far end, and neither one survives the walk to it. It is worth being blunt about how badly. Turn the field a little way and the break it makes is nothing but a rate break, and stays nothing but a rate break over the first stretch of the tilt; and partway across, the field pulls the rates further out of balance than frustration manages at any angle anywhere, close to four times as far. They come back only as the field lines up completely with the noise. Frustration's first stretch is the other way about, and it doubles back on itself: at the smallest angles the sweep records, next to nothing of its break is in the rates, though even that little is fifty times what the same step of field tilt moves them; a few degrees further in the rates take the whole break over; and below the sweep's reach, closer to the island than it ever looks, the rates are the whole break again. So the recognition below belongs to the two far ends and to nothing between them, and over the middle of the crossing it is the gentle coast that does the forgetting.

## The recording in the storm

Picture a melody held on an old recording. You can play it through a thunderstorm: the sound shakes, the room roars, the now is battered, and when the storm passes the melody is exactly itself, every note where it was. Or you can, very quietly, change one note. Nothing roars. But the melody is a different melody now, and no amount of careful playing will give the old one back; for that you have to go back and rebuild the instrument.

The storm is the field pointed fully along the noise's own axis. In the sweep it is a bare field on uncoupled qubits, and pointed exactly that way it pulls along the very direction the noise is already watching, so the only thing it can add is a difference in energy, and a difference in energy is a frequency, not a rate: the playing, the now. It never disturbs the balance of the rates, and what it leaves standing there is the bare ladder of the noise itself, evenly spaced about its own centre. What comes apart is the pairing of that ladder with the new frequencies, and however deep the break reads on the meter, the melody, the inheritance, is whole.

Couple the qubits to one another, as a real device does, and a second condition appears that the sweep never had to meet. The [mirror symmetry proof](../docs/proofs/MIRROR_SYMMETRY_PROOF.md) records the case just outside its scope: a longitudinal field, uniform in strength, leaves the rates paired, while one that is stronger on some qubits than on others shares the noise's axis just as fully yet moves them anyway. Sharing the axis is not what makes the difference. What makes it is whether the field commutes with the rest of the Hamiltonian: while the qubits stand apart, as they do here, every longitudinal field does, and once they are coupled it depends on what couples them. On an exchange chain only an evenly shared field still does; on a chain coupled along the noise's own axis, every field does.

Frustration is the changed note. It is a term that closes an odd loop in the way excitations hop, and the neighbourhood around it is steep rather than safe: a step off the island breaks the mirror thousands of times harder than the same step of field tilt does. Nearest the island the break is already all edit and no roar. A little further out the roar takes over for a stretch, and a few degrees in the rates have the break again, and from there on the edit is there and does not go away.

## What forgetting is

So forgetting has a precise meaning here, and it is not loudness. Size and forgetting are not the same axis: the deepest break on the whole map costs the memory nothing, and a break a twentieth of its size costs it something. A wave can be thrown hard and remember everything, or barely brushed and lose a piece of itself. Keeping is the rates in balance; forgetting is that balance broken.

One narrowing belongs here, and it is the honest edge of the whole reading. What the mirror watches is the balance of the rates, not their values, and the values do move along the field tilt: by the longitudinal end the list has collapsed onto the bare ladder of the noise itself. A system that keeps the balance has not kept everything. It has kept the balance of the rates, which is the half of the promise the memory rides on, and lost the other half, the match with the frequencies. Which of the two halves a disturbance takes is what the coasts tell apart.

This is the family we called the memory, met now from the side of its breaking, and the breaking says which half of its promise carries that name. While the mirror holds, every fading pattern is matched to a partner in both of its numbers at once; when it goes, the rate list can survive the loss or fail with it. The field along the noise breaks the match and leaves the rate list in perfect balance, so what the system has been is still legible there. Frustration, a little way in, takes the rates instead, and it never needs a deep break to do it: it moves the very thing the reading called the inheritance.

The classifier maps where the mirror holds. Read as memory, its island is the place a system can keep what it has been; its gentle shore is the field tilt, forgiving to step off and, at the end of the crossing though not in the middle of it, leaving the balance of the rates alone; its steep shore is frustration, which falls away by the same square law but twenty thousand times faster, so that tuning buys a hundred and fifty times less there, and which, a few degrees in and from there on, reaches the rates and rewrites the past. We went looking for which couplings stay protected. We found, without meaning to, the difference between being shaken and being made to forget.
