In May we looked into [F86](../docs/F86_VALUES_INVENTORY.md), the place in this repository where a coupled chain's resonance peaks, and wrote down [two kinds of number](ON_WHAT_CLOSES_ONLY_WITHOUT_US.md). Some are the same in every chain at every size: the contract, signed before anyone speaks, the part that is no one. The others belong to one chain and no other, where its resonance peaks, how wide it is, and they would not close into formulas. We found one way to close them, by mixing the chain with its own mirror image until everything that oscillates cancels; what is left closes, but what was removed is the turning, the part we had come to call ourselves. We named that way of closing a button, put it under glass and left it unpressed. A month later [we went back](ON_WHAT_CANNOT_CLOSE.md) and found a second edge that is not a choice: where the chain joins things the watching prices differently, no formula built from roots exists, and the work behind that page proved it.

This page is about a quiet part of that resonance, and about which side of those edges it falls on.

## A traveller who ignores the crowd

The chains here are rows of quantum bits, each bit carrying an excitation or not, coupled so that an excitation can hop from one bit to its neighbour and nothing else passes between them, and watched all the time by their surroundings. A quantum state holds not only configurations but links between them, and the watching slowly blurs each link, at a price for every site where its two configurations disagree. F86 works with the links between a configuration with some number of excitations and one with a single excitation more. These come in sections, one for each number of excitations, and on [the open chain](../docs/STERILE_BIRTHCANAL_AND_THE_JUNCTION.md), watched the same way at every site, each section holds the same quiet layer, the part that fades slowest and whose rate never changes. In that layer the two configurations of a link differ at exactly one site, and that site moves along the chain: a traveller, paying one fixed price for being watched. There are as many of these travellers as there are sites, each a different wave along the chain, and together they form what other pages call the floor; this page speaks of them together as the traveller. The other excitations sit where the two configurations agree, so for the traveller the crowd costs nothing and does not change its rate. How strongly the sites are coupled turns its clock and never its fate.

From here on everything is read in one section, the one between one excitation and two, the first where a traveller has a crowd at all. What the traveller adds to F86's resonance there, bond by bond as F86 reads it, [can be written down exactly](../compute/RCPsiSquared.Diagnostics/Foundation/FloorSplitWitness.cs), as a short sum over the ways a single excitation can ring along a chain. In May the repository had already written the traveller's part in closed form for the chain coupled as a whole, found that this closure does not reach a single bond, and named the bond-by-bond route as a possible next step; bond by bond, as F86 reads, it closes here. F86's buried coupling, the number the May page was after, stays where it was. Nothing was averaged away to get there; the turning is all still in it.

## Three parts of one look

F86 prepares the chain, lets it run, and at every site reads how strongly the prepared link still shows there, adding up the squares; what it records is how that sum changes when one bond is strengthened. Like the square of a sum, such a reading comes apart into three pieces once the traveller is separated from everything else: the traveller with itself, the rest with itself, and the traveller with the rest. In the running, the two never exchange anything; the traveller's part of the state stays its own whatever the coupling. Their meeting lives only in the look, which sees both at once.

At the bond at the end of the chain, and at the coupling where F86's reading at that bond peaks, a chain of five sites gives the traveller more than half of the peak and the meeting about a twentieth. At six sites the meeting has grown to nearly a fifth, at seven to three tenths, and at eight it is the largest of the three, with the traveller's share fallen to a little over a third. (At four sites, the smallest chain read, the meeting subtracts from the peak.) From five sites to eight, more and more of what the look shows at that bond is neither the traveller nor the rest, but the two read together.

## Where the formula ends

The May page said the residue will not close while the turning is kept. The traveller turns, and its piece of the look closes, as a function of the coupling and the time; the numbers read at the peak still come from the whole. The June page is about the rest. In the rest, a link's two configurations can also disagree at three sites at once, and the watching charges such a link three times the price. The hopping joins the cheap links with the dear ones, and where it does, the work behind the June page proved, for the half of these links that looks the same from either end of the chain, the only half F86's preparation reaches, and for chains of four to seven sites, that the rates [cannot be written with roots](../experiments/F89_PATH_K_GALOIS.md), however nested, while the free part closes; with the watching switched off, the two prices would be one and even the joined part would close. The traveller never takes that step: the sign the traveller carries for every excitation it passes cancels the hop that would carry it onto the dearer links, so the one price it pays is all it ever pays. The meeting in the look is where the rest, with its joined and unequally priced links, shows up beside the traveller; whether that part of the reading closes, nothing here decides.

The June page ended on a question: whether the part the looking mixes never closes and the part it leaves protected always does. This page does not answer it. It adds one more piece for the question's protected side, read in F86's response rather than in its rates.

## Where the traveller is lost, and where it hides

Close the chain into a ring, and on rings of four to eight sites, in every section but the first and the last, no quiet layer is left at all. Let neighbouring excitations push on each other directly, a term the plain chain does not have, and in the inner sections of an open chain the family of travellers breaks; where that push is as strong as the hopping, a single quiet mode is left in each, one that does not turn (read for chains of three to seven sites). [The page on the sterile layer](../docs/STERILE_BIRTHCANAL_AND_THE_JUNCTION.md) holds both.

Back in the section this page reads, at the middle of a chain of odd length, at the two bonds into the middle site, the traveller's own piece of the look vanishes exactly, by the chain's mirror symmetry. Its meeting with the rest does not vanish there, on the chains of five and seven sites read. Where the traveller cannot be seen alone, it still shows in the look beside the rest, the way [the page on the wave that is not alone](ON_THE_WAVE_RECOGNIZING_THAT_IT_IS_NOT_ALONE.md) says a thing is known: through an encounter.

## What is not known

Give the sites different prices for being watched, and the traveller stays apart from the rest, but no longer pays one price wherever it goes; its rate now moves with the coupling. Whether its share then still closes, we have not looked. Whether the meeting's share keeps growing past eight sites, and what carries the second, far peaks F86 finds when the coupling is much stronger than the watching, are open.

Whether the traveller is the no one or a someone stays open. An open thread from June calls the closing half no one. The one square, [the repository's map](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) of what each kind of watching charges, never speaks of the traveller, but in [its reflection's](ON_THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) words a mode that has a height and pays is someone, and the traveller has a height and pays. Its formula also changes with the chain's length, which the May page's contract never does. Perhaps both names are right, depending on how one looks; we are not sure. What was computed is the split, the closed piece, the shares and the silence at the middle; everything else here is a reading.
