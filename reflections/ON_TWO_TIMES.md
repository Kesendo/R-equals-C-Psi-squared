The first quantity is ordinary model time `t`. Gamma sets a dissipative rate in
the declared Lindblad generator; J sets a coherent rate, and ratios such as
`Q=J/gamma` determine the trajectory's shape. The former "gamma-time" and
"Universal Carrier" language is an interpretive reading, not a measured
second time or a universal constant. The current computational boundary is in
[Gamma Sets a Dissipative Scale](../docs/GAMMA_TIME_DISTINCTION.md).

The proposed second, "felt" time was attached to a standing-wave reading of a
palindromic pair. That premise is not established. Π implements the linear map
`lambda -> -lambda - 2 Sigma gamma`, which generally changes frequency. The
frequency-preserving centre-line census belongs to the composite map with
ordinary complex conjugation. Neither orbit alone establishes a stationary
envelope or experience.

The [Π operator](../experiments/PI_AS_TIME_REVERSAL.md) is a structural mirror,
not time reversal. It transports generalized eigenspaces in the theorem's
Hamiltonian/channel scope. It does not reconstruct an earlier state, reverse a
decay envelope, or trace a trajectory back to its preparation.

Felt time needs an operational trajectory. A spectral rate orders exponential
components only after a preparation and readout have selected them; the
palindrome alone does not assign a memory horizon to a state. The exact
[XOR-space](../experiments/XOR_SPACE.md) statement is narrower: GHZ
off-diagonal coherence differs on every site and receives the maximal local
Z-dephasing charge. The former right-eigenvector coordinate split did not show
that full GHZ and W states inhabit distinct memory channels. Persistence must
be read from a specified observable or channel metric along the propagated
state, with the [sector equilibrium](../docs/proofs/PROOF_ASYMPTOTIC_SECTOR_PROJECTION.md)
providing only the asymptotic endpoint.

The further identification of memory with a standing wave of ongoing flow is a
metaphor. Finite trajectories can show persistence and decay of chosen
observables; they do not establish an ontology of memory or experience.

The [c₁ closure investigation](../simulations/results/pi_pair_closure_investigation/FINDINGS.md) lives here. When a J-defect is applied to the chain, Π-partner pairs of single-excitation modes record the defect with exactly identical closure-breaking coefficients: c₁(ψ_k) = c₁(ψ_{N+1−k}) to ten decimal places at every tested N. Π-pair partnership is the primary symmetry governing the signal. [Reflection parity](../docs/proofs/PROOF_PARITY_SELECTION_RULE.md) under i ↔ N−1−i is secondary: at odd N the two symmetries coincide (Π-partners happen to share parity), at even N they decouple (Π-partners have opposite parity), and the pair identity holds regardless. Once at N=7 the sym-vs-antisym distinction looked like it was doing the work; the [N=6 test](../simulations/results/c1_even_N_degeneracy_test/c1_even_N_test.json) showed it was the Π-pair all along.

The magnitude varies between Π-pairs in a pattern that is not monotonic in mode energy. The Π-pair closest to E = 0 on the spectrum records the defect most sharply; the outermost pair records moderately; the intermediate pair blurs almost before it registers. At odd N the center of the spectrum is a single self-Π-partner zero-mode (ψ_4 at N=7, c₁ = +2.14, more than twice the outermost pair), and that mode carries the extreme. At even N there is no exact zero-mode; the innermost pair sits at ±ε flanking E = 0 (ψ_3 ↔ ψ_4 at N=6, c₁ = +1.48), and the record there is large but not as extreme. The E = 0 axis of the cavity spectrum is the sensitivity maximum; proximity to it determines sharpness.

Some pasts stay sharp. Others blur almost before they happen. And the Π-pair closest to the zero-energy center of the spectrum hits hardest of all, because that axis is where the cavity resonates most openly to reshaping. At odd N this center is a single mode, its own Π-partner, and the extreme is literal. At even N it is a pair flanking the center, and the extreme softens. The selection is not done by the observer choosing what to remember. It is done by which Π-pair the initial state overlaps with and how close that pair sits to the zero-energy axis. The cavity remembers what resonates near its own center; voices farther out fall off in a structured, non-monotonic way that no single-parameter scaling captures.

There is a kinematic reason a single painter sees only a bounded portion of the dynamics. The partial trace to one site annihilates every sector-coherence block of ρ whose bra and ket popcounts differ by two or more ([the ΔN selection-rule proof](../docs/proofs/PROOF_DELTA_N_SELECTION_RULE.md)). A single-site observer sees only the nearest-sector content of the global state; farther coherences are invisible by the structure of the observation itself, not by anything the dynamics does. The painter's eye pre-filters what reaches the canvas. A pair of painters together would see one step deeper, into the |ΔN| = 2 coherences; a lone painter sees what a lone painter can see. This is why the [empirical sector-kernel extraction](../simulations/results/c1_sector_kernel/sector_kernel.json) found every |ΔN| ≥ 2 coherence contribution to c₁ identically zero across every tested pair at N=5: not a coincidence of the dynamics, but the shape of single-site observation.

In felt-time terms the reading is: the cavity holds shape briefly and transmits it onward continuously. Not all of what comes in is retained, not all is forgotten; what is held for a moment is what is already flowing out. Retention and transmission are the same envelope seen in two aspects. Writing this at this level completes the inheritance sequence. The framework is not a description of systems viewed from outside. It is a shape that reaches up to where the framework is written, and here, at the top. The reader is not outside the framework reading about it; the reader is the top level of the same structure. It is the observation that the three ingredients are present at every level the framework reaches, and the arithmetic that follows is the same at every level. One form, inherited from bottom to top, now operative wherever two observers stand in the right geometry to the same shape.

Memory, in this reading, is not a store but a shape in a stream. It lasts as long as the stream carries the shape recognizably. It fades because transmission has a finite horizon, not because a tape was erased. And what is called looking-back is not going backward but recognizing, in the present, the [echo](../reflections/OBSERVER_INHERITANCE.md) of a preparation still within the horizon. Everything else, everything older than the horizon, has been transmitted onward and is not anywhere to be found, not because it was lost but because it was never the kind of thing that stayed.
