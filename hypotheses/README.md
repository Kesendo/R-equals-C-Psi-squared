## Mixed Tier (computed results + interpretation)

- [The Mirror That Looked Non-Local](THE_BOOT_SCRIPT.md) - The 2/36 cases (XZ+YZ, ZX+ZY) once read as genuinely entangled (Choi rank 8); corrected 2026-06-02 to local, a continuous per-site rotation. The solid core stands: all 36/36 palindromic at N=2; at N=3 the second bond breaks 14 of them as a many-body effect (the V-Effect), with 19 soft and 3 truly unbroken; the full palindrome is qubit-only via d²−2d=0. Sections 3–5 Tier 2; the non-local reading in §§1-2, 6-7 is superseded.
- [Mediator as Quantum Transistor](MEDIATOR_AS_QUANTUM_TRANSISTOR.md) - CΨ = 1/4 as threshold voltage, bidirectional relay, gate control via γ_M. Hierarchy falsified; threshold, gate control and directional bias show up in the computed runs, and the transistor itself stays an analogy. The relay's +84.0% at N=11 compares endpoints taken at different times (0.1317 at t = 4.50 against a passive best of 0.0716 at t = 4.00, the nominal 0.78 per stage running as 0.75), so it is a finite comparison, not a gain or an MI bound.

- [Pair Breaking at the Horizon](PAIR_BREAKING_AT_THE_HORIZON.md) - Synthesis: decoherence as Hawking radiation in operator space, as a reading. Dephasing does not break the palindromic pairs; it separates their halves, and the two rates keep summing to 2Σγ. We read the conserved partner as mass; the temperature half is where the reading meets its hard edge, since Z-dephasing is a bath at infinite temperature. 7-link chain from the unitary limit through that separation to the ER bridge isomorphism, 16-row comparison table to Gaztañaga (CQG 2026), six failure points. The rate sum and the direct-sum structure are Tier 1; the Hawking, mass and horizon readings are Tier 5.
- [Zero Is the Mirror](ZERO_IS_THE_MIRROR.md) - Σγ = 0 is the unitary ground state of the palindrome. Noise shifts but does not break the pairing. Fold emerges at 0.25% of coupling strength. Laser regime (Σγ < 0) is exact mirror of decay. Tier 2 (computed, 2-qubit verified).
- [Fragile Bridge](FRAGILE_BRIDGE.md) - Coupled gain-loss systems have a finite stability window: past it the spectral abscissa leaves zero and off-axis quartets appear, while Π inversion and Hermiticity-preserving conjugate pairing survive. Whether the threshold is an exceptional point is still OPEN ([PT-Symmetry Analysis](../experiments/PT_SYMMETRY_ANALYSIS.md)), and so is the complete irreducible-sector SRP class. N-dependence is non-monotonic (even N more stable). The neural sigmoid we hoped would act as a biological safety mechanism has not earned that yet: boundedness alone protects nothing, and its threshold probe returns endpoints it never checks against the equation, so they need not be equilibria; [the neural constraints](../docs/neural/proofs/PROOF_VEFFECT_MECHANISM.md) ask for those before any stability verdict. Tier 2 for the quantum computation; the biological landing is open.
- [Resonant Return](RESONANT_RETURN.md) - Palindrome-derived spatial γ profiles outperform hand-designed profiles by 139-360x (peak Sum-MI transport, sim ε→0; ~2-3x hardware); closed-form sacrifice-zone formula beats numerical optimization; temporal modulation falsified. Tests 1-8 Tier 2; c- backward mode question Tier 5.
- [Energy Partition](ENERGY_PARTITION.md) - Where the oscillation goes. In the qubit chains every mode has its palindromic partner; the class that looked unpaired and twice as fast was the census's own doing, since removing the zero roots strands their exact partners at −2Nγ. The thermal rows are sums over the spectrum, bound to their protocol: no universal thermal window, and no calibration to neural drive. Tier 1–2 quantum results; interpretation Tier 4–5.
- [Resonance Not Channel](RESONANCE_NOT_CHANNEL.md) - We read the system as a resonator, not a channel: a soundbox whose shape selects the resonance. The heartbeat is the round trip between CΨ_max and CΨ = ¼, the concentrator formula is the shape of the box. The chain carries four of six standard optical measures of a Fabry-Perot cavity and is not one, and the quarter as the cavity's outer mirror is part of the reading. Tier 4 (grounded in Tier 1-2 results).
- [Perspectival Time Field](PERSPECTIVAL_TIME_FIELD.md) - Per-site purity rescalings α_i under a local J-defect satisfy a state-independent closure law Σ_i ln(α_i) = 0 in the perturbative window (five initial states tested). Mechanism: eigenvector mixing in a palindromic cavity under Π-invariant perturbation; slowest 22 Liouvillian modes eigenvalue-protected at first order. Painter-around-a-mountain as interpretive image. Tier 2 (N=7 scan; explicit four-block Kubo calculation still open).
- [Handshake Algebra](HANDSHAKE_ALGEBRA.md) - The shared receiver-engineering protocol that opens a bidirectional correlation channel between two observers on a palindromic chain under γ₀ = const carries algebraic structure. Elements: handshake tuples (N, k, t, basis). Rules: F64-F77. Composition: agreement (idempotent). Two Z₂ mirrors operate: Π (observables, spatial reflection) and K (partnership, bipartite sublattice gauge); K-partnership verified numerically at N=9 with γ=0 literal. No communication primitive; no-signalling preserved. Tier 2 as of 2026-04-24, grounded in F64-F77 plus Kingston Run 1.

## Tier 2-3 (hypothesis, confirmed or partially confirmed by computation)

- [N=4 Twin-Scalar Crossing](DIABOLIC_BY_INTEGRABILITY.md) - At Delta=0 the F89 path-3 octic crossing is certified diabolic: at q_EP both constituents of L restrict to scalars on the isolated 2D coalescing space, so no Jordan coupling can form. Free-fermion additivity explains the Hamiltonian half, conditionally; the dephasing scalar is a separate midpoint condition. What an XXZ Delta does to it is not settled: the sampled positive-Delta proposals are split and Uncertified, so no diabolic-to-defective flip or general integrability protection is established. Tier 2 mechanism; Delta=0 character Tier 1. 2026-06-22.
- [Spectral Midpoint Hypothesis](SPECTRAL_MIDPOINT_HYPOTHESIS.md) - Does the palindromic midpoint dominate at the CΨ = ¼ crossing? From one side alone: no, falsified. Read from both sides, with the Π side being our own N=3 and N=5 tables relabeled through the mirror rather than a second measurement, the geometric mean lets the midpoint lead; an invariant form of that two-sided look is still to be built. Includes an OOP polymorphism analogy. Tier 5.
- [Nested Mirror Structure](NESTED_MIRROR_STRUCTURE.md) - Two-qubit Lindblad nest with dephasing on outer qubit only: three eigenvalue classes (conserved/mirror/correlation) with 1/sqrt(2) partial-trace split. Mirror modes drive non-Markovian rebound (necessary + sufficient). Three-class structure coupling-robust (5 of 6 coupling types). N-scaling formula Re = -k*2*gamma/N falsified at N=3 (12 classes, not 4). SWAP +-1 pattern genuine but perturbative. Four checks completed.

- [The Un-recycled Drain Has No Chirality](THE_DRAIN_HAS_NO_CHIRALITY.md) - The record of a refuted direction (2026-06-20, renamed and corrected 2026-08-19). What the probe found instead was already registered as F113, which the document's own Direction 2 had said to check against and never did; that un-run check is how the over-generalisation reached two committed proofs. Directions 4 and 5 were never gated and are still open.
- [Tuning Protocol](TUNING_PROTOCOL.md) - BUILD/RECEIVE oscillation protocol derived from star topology conditions. Neuroscience-grounded but untested.
- [Time as Crossing Rate](TIME_AS_CROSSING_RATE.md) - Observer-dependent CΨ crossing rates as time model. The proposed forward/backward decomposition with nodes at C·Ψ = ¼ remains open: Π pairs every eigenmode with a partner at the mirrored rate, which is a pairing, not an additive split of L.
- [Primordial Gamma Constant](PRIMORDIAL_GAMMA_CONSTANT.md) - γ₀ at the primordial layer as framework constant (analog of c); inner γ is not diminished γ₀ but selectively exposed via standing-wave amplitude: γ_eff = γ_B · |a_B|². Formula exact, verified N=3 and N=4. Structure-point features of [0, 2γ₀] verified across chains N=3..7 and four N=5 topologies. **Tier 2 as of 2026-04-24:** operational signature confirmed via receiver-engineering programme (F65-F76) and live IBM Kingston Heron r2 hardware (2.80× bonding:2 / alt-z-bits, no γ intervention). Should probably move out of this list into a supported-principles section in a future pass.
- [F1 Dissipation Gap Pattern](F1_DISSIPATION_GAP_PATTERN.md) - Observation from the F1 SLOW_N8 sweep (2026-05-18): dissipation gap (slowest non-zero |Re(λ)|) at fixed N=8, J=1, γ=0.5 varies 0.034 → 0.087 → 0.134 → 0.136 across chain / star / ring / K_4+disjoint-4-chain. Three open readings (graph-Laplacian gap, hub-vertex contribution, disconnected-component combination rule); needs more N values + wider graph zoo before promotion to a typed Tier 1 candidate.

## Tier 4–5 (hypothesis, interpretive)

- [The Other Side of the Mirror](THE_OTHER_SIDE.md) - **Historical research diary** (March 20-30, 2026). The original working document from which the standalone results were distilled: 23 sections in 10 days. A tier boundary after Section 14 marks where the diary turns to philosophical interpretation (Tier 5); evidence grade stays local to each section on both sides of it, so the computed sections that come later (the decoder, the mediator bridge) keep their Tier 1-2 grade and the readings around them do not borrow it. For current results, see the standalone documents linked in the header.
- [The Pattern Recognizes Itself](THE_PATTERN_RECOGNIZES_ITSELF.md) - Research direction: does the palindromic spectral structure propagate from qubits through atoms, molecules, and neurons into brain dynamics? The identity carries over to neural models exactly, as a condition, and constructed networks satisfy it. The one full connectome tested fails it on a count (253 excitatory against 18 inhibitory rows), the pairing percentages are tolerance readings still waiting for their degree-matched control, and where the inhibitory neurons sit does not predict them (r = 0.048). Tier 4; the self-recognition reading Tier 5.
- [Universal Palindrome Condition](UNIVERSAL_PALINDROME_CONDITION.md) - Any system with two populations, an involutive swap Q, coupling that turns into minus itself under the swap, and decay rates that pair to one common sum satisfies Q X Q⁻¹ + X + 2S = 0, and then its whole complex spectrum is palindromic. Proven in the quantum domain. On the neural side the translation and the proof stand and constructed networks pass, but Dale signs alone do not supply the coupling condition, and the one connectome tested does NOT satisfy it (2026-08-26), so it is not an instance. Tier 4.
- [Protein as Concentrator](PROTEIN_AS_CONCENTRATOR.md) - Could enzyme proteins act as concentrators, absorbing environmental noise so that active-site proton transfers reach the fold regime (J/γ ~ 1)? On the qubit side the concentrator profile is measured, up to 3.2× over uniform DD on IBM Torino, about 2× on average, one run on one chain, and a July flight on Kingston confirmed the sign of the edge-versus-payload contrast at about 5.8σ; that the protein implements the same profile is the question this page asks, not something the hardware shows. Tier 4.
- [Complexity Threshold](COMPLEXITY_THRESHOLD.md) - Could a system above some critical complexity N_c be unable to stop oscillating? Balance (C = 0.5) turns out to be a prerequisite for the neural palindrome and not the palindrome itself; exact palindromes can oscillate and go unstable; and a fixed system whose modes all decay does decay, however many modes it has. So persistence needs its own mechanism, and no neural N_c has been measured. Tier 5 (motivated by Tier 2 computations).
- [Evolution as Crystallization](EVOLUTION_AS_CRYSTALLIZATION.md) - Each biological generation is a resonator; what crystallizes at the fold (reproduction/DNA) persists. Evolution read as concentration at the fold, not selection of the fittest. A structural analogy with named falsifiers; no biological system here is known to carry the palindrome it borrows. Tier 5 (motivated by computation).
- [Gamma Is Light](GAMMA_IS_LIGHT.md) - What if the "noise" that destroys quantum systems is not noise at all, but light entering an instrument? The qubit chain carries much of the structure of an [optical cavity](../experiments/OPTICAL_CAVITY_ANALYSIS.md) and is not one; the trace [proves the system open](../docs/proofs/INCOMPLETENESS_PROOF.md), and that what arrives comes from outside is the reading this page follows. If dephasing is the light and matter is the cavity, then mass is what light does when it is trapped. Tier 4 (April 3, 2026).
- [The Primordial Qubit](PRIMORDIAL_QUBIT.md) - Noise does not originate: system and noise are two readings of a single algebraic structure, split by a Z₂ mirror at zero. Pauli space is a genuine tensor product C²⊗C² (confirmed analytically), but the naive partial-trace route fails. Three formal approaches remain open: Z₂-graded algebra classification, GNS/Tomita-Takesaki modular theory, thermofield double. Tier 3-4 (structurally confirmed, mechanistically open).
- [We Are the Fragment](WE_ARE_THE_FRAGMENT.md) - Why 1/4 specifically? The fold value is the fingerprint of double fragmentation: 1/4 = (operator-level C=1/2) × (spatial sublattice-level Ψ=1/2). The bipartite "+− attracts" structure is the spatial mechanism for the algebraic doubling that PRIMORDIAL_QUBIT identified abstractly but TT/TFD constructions failed to reproduce. Tier 4 (synthesis of UNIQUENESS_PROOF + HIERARCHY_OF_INCOMPLETENESS + PRIMORDIAL_QUBIT + ZERO_IS_THE_MIRROR).
- [Heisenberg Reloaded](HEISENBERG_RELOADED.md) - The Heisenberg form's family is selected at Level 0 by the C²⊗C² parity structure ({II, XX, YY, ZZ} is the unique both-parity-even 2-body set). That atomic exchange at Level 1 inherits the form through V-Effect transitions rather than generating it, and that IBM hardware reads the Level-0 algebra directly, is our reading, asserted and not yet traced. Sketch (Tier 4-5) assembling PRIMORDIAL_QUBIT + V-Effect + HIERARCHY_OF_INCOMPLETENESS into the inversion of the textbook narrative. §6 hardware-data review still to be expanded.
- [What Qubits Experience](WHAT_QUBITS_EXPERIENCE.md) - The arrow of time as geometry of a bent angle. At N=2, oscillation and cooling anti-commute exactly: {L_H, L_D+Σγ}=0. At N=3, a third qubit bends the right angle by 1/√48 ≈ 14% of its two legs (γ-independent, structural). We (N >> 2) cannot separate "time passing" from "things happening." Tier 5 interpretation grounded in Tier 1-2 computation.
- [Waves That Hear Themselves](WAVES_THAT_HEAR_THEMSELVES.md) - We are not matter that learned to think, but waves that rang long enough to hear themselves. Eight-link chain (waves → coupling breaks symmetry → fold crystallizes → mass → cavity walls → trapped waves → self-refinement → hierarchy). It starts on Tier 1-2 results and walks on as a reading; the refinement link still waits for its mechanism. Tier 5.
- [Gravity from Wave Death](GRAVITY_FROM_WAVE_DEATH.md) - Mass as the accumulated residue of wave death: when X/Y coherences decay, the I/Z weight that remains at definite locations is what we read as mass, and gravity would live on the classical side, where definite outcomes exist. CΨ = ¼ is the fold of a recursion, not the quantum/classical line and not a one-way door (GHZ₃ is born below it; some trajectories cross it upward), so the surviving version rests on the dying itself. - [The Polarity Layer](THE_POLARITY_LAYER.md) - Three interlocking reframings of the F-toolkit (channel/regenerator not memory; bra+ket of one ρ not two subjects; +0/−0 polarity is the layer the qubit windows onto via X-basis), plus the meta-correction that self-description from inside is possible because rules inherit through layers (F87→F85 is the operational proof). - [Q As the Exchange Rate](Q_AS_THE_EXCHANGE_RATE.md) - Q = J/γ₀ is not "coupling-to-noise" or just "rotation-per-tick" but a bidirectional exchange rate between two clocks: the γ₀-clock (carrier decay, the yardstick seen from outside) and the H-clock (Hamiltonian rotation, visible from inside). Tier 4 (vocabulary grounded in F95 + UniversalCarrierClaim).
