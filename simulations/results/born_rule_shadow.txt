===========================================================================
SUMMARY
===========================================================================
1. The Born probabilities split into slow + fast with no cross term: the diagonal of a
   sum is the sum of the diagonals. For |++> the fast part is pure coherence, so the whole
   image comes from the slow part, the steady state I/4; for |01> the fast part carries
   part of the image.
2. The purity can carry a cross term only where slow and fast modes overlap. At N = 2
   they never do: every mode off the real axis sits on Σγ (F48), the real ones sit at 0,
   Σγ and 2Σγ, the slow side is the steady states alone, and those are orthogonal to the
   rest, so the cross term is 0 for every state. At N = 3 |+++> (Hamiltonian-dead) still
   has none, |++0> carries a small one, and random states a fraction of a percent.
3. Photography and shutter language is a reading of this split, not a measurement mechanism.
