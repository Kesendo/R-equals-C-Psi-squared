--- 4. WHY the spectrum sees the sum: the net rate is triangular ---
    one-site thermal dissipator on the (I, X, Y, Z) Pauli basis,
    gamma_down = 0.37, gamma_up = 0.13:
      [ +0.0000   +0.0000   +0.0000   +0.0000]
      [ +0.0000   -0.2500   +0.0000   +0.0000]
      [ +0.0000   +0.0000   -0.2500   +0.0000]
      [ +0.2400   +0.0000   +0.0000   -0.5000]
    diagonal = [0, -r/2, -r/2, -r] with r = gd + gu = 0.50
    the single off-diagonal entry is (Z, I) = +0.240000 = gd - gu, the NET rate
    In this ordering that entry sits strictly below the diagonal, so the
    matrix is TRIANGULAR and the net rate cannot move an eigenvalue.
    That is the mechanism: the spectrum sees only the sum while F84's
    Pi-conjugation violation sees only the difference. Not two readings
    of one quantity; two different entries of one matrix.
