matrix of a chain with no zero bond, so it is an unreduced Jacobi
matrix in either book: real symmetric, tridiagonal, every
off-diagonal 2J_b nonzero.  L and R are the principal submatrices
the seat leaves behind, chi a characteristic polynomial.

  G1  no eigenvector vanishes at an end, read as blind(0) =
      blind(N-1) = 0
  G2  the spectrum is simple: chi(H) is squarefree
  G3  both blocks are unreduced Jacobi too, so chi(L) and chi(R)
      are squarefree and deg gcd COUNTS the shared roots
  G4  every shared root of the two blocks is a root of chi(H)
      (the gluing direction: a shared root really is a node)
  G5  deg gcd(chi(L), chi(R)) = blind(j), the node count and the
      Krylov complement agreeing at every seat

THEOREM A, THE KERNEL AS A COMMUTANT.  dim ker L_SE(j) =
1 + dim commutant(H restricted to the Krylov complement W).  Right
side never mentions a chain, a zero bond or a simplicity fence.
The span identity dim ker = 1 + blind is the special case where
H|_W has a SIMPLE spectrum, since blind(j) = dim W and a commutant
is the sum of squared multiplicities.  The framing is the repo's
(CAUGHT_ERRORS 2026-08-24, THE_SEAT_THAT_CUTS section 5, the F4 seat
sub-bullets); what is computed here is that ONE seat forces the blocks.
