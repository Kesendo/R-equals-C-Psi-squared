=== THE HORIZON READING, MEASURED ===
N=4 Heisenberg chain, uniform Z-dephasing gamma=0.05, Sigma_gamma=0.2

--- 1. the pair sum is F1's real part, and the separation is what grows ---
    r1 + r2 = 2*sigma follows from lambda -> -2*sigma - lambda in one line.
    The matching below is BUILT by nearest reflection, so the deviation of
    the sum from 2*sigma is the palindrome residual itself. Read the
    residual against eps * spectral radius, the eigensolver's error model,
    not against a digit count.
      coupling   max|sum - 2sig|   in eps*rho   min|r1-r2|   max|r1-r2|
    J=0                5.551e-17         0.62    0.000e+00     0.400000
    J=1                1.921e-14         9.14    5.551e-17     0.400000
    J=5                1.650e-13        15.70    0.000e+00     0.400000
    J=50               1.160e-12        11.04    7.772e-16     0.400000
    2*Sigma_gamma = 0.400000000000
    The deviation grows with the spectral radius and stays at the floor in
    eps*rho, which is the law; the raw digits do not survive J=50 and were
    never the point. An exact route exists where the rates are rational:
    p(2c - x) == p(x) over Q(i), implemented in thermal_palindrome_centre.py.
    The separation runs from 0 (a self-paired mode sits at the centre) to
    2*sigma (the extreme pair). That spread is the whole content of the
    reading: the binding never loosens, the fates diverge.
--- 2. the coupling moves everything except the horizon ---
      coupling             centre   max|Im lambda|
    J=0           -0.200000000000            0.000
    J=1           -0.200000000000            9.464
    J=5           -0.200000000000           47.321
    J=50          -0.200000000000          473.205
    The dynamics spans a factor of 50 in max|Im| and the centre does not
    move a digit. Not a coincidence: the centre is trace(L)/dim and the
    commutator part of L is traceless, so it is fixed by the dissipator
    alone. The illumination sets the horizon; the Hamiltonian cannot.

--- 3. the extremes are equally occupied WHILE the palindrome holds ---
                       channel     centre   rate=0  rate=2c   max rate
                   Z-dephasing    -0.2000        5        5   0.400000
             amplitude damping    -0.1000        1        1   0.200000
                  thermal bath    -0.1500        1        1   0.300000
       BROKEN: T1 + co-axial Z    -0.3000        1        0   0.500000
    (the full spectrum is 4^N = 256 modes)
    Equality is forced by the palindrome, which maps the rate-0 set onto
    the rate-2c set bijectively, so it is the pairing restated and not an
    independent finding. The last row is the point of printing it: where
    the palindrome breaks the equality goes too.

    A caution the census itself raises, about the surviving set. The I/Z
    sector has 2^N Pauli strings of dissipator rate 0, and at J = 0 that
    is exactly what survives. With the Hamiltonian on it is NOT:
       N      J=0    J=0.5      J=1      2^N      N+1
       2        4        3        3        4        3
       3        8        4        4        8        4
       4       16        5        5       16        5
       5       32        6        6       32        6
    The survivors number N+1, not 2^N: the Hamiltonian empties the sector
    down to the total-Sz projectors. Measured directly, -i[H, Z_1] puts
    100.0000% of its weight on X/Y-containing strings. So 'the immune
    sector survives forever' is a statement about the dissipator alone.
