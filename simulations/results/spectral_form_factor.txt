PHASE 7: CONNECTION TO PREVIOUS RESULTS
========================================================================

  Poisson/no-ramp behavior is compatible with integrability or block fragmentation;
  it does not prove integrability. Read the sampled SFF separately from a class claim.

  The exact palindrome pairs reflected decay-rate bands. Each pair
  contributes to the trace amplitude; the SFF then squares the full sum.
  This reflection alone does not assign a global symmetry class.

  Topo analysis said: geometric, not topological. The SFF is
  independent of localization (it measures spectral correlations,
  not spatial profiles).

  Analytical formulas: ω_min = 4J(1-cos(π/N)) (formula 2, k=1).
  A sampled FFT candidate is associated with omega_min at N=2-4 and N=6;
  it is not identified at N=5 or N=7 and does not prove the dispersion.
