change of ONE diagonal entry, so the characteristic polynomial is
AFFINE in it:

    det(lambda I - H + s E_jj)  =  chi_H(lambda) + s * chi_cut(lambda)

  with chi_cut the polynomial of H struck at row and column j.  The
  physical case is s = 2 i gamma for the generator
  L_coh = -i H - 2 gamma P_j (f64_topology_scan.py:128), and
  s = -2 i gamma for the +i H convention of EQ-015, so the sign of s
  is convention-dependent while the identity is not.
