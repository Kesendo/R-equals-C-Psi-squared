ALGEBRA: three exact facts, no propagation, no eigensolver.

(1) THE MECHANISM IS SUPPORT.  Z_k = I - 2|k><k| on the
single-excitation block; every reflection-odd state has a node at the
centre, so Z_centre acts as the identity on that subspace.
    max|Z_5 P_odd - P_odd| = 0.000e+00
    max|Z_4 P_odd - P_odd| = 1.000e+00
    max|Z_0 P_odd - P_odd| = 1.000e+00

(2) THE MECHANISM IS NOT PARITY.  With gamma on the CENTRE the
profile [b, ..., b, gamma_centre, b, ..., b] is itself
reflection-symmetric, so R permutes the jump set and the Liouvillian
is exactly reflection-covariant.  Those are the configurations where
the blindness is exact.  The sweep's off-centre rows are NOT
reflection-covariant, which is a second reason covariance cannot be
the explanation.
    R is an involution: max|R R - I| = 0.0e+00
    max|R H R - H| = 0.0e+00
    baseline 0.05, centre 0.0: max|R mask R - mask| = 0.0e+00
    baseline 0.05, centre 0.5: max|R mask R - mask| = 0.0e+00
    baseline 0.2, centre 0.5: max|R mask R - mask| = 0.0e+00

(3) AND THE PREPARATION IS Ad_R-EVEN, so the conserved reflection
parity gives it nothing: R psi = -psi makes |psi><psi| invariant.
    max|R rho0 R - rho0| = 0.0e+00

Together: reflection covariance holds and is NOT the protecting
thing, which is what THE_BLIND_SITE.md section 3 says in prose.  The
protecting thing is that the jump at the centre is the identity on
the subspace the state lives in.

(4) The trajectory consequence, the one thing here that is
propagated: Tr(P_odd rho), with P_odd of dimension 5 of 11.

CONVERGENCE.

(a) THE EXACT BOUND, and why the step was refined.  For a single
excitation in a pure state, I(A:B) = h(p_A) + h(p_B) - h(p_centre)
<= 2 bits, and with no dephasing anywhere it is exactly 2 for all t
because p_centre stays 0.  So anything above 2 below is integrator
error and nothing else.
        dt         max I(A:B)    excess over 2
    0.0500        2.001301979        +1.30e-03
    0.0200        2.000035343        +3.53e-05
    0.0100        2.000002251        +2.25e-06
    0.0050        2.000000142        +1.42e-07

The committed run's dt = 0.05 violates the bound by 1.3e-3, the
same order as the difference the centre's span is computed from, so
everything but the gate is run at dt = 0.02.

(b) THE SPANS ARE dt-CONVERGED ANYWAY, because the error is common
to both arms and cancels in the difference.

    0.0500     7.9433e-03     5.1104e-02     8.0849e-02    -2.4055e-02
    0.0200     7.4891e-03     5.0701e-02     8.0874e-02    -2.4151e-02
    0.0100     7.4630e-03     5.0626e-02     8.0887e-02    -2.4184e-02
    0.0050     7.4547e-03     5.0593e-02     8.0896e-02    -2.4201e-02

The baseline-0.001 column moves by 6 percent between the coarsest and
the finest step; the other three are stable in the fourth digit, and
the negative sign at baseline 0.2 survives every step.

(c) THE WINDOW IS NOT CONVERGED, and cannot be.  Past the transient
every profile decays toward the same limit, so a longer window
dilutes every span alike.  The committed PEAK is window-converged
(that page reports the same 15.566 percent at t_max = 20, 40 and 80);
the window MEAN is not.  What holds still is the RATIO.

So the quotable number is the RATIO, stable to about one percent
across a factor of four in the window.  The absolute percentages
elsewhere in this run are read at t_max = 20 and only there.
