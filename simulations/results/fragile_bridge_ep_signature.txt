==============================================================================
The fragile bridge threshold: where two levels of opposite Krein signature meet
==============================================================================

Two chains of 2 qubits, Heisenberg, gain-loss dephasing +/- gamma, Sigma-gamma = 0.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

At J_bridge = 1.0 and 1.9: geometric multiplicity 1 at algebraic multiplicity 2, the colliding pair
forms a 2x2 Jordan block, a second-order exceptional point (EP2) on the real gamma axis at the threshold.
Below gamma* two distinct imaginary eigenvalues with opposite Krein signatures approach each other
(a Krein collision); above it they leave the axis as a mirror pair, with the same
half-split c*sqrt|delta| on both sides.
A Hopf crossing moves a simple eigenvalue across Re = 0 with a finite slope. At these two couplings
GK keeps every simple eigenvalue of a block on the axis, and the departure has infinite slope:
the threshold is not a Hopf crossing.
At both couplings the bisected gamma_crit sits slightly below the collision: the approaching pair
is ill-conditioned, and the rounding noise in its real parts trips the 1e-12 test while the pair is
still split on the axis.

3. Zeros of the threshold: couplings where an even and an odd level already coincide
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

At gamma = 0 each block of L_H splits into G-even and G-odd levels, and further by F and S where
they act inside the block. The gain-loss part anticommutes with G, so it couples an even level to
an odd one. Where the two have the same frequency omega (an eigenvalue of i*L_H), it splits them at
first order, lambda = -i*omega +/- s*gamma + O(gamma^2), so max Re lambda = s*gamma + O(gamma^2) > 0
for small gamma > 0: gamma_crit = 0, the pair is semisimple at gamma = 0, and there is no
exceptional point.

  H on the popcount-1 sector: -x - 2sqrt(x^2 + 1) (P_site-1), x - 2 (P_site+1), -x + 2sqrt(x^2 + 1) (P_site-1), x + 2 (P_site+1)
  H on the popcount-2 sector: -x - 2sqrt(x^2 - 2x + 4) - 2 (P_site+1, X+1), -x - 2sqrt(x^2 + 1) (P_site-1, X-1), x - 2 (P_site+1, X-1), -x + 2sqrt(x^2 - 2x + 4) - 2 (P_site+1, X+1), -x + 2sqrt(x^2 + 1) (P_site-1, X-1), x + 2 (P_site+1, X+1)
  (x = J_bridge / J with J = 1; P_site the site reversal, X the spin flip; popcount 0 and 4: x + 2;
  popcount 3 repeats popcount 1)

Exact search over every pair of opposite-G levels in one symmetry sector of the blocks (0,1), (0,2), (1,1), (1,2), (2,2)
(every other block is an image of these under F, S or Hermitian conjugation, except the four 1x1
corner blocks (0,0), (0,4), (4,0), (4,4), which hold a single level and so no pair), radicals eliminated
by resultants, real roots x > 0 of every factor; a root counts where the two levels agree to 1e-25
at 40 digits. The spurious roots squaring adds leave the levels at least 2.000 apart; an
identically equal opposite-G pair: False.
