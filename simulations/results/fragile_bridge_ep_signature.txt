==============================================================================
What a coarse scan sees instead
==============================================================================

K diverges only AT threshold, so a scan that never samples close to it reads a
modest number somewhere else. The leading mode's K across a wide range:

   gamma/gamma_crit   Petermann K
              1.001         403.2
              1.010         40.92
              1.050         8.726
              1.200         2.724
              1.460         39.42
              1.500         19.18
              2.000         4.304
              3.000         2.206

There is a secondary bump above threshold, near gamma/gamma_crit = 1.46, an order
of magnitude below the reading at delta = 1e-3. A height and a location quoted
from different points are not one measurement.
