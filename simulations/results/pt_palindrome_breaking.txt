  N = 9 data points (excluding ε=0)

  STRONG correlation between pairing error and γ_crit shift
  within this one-parameter composite perturbation sweep.

  Direction: POSITIVE. Larger palindrome error = larger γ_crit.
  The same ε changes both the symmetry and the damping action;
  this sweep does not isolate either change as the cause.

========================================================================
SUMMARY
========================================================================

Phase 3: Depolarizing noise simultaneously:
  1. Breaks the palindrome (error ∝ ε, from X-dephasing component)
  2. Shifts the trace(L)/dim(L) midpoint away from zero
  3. Modifies γ_crit

  γ_crit range: 0.187310 to 0.458230
  γ_crit(ε=0) = 0.187310
