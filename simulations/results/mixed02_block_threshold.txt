G5  THE N=3 EXTRAS NEED UNIFORM BONDS: the drift law, which is tolerance-free
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Detuning one bond by delta moves the would-be extras OFF the floor. Any
    fixed membership tolerance is therefore a window on delta, and quoting
    'the extras are gone' without the law just moves the blindness one decade
    down. So measure the drift itself and fit its power.
    delta=0.01     max |Re + 2gamma| among the {0,2} modes = 9.944e-06   (admitted by a tolerance of 1e-05 or looser)
    delta=0.001    max |Re + 2gamma| among the {0,2} modes = 1.003e-07   (admitted by a tolerance of 1e-07 or looser)
    delta=0.0001   max |Re + 2gamma| among the {0,2} modes = 1.004e-09   (admitted by a tolerance of 1e-09 or looser)

Reading. The {0,2} extras are not an N = 3 accident. They are the low-N end
of a closure condition on the (n,n) sectors, met by XY at N = 2 and 3 and by
Heisenberg at N = 2 only, the difference being the SE ZZ diagonal (G6). And
at N = 2 they sit ABOVE the band edge whenever Q > 2/sqrt(3) (G4), so the
chain has a second band-edge counterexample beside the ring's 4-cycle, in
that regime. Uniform bonds are part of the statement, not a convenience (G5).
