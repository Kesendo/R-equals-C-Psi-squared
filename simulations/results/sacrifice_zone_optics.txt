Thin-film comparison:
  Quarter-wave stack: T ~ 1 - exponential (very fast)
  Our system (SumMI): ~ N^2 (polynomial, much slower)
  Interpretation: The cavity is NOT a simple dielectric stack.
  It is a dispersive cavity where mode coupling creates quadratic,
  not exponential, transmission scaling.

===========================================================================
SUMMARY
===========================================================================

1. REFLECTION/TRANSMISSION (N in [3, 4, 5, 6]):
   T_eff up at every N: yes. Q_max up at every N: yes.
   Q_med up at every N: NO, it FALLS at N=[3, 4, 6]. The gain is in
   the best mode and in how many modes ring, not in the typical mode.

2. IMPEDANCE MATCHING: gamma_edge ~ N*gamma_base (linear, not geometric
   mean). The sacrifice zone is a linear accumulator, not a classical
   AR coating. But it achieves the same effect: smooth entry of light.

3. ABSORPTION RATES: the flat uniform Re = -2*gamma_base becomes a spread.
   Concentrator Re range on the (0,1) block: N=3: [-0.14940, -0.05089], N=4: [-0.17139, -0.03054], N=5: [-0.18561, -0.02053]

4. FREQUENCIES: measured level for level on the (0,1) block, checked against
   the full Liouvillian at N=3,4,5, at equal total budget. They MOVE at
   every N tested (N=3: 0.00802, N=4: 0.01684, N=5: 0.02859, N=6: 0.04203, N=7: 0.05346, N=8: 0.05735, N=9: 0.06435, N=10: 0.11358, N=11: 0.16814, N=12: 0.17604).
   The move stays under the level's own half-width only up to N=7;
   from N=8 it exceeds it. It is not a uniformly small effect.
   At N=[9, 10, 11, 12] the pairing itself is unreliable (shift not small
   against the level spacing), so those rows are a size, not a per-level map.
   Controls: the size is monotone in how uneven the profile is (Control B),
   the N-trend at fixed shape is not monotone (Control A), and raising a
   uniform gamma moves the full spectrum by a comparable amount (Control C).
   So what an AR coating would hold fixed is touched, and not by this
   profile in particular.

5. SCALING: Quadratic (N^2), not exponential. The cavity is dispersive,
   not a simple dielectric stack.

VERDICT: The concentrator is the entrance pupil of the cavity on the
absorption side: it raises Q_max and T_eff at every N in [3, 4, 5, 6].
The analogy is neither quantitative (linear accumulation, not the
geometric mean) nor complete: it moves the resonance frequencies too,
which an AR coating does not. But moving them is not special to the
concentrator, it is what a dephasing profile does.
