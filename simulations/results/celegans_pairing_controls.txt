STEP 5: what IS different about this connectome, and what kind of fact
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

The self-paired modes are eigenvalues of f'*W_norm at zero: modes the
wiring does nothing to. Counting them needs a tolerance and an eigensolver.
The synapse matrix is an integer matrix, so the same content is exact. Two
different exact objects live here and only the second is the mode count:

  nullity(W)        = 39   (geometric: independent eigenvectors at 0)
  multiplicity of 0 = 64   (algebraic, by the rank chain: the MODES)
  float check, |lambda + 1| < 1e-4 : 64
  [PASS] G10 the exact multiplicity matches the float count: 64 == 64, so the rank chain counts what the tolerance was counting, exactly and without one
  [PASS] G11 the exact rank agrees at every prime tried: 3 primes, 1 distinct value; rank over GF(p) bounds the rational rank from below, so agreement is the check
  nullity unsigned 39, with Dale's law applied 39   (READ, not a gate:
    Dale's law is a diagonal sign matrix acting on the left, and rank is
    invariant under that, so no data could make these differ. It is stated
    because it decides a WORD: 'signed' does not belong in the claim.)

  And most of the deficit is not spectral. The structural rank is decided
  by bipartite matching on the zero pattern alone, no field, no weights,
  no signs, and it upper-bounds the true rank:

                             real  null mean   excess
  structural nullity           36      30.15     5.85
  exact nullity                39      30.16     8.84

  Of the 8.8-point excess, 5.9 is already in
  the zero pattern. The honest name for most of this is a small maximum
  matching, not a numerical degeneracy.
  [PASS] G13 the connectome is more degenerate at zero than degree-matched rewiring: multiplicity 64 against a null mean of 48.0 (range 43-56), p = 0.005

STEP 6: Q_max is arithmetic on the damping, not a measured loss
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

NEURAL_GAMMA_CAVITY, Result 2b, reads Q_max = 0.1 against the qubit
cavity's 68 to 75 as 'extremely lossy'. The eigenvalues of
-I/tau + f'*W_n are EXACTLY -1/tau + f'*mu, so Q is a function of the graph
spectrum and the two chosen constants and of nothing else. That identity is
an exact route, so it is compared rather than tolerated.

  THIS SECTION IS A READ, NOT A GATE, and the reason is worth stating.
  Q_max = f'|Im mu| / |1/tau - f' Re mu| is an identity, exact in exact
  arithmetic. Both sides of any numerical check of it are eigensolver
  output, so there is no exact route to compare along; and a bound built
  from this matrix's worst conditioning is vacuous, because the defective
  cluster on the centre pushes it past 1e40. A gate here would pass on
  anything. What the identity says needs no gate:

  worst deviation, assembled vs -1/tau + f'*mu : 4.68e-09 (read)
  Q_max across the eight rows                  : factor 51

  Q_max is therefore arithmetic on two chosen constants and the graph
  spectrum. The provenance is the finding, and it is checkable rather than
  statistical: tau = 1 ms is stipulated bare at neural_gamma_cavity.py:48-49
  for the Wilson-Cowan block, and the C. elegans Jacobian at :216 contains
  no tau at all, only a bare -I. G0 above already pins that diagonal.
