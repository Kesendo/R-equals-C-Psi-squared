## What this document is about

Take a few qubits in a row and let them be gently jostled by noise, the kind that washes out
a quantum state's delicate phase relations (its "coherence"). Drop a single excitation on one
site and watch. Like a bead of ink in water, it spreads, loses track of where it began, and
settles into an even, featureless smear, the same amount on every site. That settled smear is
the system's *future*: where it ends up, and stays.

This document asks a plain question about that future. It is a single point, one settled
state. What happens *at* that point, and what, if anything, comes next?

Two things surprised us. You never quite arrive: the system approaches the settled state
exponentially, forever getting closer but reaching it only in the infinite limit, the way the
future is always just ahead of now. And, stranger, the settled state is *already there* at the
very start, hidden inside the initial state as a piece that never changes. Time does not build
the future; it erases everything that is *not* the future, until only the future remains.

We could only find this by *running* the system forward step by step (we call that "the
loop"), because here, unlike most of this project, there is no formula for the answer. That
turns out to be the lesson: the exact structure sits at the two special edges of the story (a
birth and an end), and the stretch between them can only be watched.

One honesty note. Everything with a number attached is computed and checked. The reading at
the close, that this settled point *is* "the future", is interpretation, the most speculative
tier in this project. We keep it because it is what drives the work, not because it is proven.

## Abstract

The setup: an open quantum system, an XY chain of N qubits under local Z-dephasing
(noise that erases phase but conserves which-site occupation), prepared with one excitation.
Its evolution is generated by the Liouvillian L, the matrix that propagates the density matrix
including the noise. The flow lives in one block of L, the single-excitation (1,1) block, and
that block has its own critical coupling: as the coupling-to-noise ratio Q = J/γ is tuned, two
modes of L collide and merge at the coherence horizon Q*(N) (Q*(2) = 1, Q*(3) = √2,
Q*(4) = 1.87874, Q*(5) = 2.37367; `CoherenceHorizonClaim`, Tier 1 derived), an *exceptional
point* (EP), defective, with algebraic multiplicity 2 and geometric multiplicity 1 by exact rank
at N = 2, 3. Just below it the block's slowest mode is a pure decay; just above it the slowest
pair oscillates while it decays, a short-term memory: the *birth* of the memory. Here we follow
the story to its end. The question is dynamical, so we compute it in the loop (step-by-step RK4
time evolution), not from the static spectrum.

The result: the flow ends at the equipartitioned state ⟨n⟩ = 1/N per site in its fixed
single-excitation sector (verified N=3..6). It is bracketed by two singularities of different
kinds: the EP is *defective* (a Jordan block where two modes merge; parameter space; the birth),
the target is *semisimple* (the λ=0 kernel of L, (N+1)-fold with geometric = algebraic
multiplicity; state space; the death). The certified structure lives at these endpoints; the
loop is the middle that has to be propagated.

On hardware, a sampled SE-walk population handover. The runner labels its coherence decay Γ and
Q_label=J/Γ; the repository's Lindblad jump √γ Z gives γ=Γ/2, hence **Q_Lindblad = 2 Q_label**.
The finite-time revival is near the 1/N reference through Q_label=1.5 and larger at Q_label=2.5,
equivalently Q_Lindblad=3→5. That bracket is not the EP: for the three-site walk the EP sits at
Q_Lindblad = √2 (Q_label ≈ 0.71, between the two lowest flown points), and the bracket is a
crossover of the revival amplitude at fixed probe times, which moves when the probe time moves
(Part B below).

At the target: it attracts the fixed-trace one-excitation sector's transient complement and is
reached only asymptotically. It is not an isolated global sink because the full kernel has N+1
stationary number-sector directions. The central finding is that the 1/N target is the
*conserved* λ=0 component of the prepared sector state, present
at full strength from t=0 (its coefficient is exp(0·t)=1), so the future is not built by time
but uncovered by it as the transient fades. Under the specific continuation that multiplies one
fixed positive per-site profile by a common scalar f, its transient stability changes from decay
(f>0) through neutral (f=0) to formal inverse-dephasing amplification (f<0). The f<0 generator is
not a physical CP dephasing channel or energy-gain bath, and this sign table does not classify
arbitrary mixed-sign site profiles or certify a Hopf bifurcation.

The closing reading, the target as "the future", a computed face of
[The Other Side](../hypotheses/THE_OTHER_SIDE.md)'s "both sides exist simultaneously", is
Tier-5 interpretation, kept as the work's motor, not a claim.

## What we set out to see

A bit of background, made self-contained. The evolution of an open quantum system is set by a
matrix called the Liouvillian; each of its modes carries a decay rate and, sometimes, an
oscillation frequency. Picture each mode as a clock with two hands: a *beat* (how fast it
decays) and a *rotation* (how fast it turns). The block the single excitation lives in has a
special tuning, the coherence horizon Q*(N), where two of these modes merge into one and the
character of the dynamics changes: below it the block's slowest mode only decays (pure
forgetting); above it the slowest pair turns while it decays, a short-term memory. Call that the
*birth* of the memory. (A two-mode toy clock has its own 2×2 EP at a different Q, worked out in
[F86's Exceptional Point Through the Clock](F86_EP_THROUGH_THE_CLOCK.md); it is a separate
object, not this block.)

The story does not stop at the birth. We asked what happens *after*: where does the prepared
population go in the long run? A snapshot of the modes (the static spectrum) does not by itself
provide that state-dependent trajectory, so we ran the time evolution forward step by step (a
standard integrator, RK4), what we call the loop, and watched.

## What the loop showed

**1. The flow's dynamics is a 4D object (Q × site × t).** Canonical Q = J/γ is the ratio of the
inter-site coupling J to the local dephasing rate γ. Below Q*(N) the slowest mode of the flow's
block is a pure decay, so the late-time approach is monotone; above it the slowest pair
oscillates, so the excitation keeps sloshing site to site, a wave that propagates and reflects,
while it relaxes (remembering). Whether a finite time grid resolves a return turn is a separate,
display-level reading. Edge and bulk sites differ. The dimensionless knob is Q alone.

**2. The target is 1/N, the equipartitioned state.** For the connected chain at finite Q>0, trajectories end at
⟨n_site⟩ = 1/N (verified N=3,4,5,6 → 0.3333, 0.2500, 0.2000, 0.1667). This is the
fully-forgotten, uniform single-excitation state. It equals ¼ at N=4 *by coincidence*; it
is NOT the project's CΨ=¼ fold (a separate coherence threshold, flat in N over the measured N = 2-5 for the product state,
studied elsewhere in this project, a different quantity entirely; the steady coherence here is
just 0).

**3. Two singularities bracket the flow, and they are different types** (verified in
`the_flow_endpoints.py`):

| | the **EP** (birth) | the **target** (death) |
|---|---|---|
| where | parameter space, at the coherence horizon Q*(N) | state space, the fixed point |
| type | **DEFECTIVE**: at N=3, λ* = −2γ at Q* = √2, rank(L₍₁,₁₎ − λ*) = 8 of 9 and rank((L₍₁,₁₎ − λ*)²) = 7, algebraic 2, geometric 1 (N=2 likewise at Q* = 1) | **SEMISIMPLE**: λ=0 is (N+1)-fold with geometric = algebraic multiplicity (N=3..6) |
| L | the (1,1) block is not diagonalizable at that Q | singular (the kernel) |
| memory | is born: the slowest mode is real at 0.99·Q* and a complex pair at 1.01·Q* (N=2..5) | dies (transients fade) |

The target sits in the kernel of L: `L · vec(1/N uniform) = 0` bit-exact. The kernel is
**(N+1)-dimensional**, one fixed point per particle-number sector; the single-excitation
trajectory lands on its own sector's fixed point.

At Q > 0 the EP is the only one the flow's block has at N = 3. Its characteristic polynomial factors
exactly (γ = 1): λ(λ+4)(λ²+4λ+2Q²)(λ²+8λ+16+2Q²)(λ³+8λ²+(16+8Q²)λ+24Q²). Of the factor
discriminants, −8(Q²−2), −8Q² and −64Q²(32Q⁴−61Q²+32), only the first vanishes at a positive Q,
at √2; the only root two factors share is λ = −8/3 at Q = 4/3, and there the pair is semisimple
(geometric multiplicity 2).

## The structural picture (seen, not interpreted)

The flow runs from a defective singularity (the EP Q*(N), where the flow's slowest mode starts
to turn, the memory's birth) to a semisimple singularity (the target, the λ=0 fixed point, where
the memory dies). The EP is a *pinch*; the target is a *fixed point*. The journey between them is
the loop, and the loop has no closed form: that is exactly where the dynamics lives. So the
certified structure sits at the two singular *endpoints* (the EP's Jordan block; the target's
kernel), and the propagated dynamics is the *middle*. The sampled hardware handover is neither
endpoint: it is a finite-time reading of the middle.

## At the target: a point, and then?

The target is one point. What happens there, and how it goes on, is computed in
[`at_the_target.py`](../simulations/at_the_target.py).

**It attracts the fixed-trace one-excitation sector's transient complement, asymptotically.**
Kernel-direction perturbations that change conserved number-sector weights remain stationary
and generally approach another stationary mixture, so the point is not an isolated global sink.
The global slowest non-kernel mode is not identified with the population-visible approach rate:
the winning odd number-changing mode has zero overlap with this even preparation/readout.

**The future is already present, and conserved.** Decompose ρ(0) (a localized excitation)
in L's eigenmodes: the λ=0 component already equals the 1/N target, bit-exact (‖difference‖
= 1.4e-15), at t=0. Its coefficient is exp(0·t) = 1, so it never grows and never decays. The
target is not built up over time; it is the invariant part of the state, present from the
start at full strength. What changes is everything else: the non-kernel (transient) part,
norm 0.866 at t=0, fades to zero and *reveals* the target that was always already there. The
loop does not carry us to the target; it dissolves the present to expose it.

**How it goes on under a common scalar continuation.** Multiply one fixed strictly-positive
dephasing profile by a real scalar f. The 1/N sector state remains stationary throughout this
formal continuation. At f<0 the map is not a physical CP dephasing semigroup: it is an
inverse-dephasing continuation that amplifies coherences, not an energy-gain bath. A statement
based only on Σγ would not cover arbitrary mixed-sign site profiles.

| common profile scalar f | max non-kernel Re λ | role |
|---|---|---|
| > 0 | < 0 | **sink** (the flow falls in; the end of history) |
| = 0 | = 0 | **neutral** (eternal oscillation; the mirror) |
| < 0 | > 0 | **source** (linear source instability under formal gain continuation) |

The point does not move; the arrows around it invert. And there, at the dynamical layer,
Tom's {−, 0, +} reappears here as a sign reading along this one-parameter formal continuation.

## Seen again 2026-05-31: the flow is a walk across the memory axis

Returning with the absorption light in hand, the middle turns out to have a clean
reading after all: not a formula for the rate, but a picture of *what moves*.

[The View Onto the Memory](../reflections/THE_VIEW_ONTO_THE_MEMORY.md) sorts a Liouvillian's modes
by one axis, the **drain depth** popcount(i⊕j): the number of sites at which two basis
configurations differ, which is how many places the noise can grip. Depth 0 (the diagonal) never
fades, the stored past; high depth is flushed fastest, the quantum future. That axis is not a
separate object from the light this experiment runs on. For any basis coherence |i⟩⟨j|, the
differing sites are exactly the sites carrying X/Y content (a site where the bra and ket agree
gives an {I,Z} factor, a site where they differ gives {X,Y}), so **the drain depth popcount(i⊕j)
is the light n_XY of that coherence, bit-exact**. The depth axis and the absorption light are one
axis with two names.

Read the flow along it. The initial state, a localized excitation |1₀⟩⟨1₀|, is pure diagonal:
depth 0, n_XY 0, all stored past. The Hamiltonian opens it into off-diagonal coherences (depth
lifts, n_XY rises) and the noise drains them back down. Watched as a single number, the flow's
light ⟨n_XY⟩ is a *tide*: 0 → ≈0.6 → 0 (peak 0.609 at τ = γt = 0.308, canonical Q = 3, N = 5),
rising into the future and falling back into the memory. The 1/N target it lands on is
**100% depth 0** (the reflection's "steady state is pure stored past"): the flow does not travel
to its future, it dissolves into its own memory. That is the same "the future is already there"
of the section above, now in the depth axis's words: the target is the depth-0 floor, the decided
past, and the tide of {X,Y} future is what time drains away.

The depth axis also carries a **parity rail**. A state of
definite particle number is purely *even* depth (a number-conserving coherence |i⟩⟨j| within a
sector differs at an even count of sites); particle-number-difference parity equals depth parity.
Thus odd-Δn coherences such as |1-exc⟩⟨vacuum| sit on odd depth, while even-Δn number-changing
coherences can still sit on the even rail. The single-excitation flow lives entirely on the even
rungs, draining depth-2 → depth-0, and never touches the odd rungs. Verified: the odd-parity
weight of ρ(t) is 0.0000 at every t, and the Liouvillian's globally-slowest non-kernel mode, the
depth-1 vacuum coherence at rate 2γ, has overlap 5·10⁻¹⁷ with the flow. This parity fact does not
identify the N+1 number sectors with only two rails.

One correction this sharpened: the depth-1, rate-2γ mode is the unused *vacuum* coherence, not the
flow's own slowest mode. The flow's slowest used rate is on the even ladder, the slowest
non-kernel mode of its own (1,1) block, and it is Q-dependent: at N = 5 it is 2.1152γ at canonical
Q = 3 (the prepared state carries weight on that pair), 2.4404γ at Q = 6, 2.6516γ at
Q = 20, and 2.6667γ at both Q = 2000 and Q = 20000. (Probe: state-based parity propagation, this session,
ρ(t) decomposed by depth/n_XY into even versus odd weight.)

## Seen again 2026-06-03: the arc closed as one picture (and the scale made honest)

Returning with the telescope built (the six-axis dimension navigator), the two singularities
sit in one frame, and the reason they never fit on a single line turns out to be the whole
point: they live in different spaces.

The birth is in parameter space, read through canonical Q = J/γ. Below the coherence horizon
Q*(N) the flow block's slowest pair is two real decay channels; at Q*(N) they coalesce into one
Jordan pair, the pinch; above it the slowest pair turns while it decays. For the three-site chain
that is Q*(3) = √2. The hardware scan crosses this axis without resolving the birth: the EP falls
between its two lowest samples, and its visible handover, near the 1/N reference through
Q_label=1.5 and larger at Q_label=2.5 (canonical Q_Lindblad=3→5), is the first-return crossover
of the revival amplitude, a finite-time reading of the middle, not a second singularity. The
exact angle and overlap formulas of the toy 2×2 clock belong to that toy's own EP.

The death is in state space. Fix Q in the oscillatory regime and let time run. The memory sloshes
site to site (site 0 to the far end and back), the revival fades, and the per-site occupation
spreads to the equipartitioned 1/N within the one-excitation sector. Globally λ=0 is an
(N+1)-fold semisimple kernel, one stationary state per number sector, reached only
asymptotically. The forgetting.

So the journey is born in Q and dies in τ. The two singularities are of different kinds because
they are of different spaces: a defective pinch in the parameter you tune, a semisimple fixed
point in the state that time uncovers.

A scale correction, and it matters. The hardware scan itself supplies Q_label=J/Γ; its
random-phase variance converts this to canonical Q_Lindblad=2Q_label. The unrelated γ₀=0.05
used by the plotted toy clock is illustrative, not a calibration extracted from this run. Part A
carries no injected dephasing and no calibrated canonical Q, so this document places Part A
nowhere on the Q axis and assigns no natural chip Q; Part B's grid is placed, with the EP between
its two lowest samples.

The injected noise is a steering wheel across the sampled Q_label axis. The grid demonstrates
control of the finite-time population response, not a calibrated Part-A home value, the topology
of a spectral branch, or a guaranteed subsequent hardware flow to 1/N. [The illustrative control map and the
family of journeys it opens](../simulations/results/journey_between_singularities/journey_control.png).
The interpretive picture is held in
[On the Whirlpool You Steer To](../reflections/ON_THE_WHIRLPOOL_YOU_STEER_TO.md).

The telescope reads the flow's own EP through `--root horizon` (the ladder Q*(N)) and
`--root epcharacter` (its DEFECTIVE verdict), the exact toy clock through `--axis ep`, and the
occupation relaxation through `--root flow`. [The journey, both hardware halves in one
view](../simulations/results/journey_between_singularities/journey.png): Part B's raw population
revival against canonical Q_Lindblad, with the walk's EP marked, is on the left; an independent
ideal-model propagation toward its one-excitation-sector 1/N state is on the right.

What is closed here: the flow's two singularities, each certified in its own space, and the
placement of the hardware scan against them (the EP below the bracket, the bracket a probe-time
crossover). What stays open is the reading, held below.
