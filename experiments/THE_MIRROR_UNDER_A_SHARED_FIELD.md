# The mirror under a shared field: the palindrome's price becomes an operator, and under a field shared alike the spectrum pairs block by block, each pair about its own centre

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Authors:** Thomas Wicht, Claude (Anthropic, Opus 5.5)
**Verifier:** [`mirror_under_shared_field.py`](../simulations/mirror_under_shared_field.py) (integer inputs, every comparison exact, no tolerance in any gate; 347 claims and 25 printed values, all hold)
**Grew from:** [The singlet under a shared field](SINGLET_UNDER_A_SHARED_FIELD.md), whose two-site dose (1 − η)γt this page carries to N sites, and a question beside it: does the F1 palindrome survive when the sites share their field?
**A word fence:** *shared*, *collective* and *correlated* as on the singlet page, for the noise; a shared coherent field (the "evenly shared field" of [On Forgetting](../reflections/ON_FORGETTING.md)) is called a uniform longitudinal field here. η is the correlation coefficient of the site fields, not the Glossary's hardware watcher factor η(r) and not the η-pairing of the frozen-band proofs (that ladder appears below as W, and its chains, multiplets and lines keep the name η). γ_k is site k's Lindblad coefficient, so under local noise a coherence that differs at site k pays 2γ_k there, and σ = Σ_k γ_k is the summed dephasing rate; and the *price* is what the mirror charges, F1's 2σ, the [Glossary](../docs/GLOSSARY.md)'s "affine shift of the mirror" and the word the [Mirror](../compute/MirrorWorld/Mirror.cs) object uses. A *covariance* is an off-diagonal entry a_kl of the field's Kossakowski matrix (the model below), the covariance of two sites' fields, not the singlet page's spin-spin Cov_a of a state; κ is the covariance when one value is common to every pair (κ = γη at uniform rates). A *dose* is a rate times a time, as on the singlet page.
**Blocks and maps:** a *block* (p, q) is a joint-popcount block (the Glossary's (p,q)-block), p the popcount of the ket and q that of the bra (MirrorWorld's `Block` names the two the other way round). The ket of |i⟩⟨j| is the row label i, so the bra flip R(ρ) = ρ·X^⊗N, |i⟩⟨j| ↦ |i⟩⟨j̄|, complements the bra here, the map that PROOF_PI_FACTORS_AS_R_TIMES_D calls the ket reflection in its column-index naming, and the bra fold is the same index map taken antiunitarily, as F89d takes it; d = p − q and m = p + q − N are the block lattice's two charges ([The One Square](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §3). A block and its *image* are (p, q) and (q, N − p); under one common covariance they pair about a *pair centre* −σ(p, q), the blockwise σ (for a general pattern, a block whose two popcounts each see one value of K pairs about −(K_p + K_q)/2, below). The four one-dimensional *corner* blocks are (0, 0), (0, N), (N, 0) and (N, N), not the (1,1)-type corner block of F140. The *half-filling point* is the lattice's centre (N/2, N/2). K is the noise's decay operator Σ_μ c_μ†c_μ, the c_μ jump operators (the SO(4) proof's K is its hopping operator, the c_l of the ladder section are Jordan-Wigner fermions, and the c_ij of the price-pair page are its covariances). ℛ is the chain's site reversal, F71's mirror (not the R of the Π factorization). D in a rate formula is the set of sites where ket and bra differ, the price-pair page's pattern D, not the transpose D of the Π factorization; and "correlated ZZ-dephasing" in the F81 to F84 line means the single Pauli jump Z_kZ_l, not the correlated fields here (Quantum Sonar's "correlated ZZ", by contrast, is a shared bath of this page's kind). A decay rate is a positive amount, an eigenvalue's real part its negative (γ_k and what is built from it, γ_k − κ and (1 − η)γ, are Lindblad coefficients, called rates as on the singlet page; under local noise a coherence pays twice them per disagreeing site); two spectra pair about a centre −c when each eigenvalue λ of one has the partner −λ − 2c in the other (complex values, with multiplicity), the rates of a pair then summing to 2c, the pair sum, for a real centre; when only the real parts match that way, the rates pair. Δ is the anisotropy of the XXZ chain J Σ_bonds (XX + YY + ΔZZ).

## What this is about

The palindrome pairs every decay rate with a partner so that the two add up to one fixed total, 2σ. It is one number because each site's noise is its own: what a coherence pays at a site and what its mirror image pays there add up to that site's full share, whichever coherence it is.

Let the sites' Z dephasing share its weather. Then a coherence pays not only for where its ket and bra differ but for how those differences line up: the shared part of the field cannot see two differences that point opposite ways (|01⟩⟨10| is invisible to it) and sees two that point the same way twice over (|00⟩⟨11|). The mirror image's bill no longer completes the original's to the same total; the total now depends on which configurations the ket and the bra are in. For a real Hamiltonian that commutes with flipping every spin, the price stops being a number and becomes an operator, the anticommutator with the noise's own decay operator K = Σ c†c. And whenever the covariances of a valid generator (a positive semidefinite Kossakowski matrix) sum to a positive number, the single mirror for the whole spectrum breaks, for every Hamiltonian that keeps the all-0 and all-1 configurations as eigenstates (as every excitation-conserving one does); at the negative correlation η = −1/5 it breaks at N = 2 and 3 on the open XXZ chain, where it was read.

The palindrome does not simply die there. A field shared alike by every pair of sites splits each coherence's bill into the local one at the shifted rates γ_k − κ and an extra amount that is the same on the whole block (p, q) and zero where ket and bra hold equally many excitations. Under a Hamiltonian that keeps the blocks apart (it conserves the number of excitations), every state of fixed excitation number therefore evolves, at every N, as under independent dephasing at the rate (1 − η)γ when every site dephases at γ (with unequal rates, at the shifted rates γ_k − κ, a formula rather than a physical generator where one of them is negative).

For such a Hamiltonian that also commutes with flipping every spin (the XXZ chain is one), the mirror survives between each block and its image, each pair about its own centre, and for positive correlation the centres lie deeper the farther the block sits from the half-filling point.

The mirror carries two ladders on the block lattice onto each other, and on the open XY chain any correlation tells them apart: the one that adds an excitation to ket and bra together does not notice it, the one that moves an excitation from bra to ket does, though under one common covariance only by shifting the eigenvalues along its chains.

A uniform longitudinal field h·Σ_k Z_k, added to such a Hamiltonian, moves the centre of each block pair off the real axis by h(2p − N), p the ket popcount of the block (p, q) whose image (q, N − p) is its partner; the real parts stay paired (about −σ(p, q), and under local noise about the one −σ), while at odd N no block keeps its pairing with its image about the real centre. That is the block-level side of the Mirror Symmetry Proof's observation at N = 3, for the Heisenberg chain and field of its script, that a uniform field keeps the rates paired while the complex eigenvalues lose their partners (its own count, 0/64).

For a covariance pattern that is not one number the price identity still holds, and so does the break of the single mirror when the covariances sum to a positive number; for a Hamiltonian that conserves the excitations and commutes with flipping every spin, a block still pairs with its image where the decay operator takes one value on each of its two excitation numbers. On one excitation K takes one value exactly when every site carries the same summed covariance: at N = 3 only the common covariance does, at N = 4, for example, two disjoint shared pairs or a ring of shared neighbours also do, and in the thirteen patterns checked on the XXZ chain no block outside the qualifying ones pairs with its image.

## Abstract

Z dephasing with a real symmetric Kossakowski matrix a (diagonal for local dephasing), D_a(ρ) = Σ_kl a_kl (Z_k ρ Z_l − ½{Z_l Z_k, ρ}), and K = Σ_kl a_kl Z_l Z_k, which is Σ_μ c_μ†c_μ for any jumps c_μ realising a ⪰ 0. For every real H with [H, X^⊗N] = 0 the canonical palindromizer Π_Z(ρ) = ρᵀ·X^⊗N gives exactly

  Π_Z L Π_Z⁻¹ = −L − {K, ·},

which is F1 when K = σ·1, that is when a is diagonal; for a complex H commuting with X^⊗N, multiplying by X^⊗N on one side gives −L† − {K, ·}, and with ρ ↦ ρ† the same spectral statements, Jordan structure included. On a coherence |i⟩⟨j| the rate is ½uᵀa u with u = z(i) − z(j) (z_k(i) = ±1 the Z value of site k in basis state i); Π_Z carries u to v = z(i) + z(j) (and v to −u), and the two rates sum to z(i)ᵀa z(i) + z(j)ᵀa z(j): constant for local noise, a classical Ising energy of the two configurations otherwise. The dissipator residual is M_D = −{K − σ, ·}, ‖M_D‖²_F = 8·4^N·Σ_{k<l} a_kl². With one covariance κ shared by every pair (a_kk = γ_k, a_kl = κ) and H also popcount-conserving, the generator on every block (p, q) is the local one at the rates γ_k − κ minus 2κd², entry by entry; every diagonal block (p, p), the singlet's (1, 1) among them, runs exactly as under the local generator at those rates; and every block pairs with its image (q, N − p) about −σ(p, q), σ(p, q) = σ − Nκ + κ(d² + m²) (at uniform rates γ and correlation η, N(1 − η)γ + γη(d² + m²)). The one centre F137 allows for the whole spectrum, −σ, fails whenever the covariances have a positive sum, at every N (for a ⪰ 0 and an H keeping the all-0 and all-1 configurations as eigenstates), since the GHZ coherence's partner would have to grow; at N = 2 and 3 it fails for every η ≠ 0 checked. For any covariance pattern and a popcount-conserving, X^⊗N-even H, a block (p, q) pairs with its image about −(K_p + K_q)/2 when K takes one value K_p on the configurations of popcount p and one value K_q on those of popcount q; in the thirteen patterns checked at N = 3 and 4 these are exactly the blocks that pair. A uniform field h·Σ_k Z_k adds 2ih(2p − N) to the sum of paired eigenvalues of block (p, q) and its image. Π_Z carries the spectator ladder W onto the sideways ladder S⁺, an operator identity at every N; on the open XY chain W commutes with the generator for every real symmetric a, S⁺ for diagonal ones and for no correlated one; under one common covariance S⁺ still carries eigenvectors to eigenvectors, [L, S⁺] = −8κ(d + 1)·S⁺ on the source block. The identities are checked in exact integer arithmetic at N = 2..5 (the ladder identities at N = 3..5); the blockwise pairing is checked a second time through characteristic polynomials over ℚ(i) at N = 3 and 4, a route that never uses Π_Z.

## The model

Each site k dephases along Z, Markovian (in the white-noise limit, unlike the quasi-static noise a device also carries); the Kossakowski matrix a carries the site rates γ_k = a_kk on its diagonal and the field covariances off it, real and symmetric for classical fields, and positive semidefinite for a valid generator (the identities below need a real symmetric a, not its positivity). A field shared alike by every pair has one covariance κ = a_kl for all k ≠ l. At uniform rates this is a_kk = γ, a_kl = γη, admissible for −1/(N − 1) ≤ η ≤ 1; at η = 0 it is F1's setting, at η = 1 the single collective jump √γ·Σ_k Z_k of the Absorption Theorem's §2, and for N = 2 the singlet page's z-axis model. The Hamiltonian commutes with X^⊗N. The Π_Z similarity itself also needs H real in the computational basis; the spectral pairing, Jordan structure included, does not, and the block statements also need H to conserve the popcount. ## The price identity

Π_Z maps a matrix unit to a matrix unit, |i⟩⟨j| ↦ |j⟩⟨ī|, ī the complement. Its transpose leg turns −i[H, ·] into +i[H, ·] for real H, and its one-sided leg X^⊗N commutes with H; it is [The Pairing Condition](THE_PAIRING_CONDITION.md)'s U = X^⊗N, which anticommutes with every Z_k and so with every jump built linearly from them, multiplied on the right where that page multiplies on the left. The dissipator is diagonal on matrix units, with entry −½uᵀa u, u = z(i) − z(j) (z_k = ±1 the Z value of site k). On the image the vector becomes z(j) − z(ī) = z(i) + z(j) = v, so a coherence and its image pay ½uᵀa u and ½vᵀa v, whose sum is, by the parallelogram law,

  ½uᵀa u + ½vᵀa v = z(i)ᵀa z(i) + z(j)ᵀa z(j).

Since z(ī) = −z(i) leaves zᵀa z unchanged, this sum is the value of {K, ·} both on |i⟩⟨j| and on its image (K(i) = z(i)ᵀa z(i) on basis state i). So Π_Z L_D Π_Z⁻¹ + L_D is diagonal with entry minus that sum, which is −{K, ·}, and the Hamiltonian parts cancel: Π_Z L Π_Z⁻¹ = −L − {K, ·}, for every real symmetric a, with no assumption on the sign of its entries. Read through The Pairing Condition's one-sided multiplication ℒ_U(ρ) = Uρ, which sends block (p, q) to (N − p, q), the same computation gives ℒ_U L ℒ_U⁻¹ = −L† − {K, ·}: ℒ_U L_D ℒ_U⁻¹ and −L_D† share the sandwich term −Σ_kl a_kl Z_k ρ Z_l and differ in the anticommutator, −½{K, ·} against +½{K, ·}, a mismatch that is the number −2σ only when K = σ·1. With ρ ↦ ρ†, an antilinear symmetry of L that carries each block (p, q) onto (q, p) and conjugates its spectrum, the one-sided form gives the same blockwise pairing as Π_Z (the blocks of the sections below), Jordan structure included, for any popcount-conserving H commuting with X^⊗N, complex H included (the F1² paragraph's ρ ↦ ρ†·X^⊗N, and The Pairing Condition's "no condition on H"); only the Π_Z similarity onto −L − {K, ·} itself needs H real.

For a ±1 vector, zᵀa z = Σ_k a_kk + Σ_{k≠l} a_kl z_k z_l. Local noise has only the first term, the same for every configuration: that is why F1's price is one number. A correlation adds the second, the energy of the configuration in a classical Ising model whose couplings are the field covariances. Two fences belong here. For jumps built linearly from the Z_k with a real a, K is a number exactly when a is diagonal (for a Hermitian a, exactly when its real part is diagonal); other jumps can have a number K without the Z_k structure (multi-site Pauli strings square to the identity), and there The Pairing Condition's criterion decides with a U of its own, since Π_Z need not reflect them. And the identity needs a real a: the Absorption Theorem's pair √α(Z₀ + iZ₁), √β(Z₀ − iZ₁) with α ≠ β (the proof's a and b, renamed here because a is the Kossakowski matrix) has K = 2(α + β)·1, a number, and still breaks the identity (this pair's spectrum is not read here), because Π_Z maps the imaginary part of its Kossakowski matrix to itself, so that part doubles instead of cancelling (checked).

The price-pair page's hardware law is the sum above on the coherences that flight reads. A coherence aligned on a pattern D, all its disagreements pointing one way, such as |0…0⟩⟨1_D 0_D̄|, has as its Π_Z image the coherence aligned on the complement D̄, and for that pair the parallelogram gives 2σ + 4·Σ a_kl over the pairs k < l on the same side of the cut. In that page's book, whose γ_j is the decay rate of a single-site coherence (2γ_j here), P = Σ_j γ_j is 2σ and Cov_kl = 2a_kl (its tables' c_ij; 2ηγ for the shared field), and this is its P + 2·Σ Cov·[same side]. The residual of the dissipator, measured against F1's shift,

is that Ising term alone: it does not depend on H, and it adds to the Hamiltonian's residual M_H = Π_Z L_H Π_Z⁻¹ + L_H in squares, ‖M‖² = ‖M_H‖² + ‖M_D‖² for every Hermitian H, because M_D is real and diagonal on matrix units while the diagonal of M_H is imaginary (checked with the imaginary bond 3Y₀Z₁ at N = 2..5), so the residual-norm readings of F87 carry an H-independent floor under correlation. ## The shared field, block by block

Every coherence of block (p, q) has Σ_k u_k = −2d, so with a_kk = γ_k and a_kl = κ the rate of |i⟩⟨j| is

  2Σ_{k∈D}(γ_k − κ) + 2κd²,   D the set of sites where i and j differ,

at uniform rates 2γ[(1 − η)|D| + ηd²]. From the definition, K = σ − Nκ + 4κ·S_z², S_z = ½Σ_k Z_k: the decay operator of a shared field is a function of the total magnetization, its anticommutator giving the summed bill of a coherence and its image, 2(σ − Nκ) + 4κ(S_z(i)² + S_z(j)²) on |i⟩⟨j| (S_z(i) the value of S_z on basis state i), the squares on the two sides, while the rate of |i⟩⟨j| itself carries the square of their difference, 2κd². The extra term 2κd² depends on the block alone. For a popcount-conserving H nothing leaves a block, and on block (p, q)

  L = L_local(γ_k − κ) − 2κd² · 1,

entry by entry. On a diagonal block (p, p) the shift is zero: every state of fixed popcount, pure or mixed, evolves exactly as under the local generator at the rates γ_k − κ, at every N. At uniform rates that rate is (1 − η)γ, the rate in the singlet page's dose under z-axis noise, for every diagonal block, lower than γ for a positive correlation and higher for a negative one; where γ_k − κ is negative, which a positive semidefinite a allows when the rates are unequal, it is the formula, not a physical local generator. Where the rates γ_k − κ vanish (η = 1) these blocks are unitary: the fixed-popcount subspaces are the decoherence-free subspaces of collective dephasing, in the literature's name ([Structural Cartography](STRUCTURAL_CARTOGRAPHY.md) points to that literature). Off the diagonal, the shift 2κd² is what the collective law 2γ(Δpopcount)² becomes at a partial correlation. At the anti-correlated end of two sites, η = −1, the rate of |00⟩⟨11| is 2γ[2·2 − 4] = 0: Bell+ is dark to Z₀ − Z₁, the decoherence-free pair of [Monotonicity](../docs/proofs/PROOF_MONOTONICITY_CPSI.md) Test B.

## The two ladders

The block lattice carries two ladders ([the arc `sideways_spin_ladder`](../compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/OpenArcs/OpenArcsRegistry.cs), `SidewaysSpinLadderClaim`, [PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4](../docs/proofs/PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4.md)). With Jordan-Wigner fermions c_l (the SO(4) proof writes them d_l, and its Φ is the W here), the spectator W(ρ) = Σ_l c_l†ρ c_l moves (p, q) to (p + 1, q + 1); the sideways S⁺(ρ) = Σ_l (−1)^l c_l†ρ c_l†, sites counted from l = 0, moves it to (p + 1, q − 1). On the open XY chain both commute with the generator under any local rates (the SO(4) proof's Lemmas 2.1 to 2.3; S⁺ also needs the hopping to connect only sites of opposite parity, which the open chain does). Π_Z carries one onto the other: since c_lᵀ = c_l† and X^⊗N c_l X^⊗N = (−1)^l c_l†, Π_Z W Π_Z⁻¹ = S⁺ at every N (checked at N = 3..5; with sites counted from 1, as the SO(4) proof counts them, the image is −S⁺). The identity is the bra flip's alone: the transpose leaves W fixed, and R(ρ) = ρ·X^⊗N, a particle-hole transformation of the bra species, carries W onto S⁺ (both checked), the staggered map named above, not the SO(4) proof's; the F89 Path-k walk reads the same map at block level. Under a correlated field they part:

- W commutes with the generator for every real symmetric a. It fills a site where ket and bra agree on empty and leaves them agreeing on full, so u does not change, and no correlation pattern can see the step; the staircase flight design holds this at the level of one cell. The verifier shows on the Heisenberg chain that W's commutation is not automatic: that chain is not quadratic in the fermions and so lies outside F125's spectator intertwiner (Theorem B of [PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY](../docs/proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md)): W fails there already at η = 0.
- S⁺ commutes for every diagonal a and for no correlated one. Conjugating [W, L] = 0 by Π_Z and using the price identity gives [S⁺, L] = −[S⁺, {K, ·}] (checked), and since S⁺ commutes with the rest of L, [S⁺, L] = [S⁺, L_off], L_off the dissipator of the off-diagonal part of a. It fills the ket and empties the bra at one site, so it keeps the disagreement set, which is all local noise reads, and flips the sign of one entry of u, which any nonzero covariance reads: on a coherence that disagrees at l (ket empty, bra full) and at k and agrees elsewhere, the S⁺ step at l lands on a matrix unit no other step reaches, and its rate changes by 8|a_lk| ≠ 0 (the commutator's nonvanishing checked on random a, the shared field and a single small covariance between the two end sites). Under one covariance common to every pair the failure is only a shift: K is one number on each popcount, so [L, S⁺] = −8κ(d + 1)·S⁺ on the source block (p, q), and S⁺ still carries each eigenvector of L to an eigenvector or to zero, the eigenvalue shifted by −8κ(d + 1), the chains of S⁺ being those of the local generator at the rates γ_k − κ (checked at N = 3..5); for a random covariance pattern [L, S⁺] is no such shift (checked).

In the SO(4) proof's Hubbard reading (Medvedyeva, Essler and Prosen's η-pairing in dissipative fermions, in the repository's literature review), with ket and bra as the two species and the local spin s^z_k = ½(n_k^ket − n_k^bra) (not the S_z above), u_k = −4s^z_k and the dissipator is −8·Σ_kl a_kl s^z_k s^z_l. Its diagonal part is −8·Σ_k a_kk (s^z_k)², and (s^z_k)² is a third of the local spin's square, untouched by either SU(2); the off-diagonal part is an Ising exchange between the sites' spins, which the spin SU(2) does not leave invariant (so S⁺ fails to commute) and which commutes with the η-pairing ladder (so W survives): the split read in the symmetry's own terms. For one common covariance it is local terms plus −8κ(Σ_k s^z_k)², with Σ_k s^z_k = d/2, a function of the spin Cartan alone, which lowers the spin SU(2) to its U(1): the multiplets' eigenvalues split while S⁺ still steps along them, the shift above. So the parenthesis in `SidewaysSpinLadderClaim`, "the ladder never moves the disagreement set", is exactly the scope of S⁺'s immunity: the set, not its signs.

One consequence reaches back into the arc. Π_Z is a permutation of matrix units, so it preserves norms, and at local rates on the open XY chain it carries each eigenvector of L to an eigenvector of L (at −λ − 2σ); since Π_Z⁻¹ S⁺ Π_Z = W, Π_Z⁻¹ therefore carries every chain of eigenvectors that S⁺ generates onto a chain of W with the same transport norms (the norms ‖S⁺v‖ of the steps along the chain), ‖W·Π_Z⁻¹x‖ = ‖S⁺x‖, and the inverse quarter turn moves the lattice's spin lines m = ±1 onto its η lines d = ±1. `SidewaysSpinLadderClaim` records the S⁺ chains as measured and gated at N = 4, 5, 7 and 9, their transport norms the Clebsch-Gordan coefficients √(ℓ(ℓ + 1) − μ(μ + 1)) (ℓ = (N − 3)/2 the chains' spin, μ the weight), and the η chains' norms as untested. By this identity every S⁺ chain on m = ±1 has an image, a W chain on d = ±1 at the mirrored eigenvalue, with the same norms; whether that image is the arc's η chain on its line turns on a multiplicity, below.

The arc's two η chains lie in the band half of the F125 orbit at odd N (the arc's band and fold blocks, whose band half is the blocks with |d| = 1), one multiplet on each line d = ±1; the measured S⁺ chains are its fold half (its blocks on m = ±1). The records place the two halves at mirrored values: the claim names the F125 fold family "exactly the interiors of the two S⁺ chains", [PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY](../docs/proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md) Theorem B and §7 carry the band sectors at λ and the fold at its partner, and the arc records band and fold as one orbit under λ ↦ −λ − 2N, the holomorphic fold that PROOF_CODIM1 §7 derives (in its units γ = 1, which is Π_Z's −λ − 2σ; Theorem B's partner is −λ̄ − 2N, the same on the real axis). Those records identify blocks and eigenvalues, not eigenvectors. Where the η multiplet is L's only multiplet on its line d = ±1 at that eigenvalue, Π_Z⁻¹ of a measured S⁺ chain is the arc's η chain, and the η norms are the measured Clebsch-Gordan values at the odd N where the S⁺ chains were measured (5, 7 and 9). That multiplicity we did not check, so the η chains on d = ±1 stay unmeasured: once it holds, the identity reduces their norms to the S⁺ ones. The F89 Path-k walk measured the η-side norms on the d = −2 chain.

## What this does and does not say

It says that, for jumps built linearly from the Z_k with a real Kossakowski matrix and an H commuting with X^⊗N (real, for the similarity), the palindrome's price is the anticommutator with the decay operator Σ c†c, a number exactly when the matrix is diagonal, and that for a field shared alike by all pairs and a popcount-conserving H commuting with X^⊗N the mirror survives between each block and its image about centres set by the block's distance from the half-filling point, with a uniform longitudinal field adding an imaginary part. The identities are proved above for every N, and so is the global break for a positive summed covariance (for a ⪰ 0 and an H keeping the all-0 and all-1 configurations as eigenstates); the exact checks run at N = 2..5 for the identities and the block law at uniform rates, N = 3..5 for the block law at site-dependent rates and for the ladders, N = 3 and 4 for the characteristic polynomials of blocks, and N = 2 and 3 for the whole spectrum.

It covers Z dephasing only. A shared field along a single other axis is the z case in a turned basis, so the block statements hold for an H that conserves the popcount and commutes with the global flip in that basis (the Heisenberg chain for every axis), and not for the z-anisotropic XXZ chain under a shared x or y noise field, which breaks that grading; a field shared along several axes at once, the singlet page's three-axis case, breaks that grading in any case, and none of the block statements apply there. The block law is written for one covariance common to every pair; for a general covariance pattern the identity still holds, the global palindrome breaks whenever the covariances sum to a positive number, and a block pairs with its image where K takes one value on each of its two popcounts, which in the thirteen patterns checked are exactly the blocks that pair. It does not say how large the covariances are on any device. The price-pair campaign read them in three of its four runs: run 1 flagged one at 2.2 standard errors, run 2 found c₀₂ at 5.1 standard errors on the outer pair, which did not recur and stays unexplained, and run 3 read all three local; the price-pair page's verdict reads "No *persistent* correlated bath is required by the clean run-3 session". Nor can the [F81 hardware bridge](F81_VIOLATION_HARDWARE_BRIDGE.md)'s scalar f81_violation see the covariances: it reads the Π²-odd part of the noise, which correlated Z dephasing does not have. The conversion c_ij = 2ηγ is the singlet page's: it is Cov = 2a of the price-pair book (above) for the shared field. The rung-ladder page's c_ij, twice its Lindblad coefficient, is the same book. The conversion is a change of book, not a measurement of η on any device. On the ladders this page reduces the η chains' transport norms on d = ±1 to the measured S⁺ ones only where the η multiplet is the only one on its line at that eigenvalue, which was not checked. And it does not touch F158's criterion, whose class needs jumps that square to a multiple of the identity: a jump in the span of the Z_k (real coefficients) does so only when it is a multiple of a single Z_k, so no jump set of a non-diagonal Kossakowski matrix is in it.
