## What this is about

The palindrome pairs every decay rate with a partner so that the two add up to one fixed total, 2σ. It is one number because each site's noise is its own: what a coherence pays at a site and what its mirror image pays there add up to that site's full share, whichever coherence it is.

Let the sites' Z dephasing share its weather. Then a coherence pays not only for where its ket and bra differ but for how those differences line up: the shared part of the field cannot see two differences that point opposite ways (|01⟩⟨10| is invisible to it) and sees two that point the same way twice over (|00⟩⟨11|). The mirror image's bill no longer completes the original's to the same total; the total now depends on which configurations the ket and the bra are in. For a real Hamiltonian that commutes with flipping every spin, the price stops being a number and becomes an operator, the anticommutator with the noise's own decay operator K = Σ c†c.

The palindrome does not simply die under a shared field. A field shared alike by every pair of sites splits each coherence's bill into the local one at the shifted rates γ_k − κ and an extra amount that is the same on the whole block (p, q) and zero where ket and bra hold equally many excitations. Under a Hamiltonian that keeps the blocks apart (it conserves the number of excitations), every state of fixed excitation number therefore evolves, at every N, as under independent dephasing at the rate (1 − η)γ when every site dephases at γ (with unequal rates, at the shifted rates γ_k − κ, a formula rather than a physical generator where one of them is negative).

For such a Hamiltonian that also commutes with flipping every spin (the XXZ chain is one), the mirror survives between each block and its image, each pair about its own centre, and for positive correlation the centres lie deeper the farther the block sits from the half-filling point.

For a covariance pattern that is not one number and a Hamiltonian that conserves the excitations and commutes with flipping every spin, a block still pairs with its image where the decay operator takes one value on each of its two excitation numbers. Which excitation numbers those are is settled for every pattern: the empty and the full one always, one excitation and one hole exactly when every site carries the same summed covariance, half filling (at even N) exactly when the covariance of two sites is a sum of one value at each, and every other excitation number exactly under one common covariance; on the excitation numbers that qualify the decay operator takes the shared field's values at the mean covariance, and in the thirteen patterns checked on the XXZ chain no block outside the qualifying ones pairs with its image.

Whenever the covariances of a valid generator (a positive semidefinite Kossakowski matrix) sum to a positive number, the single mirror for the whole spectrum breaks, for every Hamiltonian that keeps the all-0 and all-1 configurations as eigenstates (as every excitation-conserving one does): the GHZ coherence then decays faster than the mirror allows. For a valid field and an excitation-conserving Hamiltonian that commutes with flipping every spin it also breaks when the decay operator takes one value above its average on some excitation number, the populations there with every bra spin flipped then decaying too fast; under a field shared alike by every pair that happens for either sign of the correlation, at every N, in the middle excitation numbers for a negative one. A mode that decays too fast rules out the pairing of the decay rates as well as that of the complex eigenvalues. It breaks at weak enough coupling for every pattern with a covariance and every Hamiltonian, the mode that decays too fast continued from the coherence between the configuration the noise charges most and its full flip; and its complex eigenvalues lose their pairing, for a field with a covariance and a Hamiltonian that commutes with flipping every spin, when the covariances, each weighted by the two-site ZZ content of the squared Hamiltonian (the Hamiltonian made traceless), sum to zero or less. On the open XY chain that weight is negative on the bonds, so there the condition is that the covariances on the bonds sum to zero or more. The reason is plain: the spectrum's third moment about −σ, which a pairing would make zero, gets from the noise an amount of one sign whenever the field has a covariance, and the pairing breaks at least wherever the part the Hamiltonian contributes through the covariances does not push the other way. Where neither a mode that decays too fast nor weak coupling decides, whether the decay rates pair stays open.

A uniform longitudinal field h·Σ_k Z_k, added to such a Hamiltonian, moves the centre of each block pair off the real axis by h(2p − N), p the ket popcount of the block (p, q) whose image (q, N − p) is its partner; the real parts stay paired (about −σ(p, q), and under local noise about the one −σ), while at odd N no block keeps its pairing with its image about the real centre. That is the block-level side of the Mirror Symmetry Proof's observation at N = 3, for the Heisenberg chain and field of its script, that a uniform field keeps the rates paired while the complex eigenvalues lose their partners (its own count, 0/64).

The mirror carries two ladders on the block lattice onto each other, and on the open XY chain any correlation tells them apart: the one that adds an excitation to ket and bra together does not notice it, the one that moves an excitation from bra to ket does, though under one common covariance only by shifting the eigenvalues along its chains.

## Abstract

Z dephasing with a real symmetric Kossakowski matrix a (diagonal for local dephasing), D_a(ρ) = Σ_kl a_kl (Z_k ρ Z_l − ½{Z_l Z_k, ρ}), and K = Σ_kl a_kl Z_l Z_k, which is Σ_μ c_μ†c_μ for any jumps c_μ realising a ⪰ 0. For every real H with [H, X^⊗N] = 0 the canonical palindromizer Π_Z(ρ) = ρᵀ·X^⊗N gives exactly

  Π_Z L Π_Z⁻¹ = −L − {K, ·},

which is F1 when K = σ·1, that is when a is diagonal; for a complex H commuting with X^⊗N, multiplying by X^⊗N on one side gives −L† − {K, ·}, and with ρ ↦ ρ† the same spectral statements, Jordan structure included. On a coherence |i⟩⟨j| the rate is ½uᵀa u with u = z(i) − z(j) (z_k(i) = ±1 the Z value of site k in basis state i); Π_Z carries u to v = z(i) + z(j) (and v to −u), and the two rates sum to z(i)ᵀa z(i) + z(j)ᵀa z(j): constant for local noise, a classical Ising energy of the two configurations otherwise. For a ⪰ 0 a cell is dark, commuting with every jump, exactly when au = 0, and the GHZ coherence |0…0⟩⟨1…1| exactly when every row of a sums to zero. The dissipator residual is M_D = −{K − σ, ·}, ‖M_D‖²_F = 8·4^N·Σ_{k<l} a_kl². With one covariance κ shared by every pair (a_kk = γ_k, a_kl = κ) and H also popcount-conserving, the generator on every block (p, q) is the local one at the rates γ_k − κ minus 2κd², entry by entry; every diagonal block (p, p), the singlet's (1, 1) among them, runs exactly as under the local generator at those rates; and every block pairs with its image (q, N − p) about −σ(p, q), σ(p, q) = σ − Nκ + κ(d² + m²) (at uniform rates γ and correlation η, N(1 − η)γ + γη(d² + m²)).

For any covariance pattern and a popcount-conserving, X^⊗N-even H, a block (p, q) pairs with its image about −(K_p + K_q)/2 when K takes one value K_p on the configurations of popcount p and one value K_q on those of popcount q. Which popcounts these are is settled for every pattern: 0 and N always, 1 and N − 1 exactly when every site carries the same summed covariance, N/2 at even N exactly when the pattern is additive, a_kl = y_k + y_l for k ≠ l, and every other popcount exactly under one common covariance, so the qualifying blocks, those whose two popcounts both see one value of K, number 4, 16, 9 or (N + 1)², and on them K takes the shared field's values at the mean covariance; in the thirteen patterns checked at N = 3 and 4 these are exactly the blocks that pair.

The one centre F137 allows for the whole spectrum, −σ, fails whenever the covariances have a positive sum, at every N (for a ⪰ 0 and an H keeping the all-0 and all-1 configurations as eigenstates), since the GHZ coherence's partner would have to grow, the rates included; for a ⪰ 0 and a popcount-conserving X^⊗N-even H it fails, the rates included as well, wherever K takes one value K_p > σ on some popcount p, since P_p·X^⊗N (P_p the projector onto popcount p) is then an eigenvector at −2K_p, and under one common covariance that is every allowed κ ≠ 0 at every N (p = ⌊N/2⌋ for κ < 0). The rates fail to pair too, for a ⪰ 0 with a covariance and H = J·H₀, at weak enough J, where an eigenvalue lies below −2σ (at J = 0 that of the cell |i⟩⟨ī| of a configuration with the largest K). For every real symmetric a = Λ + C (Λ the diagonal, C the covariances) and every traceless Hermitian H, Tr((L + σ)³) = −4^N·[4·tr(C·(2a + Λ)·C) − 3·tr(CΘ) − 6·ξᵀa ξ], with Tr over the 4^N cells for a map on the coherences such as L and over the 2^N basis states for an operator such as H²Z_kZ_l, tr that of an N × N matrix, Θ_kl = Tr(H²Z_kZ_l)/2^N and ξ_k = Tr(H Z_k)/2^N. For the shared field at uniform rates on the open XY and Heisenberg chains the third and fifth moments together reach the verdict of the P_p·X^⊗N eigenvectors for the complex eigenvalues by a second route at N = 2..7.

A uniform field h·Σ_k Z_k adds 2ih(2p − N) to the sum of paired eigenvalues of block (p, q) and its image. ## The model

Each site k dephases along Z, Markovian (in the white-noise limit, unlike the quasi-static noise a device also carries); the Kossakowski matrix a carries the site rates γ_k = a_kk on its diagonal and the field covariances off it, real and symmetric for classical fields, and positive semidefinite for a valid generator (the identities below need a real symmetric a, not its positivity). A field shared alike by every pair has one covariance κ = a_kl for all k ≠ l. At uniform rates this is a_kk = γ, a_kl = γη, admissible for −1/(N − 1) ≤ η ≤ 1; at η = 0 it is F1's setting, at η = 1 the single collective jump √γ·Σ_k Z_k of the Absorption Theorem's §2, and for N = 2 the singlet page's z-axis model. The Hamiltonian commutes with X^⊗N. The Π_Z similarity itself also needs H real in the computational basis; the spectral pairing, Jordan structure included, does not, and the block statements also need H to conserve the popcount. ## The price identity

Π_Z maps a matrix unit to a matrix unit, |i⟩⟨j| ↦ |j⟩⟨ī|, ī the complement. Its transpose leg turns −i[H, ·] into +i[H, ·] for real H, and its one-sided leg X^⊗N commutes with H; it is [The Pairing Condition](THE_PAIRING_CONDITION.md)'s U = X^⊗N, which anticommutes with every Z_k and so with every jump built linearly from them, multiplied on the right where that page multiplies on the left. The dissipator is diagonal on matrix units, with entry −½uᵀa u, u = z(i) − z(j) (z_k = ±1 the Z value of site k). On the image the vector becomes z(j) − z(ī) = z(i) + z(j) = v, so a coherence and its image pay ½uᵀa u and ½vᵀa v, whose sum is, by the parallelogram law,

  ½uᵀa u + ½vᵀa v = z(i)ᵀa z(i) + z(j)ᵀa z(j).

Since z(ī) = −z(i) leaves zᵀa z unchanged, this sum is the value of {K, ·} both on |i⟩⟨j| and on its image (K(i) = z(i)ᵀa z(i) on basis state i). So Π_Z L_D Π_Z⁻¹ + L_D is diagonal with entry minus that sum, which is −{K, ·}, and the Hamiltonian parts cancel: Π_Z L Π_Z⁻¹ = −L − {K, ·}, for every real symmetric a, with no assumption on the sign of its entries. Read through The Pairing Condition's one-sided multiplication ℒ_U(ρ) = Uρ, which sends block (p, q) to (N − p, q), the same computation gives ℒ_U L ℒ_U⁻¹ = −L† − {K, ·}: ℒ_U L_D ℒ_U⁻¹ and −L_D† share the sandwich term −Σ_kl a_kl Z_k ρ Z_l and differ in the anticommutator, −½{K, ·} against +½{K, ·}, a mismatch that is the number −2σ only when K = σ·1. With ρ ↦ ρ†, an antilinear symmetry of L that carries each block (p, q) onto (q, p) and conjugates its spectrum, the one-sided form gives the same blockwise pairing as Π_Z (the blocks of the sections below), Jordan structure included, for any popcount-conserving H commuting with X^⊗N, complex H included (the F1² paragraph's ρ ↦ ρ†·X^⊗N, and The Pairing Condition's "no condition on H"); only the Π_Z similarity onto −L − {K, ·} itself needs H real.

For a ±1 vector, zᵀa z = Σ_k a_kk + Σ_{k≠l} a_kl z_k z_l. Local noise has only the first term, the same for every configuration: that is why F1's price is one number. A correlation adds the second, the energy of the configuration in a classical Ising model whose couplings are the field covariances. Two fences belong here. For jumps built linearly from the Z_k with a real a, K is a number exactly when a is diagonal (for a Hermitian a, exactly when its real part is diagonal); other jumps can have a number K without the Z_k structure (multi-site Pauli strings square to the identity), and there The Pairing Condition's criterion decides with a U of its own, since Π_Z need not reflect them. And the identity needs a real a: the Absorption Theorem's pair √α(Z₀ + iZ₁), √β(Z₀ − iZ₁) with α ≠ β (the proof's a and b, renamed here because a is the Kossakowski matrix) has K = 2(α + β)·1, a number, and still breaks the identity (this pair's spectrum is not read here), because Π_Z maps the imaginary part of its Kossakowski matrix to itself, so that part doubles instead of cancelling (checked).

The price-pair page's hardware law is the sum above on the coherences that flight reads. A coherence aligned on a pattern D, all its disagreements pointing one way, such as |0…0⟩⟨1_D 0_D̄|, has as its Π_Z image the coherence aligned on the complement D̄, and for that pair the parallelogram gives 2σ + 4·Σ a_kl over the pairs k < l on the same side of the partition D|D̄. In that page's book, whose γ_j is the decay rate of a single-site coherence (2γ_j here), P = Σ_j γ_j is 2σ and Cov_kl = 2a_kl (its tables' c_ij; 2ηγ for the shared field), and this is its P + 2·Σ Cov·[same side]. There the price's invariance was the measurement, read as locality of the watching; here it is the vanishing of the off-diagonal a, for every coherence at once.

The residual of the dissipator, measured against F1's shift,

  M_D = Π_Z L_D Π_Z⁻¹ + L_D + 2σ = −{K − σ, ·},   ‖M_D‖²_F = 8·4^N·Σ_{k<l} a_kl²,

is that Ising term alone: it does not depend on H, and it adds to the Hamiltonian's residual M_H = Π_Z L_H Π_Z⁻¹ + L_H in squares, ‖M‖² = ‖M_H‖² + ‖M_D‖² for every Hermitian H, because M_D is real and diagonal on matrix units while the diagonal of M_H is imaginary (checked with the imaginary bond 3Y₀Z₁ at N = 2..5), so the residual-norm readings of F87 carry an H-independent floor under correlation. M_D vanishes exactly when a is diagonal, for the uniform shared field its squared norm is 4^(N+1)·N(N − 1)γ²η², and it is Π_Z-invariant (the invariance checked at N = 2..5), as F81's rule requires: jumps of one bit_b parity have no Π²-odd part, so in F82's Π·M·Π⁻¹ = M − 2L_{H_odd} − 2D_odd the correlated dissipator adds no term. ## The shared field, block by block

Every coherence of block (p, q) has Σ_k u_k = −2d, so with a_kk = γ_k and a_kl = κ the rate of |i⟩⟨j| is

  2Σ_{k∈D}(γ_k − κ) + 2κd²,   D the set of sites where i and j differ,

at uniform rates 2γ[(1 − η)|D| + ηd²]. From the definition, K = σ − Nκ + 4κ·S_z², S_z = ½Σ_k Z_k: the decay operator of a shared field is a function of the total magnetization, its anticommutator giving the summed bill of a coherence and its image, 2(σ − Nκ) + 4κ(S_z(i)² + S_z(j)²) on |i⟩⟨j| (S_z(i) the value of S_z on basis state i), the squares on the two sides, while the rate of |i⟩⟨j| itself carries the square of their difference, 2κd². The extra term 2κd² depends on the block alone. For a popcount-conserving H nothing leaves a block, and on block (p, q)

  L = L_local(γ_k − κ) − 2κd² · 1,

entry by entry. On a diagonal block (p, p) the shift is zero: every state of fixed popcount, pure or mixed, evolves exactly as under the local generator at the rates γ_k − κ, at every N. At uniform rates that rate is (1 − η)γ, the rate in the singlet page's dose under z-axis noise, for every diagonal block, lower than γ for a positive correlation and higher for a negative one; where γ_k − κ is negative, which a positive semidefinite a allows when the rates are unequal, it is the formula, not a physical local generator. Where the rates γ_k − κ vanish (η = 1) these blocks are unitary: the fixed-popcount subspaces are the decoherence-free subspaces of collective dephasing, in the literature's name ([Structural Cartography](STRUCTURAL_CARTOGRAPHY.md) points to that literature). Off the diagonal, the shift 2κd² is what the collective law 2γ(Δpopcount)² becomes at a partial correlation.

For two sites, at the anti-correlated end η = −1, the rate of |00⟩⟨11| is 2γ[2·2 − 4] = 0: Bell+ is dark to Z₀ − Z₁ (Test B's Z₁ − Z₂), the decoherence-free pair of [Monotonicity](../docs/proofs/PROOF_MONOTONICITY_CPSI.md) Test B. Behind it is a criterion for every a ⪰ 0 at every N: a cell |i⟩⟨j| is dark, commuting with every jump, exactly when au = 0, u = z(i) − z(j), and its rate ½uᵀa u vanishes exactly then (the decoherence-free condition of the literature Structural Cartography points to). For jumps c_μ = Σ_k b_μk Z_k realising a, a_kl = Σ_μ b_μk b_μl (for complex b one factor conjugated), so ½uᵀa u = ½Σ_μ|Σ_k b_μk u_k|², while c_μ|i⟩⟨j| − |i⟩⟨j|c_μ = (Σ_k b_μk u_k)|i⟩⟨j|: the rate vanishes exactly when every Σ_k b_μk u_k does, which is commuting with every jump, and that is au = 0. For an indefinite a, which no jumps realise, a rate can vanish off the kernel.

For a positive definite a (at uniform rates, every η strictly between −1/(N − 1) and 1) the dark cells are the 2^N populations alone, which span the diagonal operators, the Pauli strings in I and Z ({I, Z}^⊗N, the Glossary's lens letters); for local noise (a diagonal) this is Step 2 (a) of [the asymptotic sector projection](../docs/proofs/PROOF_ASYMPTOTIC_SECTOR_PROJECTION.md), there within one popcount sector. At η = 1, where a_kl = γ, the kernel is the vectors of zero sum, and since Σ_k u_k = −2d on block (p, q), the dark cells are every cell of every diagonal block (p, p), populations included, Σ_p C(N, p)² = C(2N, N) in all. At the anti-correlated end, η = −1/(N − 1), where a_kl = −γ/(N − 1) and every row of a sums to zero, a stops being positive definite and its kernel is the all-ones direction; u has entries 0 or ±2, so the only nonzero u on that line is ±2(1, …, 1): the ket is |0…0⟩ and the bra |1…1⟩, or the reverse. The dark cells are then the populations and the two GHZ cells |0…0⟩⟨1…1| and |1…1⟩⟨0…0|, the corner blocks (0, N) and (N, 0), 2^N + 2 in all: there this section's uniform-rate law 2γ[(1 − η)|D| + ηd²], with |D| = N and d² = N², gives the GHZ coherence 2γN[1 + (N − 1)η] = 0.

For any a ⪰ 0 the GHZ coherence pays 2·Σ_kl a_kl = 2σ + 4·Σ_{k<l} a_kl and is dark exactly when every row of a sums to zero, that is, when the sum of the site fields does not fluctuate; a field with one common covariance reaches that only at equal rates, at its anti-correlated end.

Read as a partition, the criterion sorts the configurations: i and j fall in one *dark class* when a z(i) = a z(j), configurations the noise cannot tell apart, so for a ⪰ 0 the dark cells are the cells inside the classes, Σ_c n_c² of them over the class sizes n_c, and every coherence among the configurations of one class commutes with every jump. At η = 1 the classes are the popcount sectors; at the anti-correlated end the one class larger than a single configuration is the GHZ pair, whose four cells are the operator block of [J-Blind Receiver Classes](J_BLIND_RECEIVER_CLASSES.md)' Class 2 example, there under local noise, where its coherence decays. Where a is a direct sum over disjoint sets of sites, the classes are products and the dark cells multiply: anti-correlated pairs, a = γ·[[1, −1], [−1, 1]] on each, leave (2² + 2)^(N/2) dark cells at even N, the largest class taking 00 or 11 on every pair, each pair's dark coherence the Bell+ coherence of Test B. On a ring of N ≥ 3 sites with one covariance κ between neighbours at γ = 2|κ|, a is |κ| times the ring's Laplacian D − A for κ < 0 and its signless Laplacian D + A for κ > 0, the pair [F152](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f152) names as cospectral on a bipartite graph. The first leaves the GHZ pair dark at every N; the second leaves the Néel pair 0101… and 1010… at even N, its kernel the alternating vector, the same N-vector as the staggered mode m = N/2 of [Ring Gap Dominance](../docs/proofs/PROOF_RING_GAP_DOMINANCE.md), there a single-excitation amplitude and here a direction of the site fields, and at odd N, where no alternating sign closes around the ring, nothing beyond the populations. Flipping every other site, X on the odd sites, carries the one covariance pattern and its class onto the other: on the configurations' Z values it acts as z ↦ Σz with Σ = diag((−1)^k), so on the covariances as a ↦ ΣaΣ, and Σ(D + A)Σ = D − A, which around a ring only an even N admits. [F157](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f157) uses the same N × N matrix on the Hamiltonian's side, Σ H(Δ) Σ = −H(−Δ), where it is the single-excitation face of a different operator, Z on the odd sites (the sublattice rotation of [The Spread Is a Resonance](THE_SPREAD_IS_A_RESONANCE.md)); the two operators, turns of the odd sites by π about x and about z, share the matrix, not the action. Both pairs are complementary configurations, so their cells |i⟩⟨ī| disagree at every site: local noise charges them the most, 2σ ([F22](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f22-ghz-maximum-disagreement-support-tier-2-verified-n2-5)), and a covariance that makes a singular along z(i) charges them nothing. Dark to the noise is the first of two conditions. To stay dark under the dynamics an operator must also stay inside the span of the dark cells, the second condition [The Blind Site](THE_BLIND_SITE.md) §6 adds ("requiring the trajectory to stay there adds H-invariance"). A class whose span the Hamiltonian keeps meets it whole; that is sufficient and not necessary, since sums across classes can meet it too, as the sector steady states there do. The open XY chain keeps the span of each popcount sector, which it conserves, its cells moving only among themselves, and leaves the GHZ cells unmoved, since it annihilates |0…0⟩ and |1…1⟩; its hopping carries the Néel cell out of the dark cells.

## The blockwise pairing

Π_Z carries block (p, q) onto its image (q, N − p), F1's own orbit (`F1PalindromeOrbitPairing`), and the price identity gives the centre at once: on the block, {K, ·} is the number K(i) + K(j) = 2(σ − Nκ) + κ[(N − 2p)² + (N − 2q)²] = 2σ(p, q) (K(i) = z(i)ᵀa z(i) the value of K on basis state i), since (N − 2p)² + (N − 2q)² = 2(d² + m²). The same follows from F1 at the rates γ_k − κ, spec_local(q, N − p) = −spec_local(p, q) − 2(σ − Nκ), with the block's extra −2κd² and its image's −2κm² (the image's charge d is the original's m). So the spectra of a block and its image pair about −σ(p, q),

  σ(p, q) = σ − Nκ + κ(d² + m²),   at uniform rates   N(1 − η)γ + γη(d² + m²),

spec(q, N − p) = −spec(p, q) − 2σ(p, q), Jordan structure included, for any popcount-conserving H commuting with X^⊗N: through Π_Z for a real H, through the one-sided form otherwise (the spectra checked with a complex X^⊗N-even H on the triangle at N = 3, whose single-excitation hopping carries a flux that no diagonal unitary removes, every block, exact characteristic polynomials; the Jordan structure follows from the antilinear similarity). The image map has order four, so each block pairs with its image and with its preimage. On the block lattice the image map is the quarter turn of [The One Square](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) (§11, the map in §3's table), (d, m) ↦ (m, −d), its square conjugation by X^⊗N, which every real symmetric a leaves a symmetry; the quarter turn exchanges the lines d = ±1, where the η multiplets sit, with m = ±1, where the spin multiplets sit, which is Π_Z W Π_Z⁻¹ = S⁺ below seen on the lattice.

The centre depends only on d² + m², twice the squared distance from the half-filling point, so it is the same for a block, its image and all eight images under the lattice's group of eight. For κ < 0 the pair centres move the other way: away from the half-filling point the centres −σ(p, q) rise toward zero.

The bra fold |i⟩⟨j| ↦ |i⟩⟨j̄|, taken antiunitarily as F89d takes it, is Π_Z composed with the dagger, ρ ↦ Π_Z(ρ†), so it is the same pairing read through ρ ↦ ρ†: it relates block (p, q) to (p, N − q), the image of (q, p), and gives spec(p, N − q) = −conj spec(p, q) − 2σ(p, q), a reflection in the vertical line Re λ = −σ(p, q) (checked as an operator identity at Δ = 0, 2/5, 1 and 3); this relation between (p, q) and (p, N − q) takes the form spec ↦ −spec − 2σ(p, q) only where the spectrum of (p, q) is closed under conjugation, as The Pairing Condition distinguishes the reflection in a vertical line from the point reflection. Taken linearly, composed with the alternating-site-sign gauge 𝒟 as in the fold lattice's holomorphic legs ([PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY](../docs/proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md) §7, the gauge of F151), it is a similarity of each block onto −L − 2σ(p, q) on its partner at Δ = 0 and fails at Δ = 2/5 (checked).

At N = 4 and η = 3/10, the half-filled block (2, 2) is its own image and pairs with itself about −2.8γ, the local centre at the rate (1 − η)γ; the corner block (0, 4) pairs with (4, 4) about −7.6γ. The F22 modes at the far end, at −2σ under local noise, split the same way: the image of the stationary popcount projector P_p (F22's X^⊗N·P_p is its adjoint) sits at −2γ[(1 − η)N + η(N − 2p)²], so the N + 1 far-end modes that local noise keeps at one rate spread over ⌊N/2⌋ + 1 rates; for a positive correlation the GHZ coherence (p = 0 or N) decays fastest among them, its superdecoherence, and for a negative one slowest. On hardware, in the [price-pair campaign](PRICE_PAIR_HARDWARE_PREDICTION.md) on three qubits, the GHZ coherence outlived the product of its three single-qubit coherences, read in the product state |+++⟩; under independent noise the two would be equal. Anti-correlated slow noise, Var(φ₀ + φ₁ + φ₂) ≪ Σ_j Var(φ_j) for the qubits' random phases φ_j, would do this, the quasi-static counterpart of the dark GHZ coherence above, and was the campaign's first reading. A control on the three-qubit W state (not the ladder W of this page) ruled out every pairwise Gaussian covariance model, and the campaign traces the bulk to coherent ZZ crosstalk, which the GHZ coherence does not feel and the single-qubit coherences of that product state do, each beside superposed neighbours (the mechanism is set out there), supported directly in its fourth run, through conditional shifts; how much room that leaves for a residual anti-correlated component depends on the shifts' correction, "from none to about 30 % of the signal", an upper bound, not a claim. Such a residual would be slow noise, outside this page's white-noise model.

The block law is written for one common covariance; for a general pattern the price identity decides block by block. K is unchanged by flipping every spin, so the popcounts p and N − p see the same values of it. When K takes one value K_p on all configurations of popcount p and one value K_q on those of popcount q, {K, ·} is the number K_p + K_q on the image (q, N − p), and block (p, q) pairs with its image about −(K_p + K_q)/2, Jordan structure included, for every covariance pattern (H popcount-conserving and commuting with X^⊗N, complex H included through the one-sided form). The empty and the full configuration are single, so the four one-dimensional corner blocks (0, 0), (0, N), (N, 0) and (N, N) always qualify, trivially. On popcount 1, with site k excited, K = σ + 2Σ_{l<l′} a_ll′ − 4r_k, r_k = Σ_{l≠k} a_kl the site's summed covariance, so popcounts 1 and N − 1 qualify exactly when every site has the same r_k; at N = 3 that forces one common covariance, at N = 4 it does not, and the law after the list below settles every popcount. On the open XXZ chain the blocks that pair with their images (exact characteristic polynomials, about the centre their traces fix, the trace of the block plus that of its image over twice the dimension) are exactly the qualifying ones in all thirteen patterns checked:

- the four corners alone at N = 3 for one covariance on a bond, one between the two end sites, two different covariances on the two bonds, three different covariances on all three pairs (at unequal rates), and the sign pattern defined after this list with s = (1, −1, 1); and at N = 4 for a pattern decaying with distance (3γ/10, γ/10, 0) and for one covariance on the bond (0, 1) or on the middle bond (1, 2);
- 16 of the 25 blocks, every block with p and q in {0, 1, 3, 4}, at N = 4 for two disjoint shared pairs (0, 1) and (2, 3), a ring of nearest-neighbour covariances, a pattern that puts one covariance on each of the three perfect matchings of four sites (3γ/10 on (0, 1) and (2, 3), γ/10 on (0, 2) and (1, 3), −γ/5 on (0, 3) and (1, 2)), and the alternating sign pattern s = (1, −1, 1, −1): each has the same summed covariance at every site, while K still takes several values on popcount 2;
- 9 of 25 at N = 4 for the sign pattern s = (1, 1, 1, −1), where popcount 2, beside the corners' 0 and 4, sees one value of K and the blocks with p and q in {0, 2, 4} pair.

A sign pattern is 3γ/10·s_k s_l off the diagonal, γ on it, the shared field read after flipping the sites with s_k = −1, a flip that does not preserve the popcount blocks, so these are new patterns in this grading. That the qualifying blocks pair is proved; that no other block does is read in the thirteen patterns, not proved. One reading holds for every pattern: a coherence that disagrees at a single site l pays 2a_ll whatever the covariances, so for a popcount-conserving H the four edge blocks (0, 1), (1, 0), (N − 1, N) and (N, N − 1), all of whose coherences disagree at one site, run exactly as under local noise (F2's (0,1) block among them), while their Π_Z images, such as (1, N) for (0, 1), carry the covariances (checked on random a at N = 3..5).

**Which popcounts see one value of K.** The diagonal of a enters K only through σ, since z_k² = 1, so the question belongs to the covariances a_kl, k ≠ l, alone, at any rates. With 𝒜 the set of excited sites, |𝒜| = p, a pair of sites flips the sign of its term a_kl z_k z_l exactly when 𝒜 separates them, so

  K(𝒜) = σ + 2T − 4·cut(𝒜),  T = Σ_{k<l} a_kl,

cut(𝒜) the summed covariance across 𝒜; the [price-pair page](PRICE_PAIR_HARDWARE_PREDICTION.md) writes its hardware law over the same partition, as the sum on each side, which is T − cut. For N ≥ 3 split the covariances as a_kl = κ̄ + x_k + x_l + Ω_kl (k ≠ l), with κ̄ = 2T/(N(N − 1)) the mean covariance and x_k = (r_k − (N − 1)κ̄)/(N − 2), r_k = Σ_{l≠k} a_kl the site's summed covariance; then Σ_k x_k = 0, the rows of Ω, what is left, sum to zero, and the split is unique, summing over all pairs fixing κ̄ and the row sums then fixing x. It is the split of a symmetric pattern into the three parts the permutations of the sites keep apart: x = 0 exactly when every site carries the same summed covariance, and Ω = 0 exactly when the pattern is additive, a_kl = y_k + y_l. Then

  cut(𝒜) = κ̄·p(N − p) + (N − 2p)·x(𝒜) − 2·Ω(𝒜),

x(𝒜) the sum of x over 𝒜 and Ω(𝒜) the sum of Ω over the pairs inside 𝒜. The law: for 1 ≤ p ≤ N − 1, K is one number on popcount p exactly when

- p = 1 or N − 1, and x = 0;
- p = N/2 at even N ≥ 4, and Ω = 0;
- any other p, 2 ≤ p ≤ N − 2, and x = 0 and Ω = 0, one common covariance.

At N = 2 every pattern qualifies, and at N = 3 Ω vanishes for every pattern. The conditions suffice: Ω(𝒜) vanishes on single sites and, its rows summing to zero, on sets of N − 1 sites, and the middle term vanishes at p = N/2. They are needed: let cut be one number χ_p on popcount p and sum over the p-sets that contain a fixed site k. For the x term, Σ_{𝒜∋k} x(𝒜) = C(N − 2, p − 1)·x_k because the x_i sum to zero, the identity with which [PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY](../docs/proofs/PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md#the-zeno-end) reads the single-site density of its lifted mode, Σ_{𝒜∋j} Σ_{i∈𝒜} f(i) = C(N − 2, p − 1)·f(j) when the f(i) sum to zero; the Ω term gives nothing, its rows summing to zero; so (χ_p − κ̄·p(N − p))·C(N − 1, p − 1) = (N − 2p)·C(N − 2, p − 1)·x_k for every k. Summing over k gives χ_p = κ̄·p(N − p), and then (N − 2p)·x = 0, which settles p = 1 and N − 1 and leaves Ω(𝒜) = 0 on every p-set. For 2 ≤ p ≤ N − 2 compare a p-set 𝒜 ∋ k with 𝒜 − k + l, l ∉ 𝒜: Σ_{v∈𝒜−k}(Ω_kv − Ω_lv) = 0 for every choice of the p − 1 sites 𝒜 − k among the N − 2 sites other than k and l. Since 1 ≤ p − 1 ≤ N − 3, exchanging one of those sites for one outside them shows that Ω_kv − Ω_lv takes one value for every v, and p − 1 times that value is zero. So Ω_kv = Ω_lv whenever k, l and v are distinct, each column of Ω is one number off the diagonal, the symmetry of Ω makes it the same number for every column, and the zero row sums make it zero. ∎

So the popcounts that see one value of K form one of four sets, and the qualifying blocks, those whose two popcounts both do, one of four shapes: {0, N}, the four corners, when the sites' summed covariances differ and, at even N, the pattern is not additive; {0, 1, N − 1, N}, 16 blocks in four 2 × 2 corner squares, when every site carries the same summed covariance and the pattern is not one number; at even N, {0, N/2, N}, 9 blocks at the corners, the edge midpoints and the centre, when the pattern is additive and not one number; and every popcount, (N + 1)² blocks, under one common covariance, which equal row sums and an additive form together already force. At N = 3 only the first and the last occur, Ω being zero there, and at odd N the third does not. The patterns above are instances: the sign pattern s = (1, 1, 1, −1) is additive, y = (3γ/20)·(1, 1, 1, −3), and so is a star of covariances, y at the hub the covariance of each spoke and 0 at every leaf, which is why the star of the [gallery's figure](../visualizations/README.md#the-atmosphere-its-price-and-what-it-cannot-tell-apart) at N = 6 keeps popcount 3. On popcount p an additive pattern gives K = σ − 2Σ_k y_k + 2(N − 2p)·Σ_k y_k z_k, a site-dependent Z term weighted by N − 2p, which half filling does not see. On every popcount that sees one value, K_p = σ − Nκ̄ + κ̄(N − 2p)², so (K_p + K_q)/2 is the shared field's σ(p, q) at κ = κ̄: a pattern that is not one number keeps some of the tiles the shared field of its mean covariance would draw, the gallery's bowl, or its dome for a negative mean. For a Hamiltonian whose hopping connects the configurations within each popcount sector, as the open XY chain's does, K is one number on popcount p exactly when it commutes with the Hamiltonian there, by Step 2 (b) of [the asymptotic sector projection](../docs/proofs/PROOF_ASYMPTOTIC_SECTOR_PROJECTION.md), the commutant within the diagonal operators. The law settles which blocks qualify; whether a block outside them pairs with its image stays read in the thirteen patterns.

The same law answers, on the Hamiltonian's side, where a ZZ interaction Σ_{k<l} w_kl Z_k Z_l, couplings w_kl, is one number on an excitation sector. At p = 1 it is the degree diagonal: for one coupling J on every bond of a graph with |E| bonds, the configuration with site l excited carries J[|E| − 2·deg(l)] ([D10](../docs/proofs/derivations/D10_W1_DISPERSION.md), [F152](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f152)), which [PROOF_K_PARTNERSHIP](../docs/proofs/PROOF_K_PARTNERSHIP.md) calls "a constant shift on uniform-degree topologies", one number exactly on a regular graph; at p = 2 the "genuine two-particle interaction" that page finds in the two-excitation sector is one number from N = 5 on only when every pair of sites carries the same coupling. Where the ZZ energy V is one number on a sector no hop changes it, and there the detuning split of the Zeno end, the part of block p's slowest rate at strong dephasing that the diagonal terms set (Theorem E (d)'s δ_p), vanishes ([Theorem E (d)](../docs/proofs/PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md#the-zeno-end), "zero where no hop changes V"). Theorem E (d) proves that split on chains, uniform or not and with ZZ of any range, so a chain whose ZZ couplings give every site the same summed coupling, as (c, 0, c) on the three bonds at N = 4 does, or form an additive pattern, as (c, 0, −c) does, has that zero too, at popcounts 1 and 3 for (c, 0, c) and at 2 for (c, 0, −c); on the uniform chain the law leaves no popcount strictly between 0 and N from N = 3 on, and for Δ ≠ 0 the end bond's weight Δ²C(N − 2, p − 1)/2 in the witness (`ZenoEndWitness`, `inspect --root zeno`, at J = γ = 1, the ZZ coupling JΔ) never vanishes there. On the ring and the star, which the same proof reads in "Which filling lasts longest", the witness's closed weights (Δ ≠ 0) vanish exactly where the law says: every ring bond's 4Δ²C(N − 4, p − 2) at popcounts 1 and N − 1, the ring being regular, and every star bond's Δ²(N − 2p)²C(N − 2, p − 1)/2 at N/2, the star being additive. The same proof's form for ZZ couplings of any range, a hop between sites i and j changing the ZZ energy by 2|Σ_k c_k z_k| with c_k = w_ik − w_jk over the other sites, holds the additive face in general: for an additive pattern c_k = y_i − y_j for every k, and the other sites' z_k sum to N − 2p, so every hop of block p changes the energy by 2|y_i − y_j|·|N − 2p|, nothing at half filling; the star's "imbalance of the other arms", 2J|Δ|·|N − 2p|, is its instance.

## Where the single mirror breaks

By F137 the whole spectrum has one candidate centre, trace/dim = −σ, whatever the covariances, since their terms average to zero over the coherences; the dimension-weighted mean of the σ(p, q) is σ. Whenever the covariances have a positive sum that candidate fails, at every N and for any covariance pattern, given a ⪰ 0 and an H that keeps |0…0⟩ and |1…1⟩ as eigenstates (a popcount-conserving H does): the GHZ coherence |0…0⟩⟨1…1| is then an eigenvector (for a popcount-conserving H a block of its own), its real part −2σ − 4·Σ_{k<l} a_kl, and a partner about −σ would have real part +4·Σ_{k<l} a_kl, above zero, where the generator of a valid evolution has no eigenvalue, so not even the rates pair (the real parts have the one candidate centre −σ as well, the real part of the trace being −4^N·σ). For the shared field the two real parts are −2γ[(1 − η)N + ηN²] and +2γηN(N − 1), read at N = 2..5 and on random covariance patterns with entries of either sign.

For the shared field at κ < 0 the GHZ coherence decays slowest among the far-end modes, and another of them breaks the mirror. For a popcount-conserving H commuting with X^⊗N and a pattern on which K takes one value K_p on popcount p, P_p·X^⊗N, the bra-flipped image of the populations of popcount p, is an eigenvector at −2K_p: H commutes with it, and each of its cells |i⟩⟨ī| pays the rate 2K(i), since there ket and bra differ at every site. Given a ⪰ 0, wherever K_p > σ its partner about −σ would have real part 2K_p − 2σ > 0, so not even the rates pair. On such a popcount K_p = σ − Nκ̄ + κ̄(N − 2p)², κ̄ = 2T/(N(N − 1)) the mean covariance and T = Σ_{k<l} a_kl the summed one (by the four shapes above, K on such a popcount is the shared field's value at the mean covariance), so K_p > σ exactly when T·((N − 2p)² − N) > 0. Under one common covariance every popcount qualifies and K_p = σ − Nκ + κ(N − 2p)² is half the far-end rate of the modes in the section on the blockwise pairing (they sit at −2K_p): for κ < 0 the mode at p = ⌊N/2⌋ breaks the mirror, (N − 2p)² ≤ 1 < N there, and the GHZ argument is the case p = 0 for κ > 0; in general the GHZ coherence is the far-end mode at p = 0 (K_0 > σ exactly when T > 0), its route asking less of H. So a valid shared field of either sign breaks the global palindrome at every N, the rates included, for every popcount-conserving H commuting with X^⊗N, and the mirror lives on block by block.

A direct check on the whole spectrum agrees: on the XXZ chain at J = γ/2 and Δ = 2/5 the whole characteristic polynomial at N = 2 and at N = 3 pairs about −Nγ at η = 0 and at none of η = −1/5, 3/10, 9/10. Block by block, a block and its image pair about −Nγ at η ≠ 0 exactly when d² + m² = N, since a finite spectrum cannot pair about two different centres; that is none of the 16 blocks at N = 3 and four of the 25 at N = 4 (read in exact characteristic polynomials over ℚ(i)). The global mirror holds at η = 0, where every σ(p, q) is σ.

An additive pattern at even N whose covariances sum to a negative number has such a far-end mode at p = N/2 (by the four-shapes law at every even N ≥ 4; read at N = 4). For a negative sum a pattern has no such mode when its only qualifying popcounts are 0 and N or, at N ≥ 4, {0, 1, N − 1, N}, since (N − 2)² − N = (N − 1)(N − 4) ≥ 0; negative covariances on the four bonds of a ring at N = 4 are an example, and there the routes below partly take over.

At weak enough coupling the rates fail to pair for every valid field with a covariance and every H. Write H = J·H₀. At J = 0 every cell is an eigenvector, the cell |i⟩⟨ī| at −2K(i), and K − σ = Σ_{k≠l} a_kl Z_k Z_l is a nonzero combination of distinct ZZ strings, so over the configurations it averages to zero without being constant and its largest value lies above zero: that cell sits below −2σ. The eigenvalues move continuously with J, so for small enough J one of them stays below −2σ, and its partner about −σ would grow. Any eigenvalue with real part below −2σ rules out the pairing of the rates; the GHZ coherence, the far-end modes and this cell are the three rate routes here, each guaranteeing one. No real part can lie lower than that cell's: by the Rayleigh identity Re λ = v†Herm(L)v/‖v‖², which holds for every eigenvector v and which the [Absorption Theorem](../docs/proofs/PROOF_ABSORPTION_THEOREM.md) reads, every real part lies between minus the largest cell rate and zero, Herm(L) = (L + L†)/2 in the Hilbert-Schmidt product being minus the diagonal of the cell rates; the largest cell rate is 2·max K, reached on the cells |i⟩⟨ī| (a convex function of u takes its largest value on the cube at a corner), so at J = 0 the cell of the configuration with the largest K attains that end. The limit is the Zeno end's, J/γ → 0, which Theorem E of [the weight-1 proof](../docs/proofs/PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md#the-zeno-end) reads on the slow population modes of the blocks (p, p); here it is read at the fast end.

The GHZ route needs a positive sum and an H keeping the all-0 and all-1 configurations; the far-end route needs a popcount on which K takes one value above σ and an H that conserves the popcount and commutes with X^⊗N; the weak-coupling route needs J small enough. Beyond them, at any coupling, a trace can still decide for the complex eigenvalues. A spectrum paired about −σ has every odd power sum of λ + σ equal to zero (one direction of the power-sum lemma in §1 of [the windowed converse](../docs/proofs/PROOF_F87_WINDOWED_MONOMIAL_CONVERSE.md)), and the cubic one, Tr((L + σ)³), has a closed form for every real symmetric a and every Hermitian H, taken traceless (a multiple of the identity in H changes nothing in L, but it would change the terms below). Write a = Λ + C, Λ the diagonal of a (the rates a_kk) and C the matrix of the covariances, Θ_kl = Tr(H²Z_kZ_l)/2^N for the two-site ZZ content of H² and ξ_k = Tr(H Z_k)/2^N for the single-site Z content of H, with Tr over the 4^N cells |i⟩⟨j| (populations included) for a map on the coherences such as L, over the 2^N basis states for an operator such as H²Z_kZ_l, and tr the trace of an N × N matrix; then

  Tr((L + σ)³) = −4^N·[4·tr(C·(2a + Λ)·C) − 3·tr(CΘ) − 6·ξᵀa ξ].

On the cell |i⟩⟨j|, with z = z(i) the ket's Z values and z′ = z(j) the bra's, L + σ is the commutator part L_H plus the diagonal g = Σ_k a_kk z_k z′_k − Σ_{k<l} a_kl (z_k − z′_k)(z_l − z′_l). Tr(L_H³) vanishes (the terms of (H ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ Hᵀ)³ cancel in pairs under the trace), and so does Tr(L_H g²), because g is unchanged when ket and bra trade places while the diagonal of L_H changes sign (the swap behind the parity in γ of [the moment tower](../docs/proofs/PROOF_MOMENT_TOWER_PUMP_CHANNEL.md)); that leaves 3·Tr(L_H² g) + Tr(g³). The first gives the two Hamiltonian terms: on |i⟩⟨j| the diagonal of L_H² is −[(H²)_ii + (H²)_jj − 2H_ii H_jj]; the parts of g that see one configuration alone, −Σ_{k<l} a_kl (z_k z_l + z′_k z′_l), meet (H²)_ii and (H²)_jj and give the Θ term (summing over the cell's other index, where Tr Z_k Z_l = Tr Z_k = 0), and, H being traceless, nothing with H_ii H_jj; the parts that tie ket to bra, Σ_k a_kk z_k z′_k + Σ_{k<l} a_kl (z_k z′_l + z′_k z_l), meet H_ii H_jj and give the ξ term, so 3·Tr(L_H² g) = 3·4^N·[tr(CΘ) + 2·ξᵀa ξ]. The second is 4^N times the mean of g³ over the cells, the sites independent, each carrying (z_k z′_k, z_k − z′_k) = (1, 0) twice, (−1, 2) and (−1, −2), with equal weight; per site the mean of z_k z′_k (z_k − z′_k)² is −2 and that of (z_k − z′_k)² is 2; writing g = A − B with A = Σ_k a_kk z_k z′_k, only 3AB² and −B³ survive the average of (A − B)³, the first as a covariance squared beside the diagonal entry of one of its ends (3·(−2)·2 = −12 each), the second as a triangle of covariances (3!·2³ = 48): −[12·Σ_{k<l} a_kl²(a_kk + a_ll) + 48·Σ_{k<l<l′} a_kl a_ll′ a_kl′] = −[12·tr(ΛC²) + 8·tr(C³)] = −4·tr(C·(2a + Λ)·C). Under local noise C = 0 and what remains is 6·4^N·Σ_k γ_k ξ_k², F117's third-moment face (there at one rate): already under independent noise a longitudinal term of H on a single site breaks the pairing of the complex eigenvalues, as the uniform field below does.

The bracket's noise term is positive for every valid generator with a covariance: 4·tr(C·(2a + Λ)·C) = 8·tr(CaC) + 4·tr(CΛC), where tr(CaC) ≥ 0 for a ⪰ 0 and tr(CΛC) = Σ_k a_kk·Σ_{l≠k} a_kl² > 0, since a ⪰ 0 gives a_kk a_ll ≥ a_kl² > 0 wherever a covariance sits; an indefinite a can turn it negative (zero diagonal and every covariance −1 at N = 3 give −48). An H commuting with X^⊗N has ξ = 0, since X^⊗N Z_k X^⊗N = −Z_k. So for such an H, taken traceless, and a ⪰ 0 with a covariance the global palindrome (the pairing of its complex eigenvalues) breaks whenever Σ_{k≠l} a_kl·Tr(H²Z_kZ_l) ≤ 0, at every N and for either sign of the summed covariance, and nothing about the all-0 and all-1 configurations or the popcount is needed. On the open XY chain Θ_kl is −2J² on the bonds and zero elsewhere: a bond squares to (XX + YY)² = 2 − 2ZZ, and the anticommutator of two bonds sharing a site is 2(XX + YY) on the outer pair, which holds no ZZ. On the open Heisenberg chain the bond square gives the same −2J², and the anticommutator of two bonds sharing a site is 2(XX + YY + ZZ) on the outer pair, so Θ carries +2J² on the next-nearest pairs as well. On the XY chain the condition is therefore that the covariances on the bonds sum to zero or more: a field correlated only between sites that share no bond breaks the pairing of the complex eigenvalues whatever its sign (read at N = 3 with the covariance −3γ/10 between the two end sites and J = γ/2, by the third moment and by the whole characteristic polynomial; there K takes one value only on the empty and the full configuration, and that value lies below σ, so neither the GHZ coherence nor a far-end mode decides; its spectrum, read in floating point, has an eigenvalue below −2σ all the same, near −6.92γ at N = 3 and below −2σ still at J = 20γ, and at N = 4 and 5 as well, so there the rates fail too, through a mode none of the three rate routes names).

Where the condition fails, the third moment along a line a = Λ + τC through the diagonal Λ is τ times a quadratic in τ (ξ being zero), whose linear coefficient 12·tr(ΛC²) is positive for a valid field with a covariance, so it vanishes at no more than two nonzero strengths per line. For the shared field at uniform rates it is −4^N·4(N − 1)κ·[2N(N − 2)κ² + 3Nγκ + 3J²] on the open XY chain and −4^N·4κ·[2N(N − 1)(N − 2)κ² + 3N(N − 1)γκ + 3J²] on the open Heisenberg chain: for κ > 0 never zero, and for κ < 0 zero inside the allowed range −γ/(N − 1) ≤ κ at weak enough coupling, where the bracket's root sits near κ = −J²/(Nγ) on the XY chain and near κ = −J²/(N(N − 1)γ) on the Heisenberg chain; there the far-end mode decides.

For the shared field the moments give a cross-check: they reach the verdict of the far-end modes for the complex eigenvalues on a route of their own, and nothing beyond it. On the open XY and Heisenberg chains at uniform rates the third and fifth moments, Tr((L + σ)³) and Tr((L + σ)⁵), are polynomials in γ, κ and J with integer coefficients, homogeneous, so γ = 1 loses nothing, read from sums over cells and pairs of cells without computing an eigenvalue. Their common zeros with κ ≠ 0 are located exactly: the resultant in κ vanishes at exactly the couplings where the two share a zero (their leading coefficients in κ being nonzero constants), and the first subresultant gives that zero where it is simple (the script stops with an error otherwise). They lie outside the allowed range, at κ < −γ/(N − 1) (the XY chain at N = 3..7, the Heisenberg chain at N = 7), or exist for no J > 0 (both chains at N = 2, the Heisenberg chain at N = 3..6), so at N = 2..7 one of the two is nonzero at every κ ≠ 0 in the allowed range and every J > 0 ([`shared_field_odd_moments.py`](../simulations/shared_field_odd_moments.py)). At N = 3 on the XY chain the third moment's zero reaches the anti-correlated end itself: at κ = −γ/2 and J = γ it is exactly zero, while the fifth moment is −11520·γ⁵ by two routes (the cell sums and matrix powers), the far-end mode at p = 1 sits at −8γ, below −2σ = −6γ, and the whole characteristic polynomial does not pair.

Four routes and a cross-check side by side, each for a valid field (a ⪰ 0):

| Route | Noise | Hamiltonian | Reach | What it rules out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The GHZ coherence | covariances summing to a positive number | keeps the all-0 and all-1 configurations as eigenstates | every coupling, every N | the pairing of the rates and of the complex eigenvalues |
| A far-end mode P_p·X^⊗N | K one value K_p > σ on some popcount p (under one common covariance: every allowed κ ≠ 0) | conserves the popcount and commutes with X^⊗N | every coupling, every N | the pairing of the rates and of the complex eigenvalues |
| The fastest cell at weak coupling | at least one covariance | any H = J·H₀ | J small enough (not quantified) | the pairing of the rates and of the complex eigenvalues |
| The third moment | at least one covariance | commutes with X^⊗N (taken traceless) | decides wherever Tr((L + σ)³) ≠ 0: at every coupling and N when Σ_{k≠l} a_kl·Tr(H²Z_kZ_l) ≤ 0, otherwise at all but at most two nonzero strengths per line a = Λ + τC | the pairing of the complex eigenvalues only |
| The third and fifth moments together (the cross-check) | one common covariance at uniform rates, every allowed κ ≠ 0 | the open XY and Heisenberg chains | every J > 0, N = 2..7 | the pairing of the complex eigenvalues only |

Three limits. First, the moments rule out only the pairing of the complex eigenvalues; where none of the three rate routes reaches, whether the rates pair is not decided here. In every case read on this page, the end-site example included, an eigenvalue lies below −2σ as well (read in floating point), so whether the third moment ever breaks the complex pairing where no eigenvalue lies below −2σ is open. Second, the moment test runs one way: vanishing low odd moments do not prove a pairing (all of them together would), as the K3 pair of [F117](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md) (one of its verified cases, a pair of Pauli terms whose hopping graph closes a triangle, firing first at the ninth power sum) shows at N = 4 under local noise, its third, fifth and seventh power sums vanishing identically while its spectrum does not pair. Third, where the third moment vanishes, outside the cross-check's setting (one common covariance at uniform rates on the two chains, N = 2..7) and with none of the rate routes reaching, whether the complex pairing breaks is not read.

## A uniform longitudinal field

Add a uniform longitudinal field h·Σ_k Z_k to a popcount-conserving, X^⊗N-even H under one common covariance. It is odd under X^⊗N, so it is not part of the H the identity needs; but its commutator term −i[h·Σ_k Z_k, ·] is a constant on a block, −2ih(q − p), and on its image (q, N − p) the constant −2ih(N − p − q); shifting both spectra by them turns the pairing above into

  spec(q, N − p) = −spec(p, q) − 2σ(p, q) + 2ih(2p − N),

for every block and every rate profile with one common covariance, checked at uniform rates and η = 0, 3/10, 9/10, and at site-dependent local rates (where σ(p, q) is σ), exact at N = 2..5 (and through characteristic polynomials at N = 3 at uniform rates, η = 0 and 3/10, where no block pairs with its image about the real centre −σ(p, q)). The real parts still pair about −σ(p, q); the complex values pair about a centre moved off the real axis by h(2p − N), which differs from block to block, vanishes for the blocks whose ket is half filled (p = N/2, at even N), and at odd N is never zero, so that at odd N no block pairs with its image about the real centre (a finite spectrum pairs about one centre at most); at N = 3, at its field, the Mirror Symmetry Proof records on its Heisenberg chain that no eigenvalue keeps its partner either (0/64). This is the block-level form of four observations. [The Mirror Symmetry Proof](../docs/proofs/MIRROR_SYMMETRY_PROOF.md) records rates paired at 64/64 and eigenvalues at 0/64 under a uniform field at N = 3. [The Classifier](THE_PALINDROME_CLASSIFIER.md)'s field, at its uniform, bond-free far end, "leaves both lists balanced and breaks only the pairing between them" (the lists of rates and of frequencies). F22's far-end eigenvectors carry the shift 2ih(N − 2k). Complement Connection's Theorem 1 is the global form of the break. In the language of the no-jump Hamiltonian H_eff = H − (i/2)K, the field adds 2h·S_z, Hermitian and odd under X^⊗N, which the mirror reads as an imaginary centre, and a shared field's correlation adds −2iκ·S_z² (up to a constant), anti-Hermitian and even, which it reads as a real one; both are functions of S_z, so a shared fluctuating field with a nonzero mean moves the centre along both axes at once.

A non-uniform field Σ_k h_k Z_k is not a constant on a block, and the block identity fails. The spectral pairing then holds for some blocks and not others. On the verifier's XXZ chain at uniform rates and η = 3/10, with one field tried per case, the blocks that still pair with their image about the centre their traces fix are 8 of 16 at N = 3 and 12 of 25 at N = 4 for a generic field, and 12 of 16 and 16 of 25 for an anti-palindromic one (h_k + h_{N−1−k} the same for every k, the anti-palindromic class of `ParameterKlein`); in both classes every block whose ket is empty or full is among them, as it must be: the field's term in Π_Z L Π_Z⁻¹ + L is −2i Σ_k h_k z_k(i) on the image of |i⟩⟨j|, one number on the image of a block whose ket is a single configuration. The anti-palindromic class, F93's, keeps more because a second map works for it: composing Π_Z with the site reversal ℛ (a symmetry of the uniform chain and of the shared field) leaves the field term −i Σ_k [(h_k + h_ℛ,k) z_k(i) + (h_ℛ,k − h_k) z_k(j)], h_ℛ the reversed profile, and when h + h_ℛ is constant that is one number on the image of every block whose bra is empty or full (at uniform rates, where ℛ is a symmetry). ℛ carries the anti-palindromic field h̄ + δ (δ_k = h_k − h̄) onto h̄ − δ, the reflected scan along an odd direction of F131's Theorem A. The anti-palindromic survivors are exactly the blocks with an empty or full ket or bra, 12 of 16 and 16 of 25 (that these pair is proved, the full residual being a multiple of the identity on those blocks; that no others do is read for the one field per class). Beyond its empty- or full-ket blocks a generic field keeps two more at N = 4 (the counts are of the blocks that pair with their image, which under a generic field need not be those that pair with their preimage), (2, 0) and (2, 4), for a reason of their own: their images (0, 2) and (4, 2) are their Hermitian conjugates, (0, 2) = (2, 0)† and (4, 2) = (2, 4)† (the image (q, N − p) is the conjugate block (q, p) exactly when p = N/2, and of those blocks only these two have a bra that is a single configuration), whose spectra are the conjugate ones, so such a block pairs with its image exactly when its spectrum is symmetric about the vertical line Re λ = −σ(p, q); with the bra a single configuration and the rates uniform the dissipator is one number on the block, −σ(p, q) itself, the spectrum lies on that line, and it does. At site-dependent rates with one common covariance these two drop out and 10 of 25 remain (checked). Which blocks survive for other Hamiltonians we did not classify.

## The two ladders

The block lattice carries two ladders ([the arc `sideways_spin_ladder`](../compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/OpenArcs/OpenArcsRegistry.cs), `SidewaysSpinLadderClaim`, [PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4](../docs/proofs/PROOF_FROZEN_BAND_SO4.md)). With Jordan-Wigner fermions c_l (the SO(4) proof writes them d_l, and its Φ is the W here), the spectator W(ρ) = Σ_l c_l†ρ c_l moves (p, q) to (p + 1, q + 1); the sideways S⁺(ρ) = Σ_l (−1)^l c_l†ρ c_l†, sites counted from l = 0, moves it to (p + 1, q − 1). On the open XY chain both commute with the generator under any local rates (the SO(4) proof's Lemmas 2.1 to 2.3; S⁺ also needs the hopping to connect only sites of opposite parity, which the open chain does). Π_Z carries one onto the other: since c_lᵀ = c_l† and X^⊗N c_l X^⊗N = (−1)^l c_l†, Π_Z W Π_Z⁻¹ = S⁺ at every N (checked at N = 3..5; with sites counted from 1, as the SO(4) proof counts them, the image is −S⁺). The identity is the bra flip's alone: the transpose leaves W fixed, and R(ρ) = ρ·X^⊗N, a particle-hole transformation of the bra species, carries W onto S⁺ (both checked), the staggered map named above, not the SO(4) proof's; the [F89 Path-k](F89_PATH_K_DIABOLIC.md) walk reads the same map at block level. Under a correlated field they part:

- W commutes with the generator for every real symmetric a. It fills a site where ket and bra agree on empty and leaves them agreeing on full, so u does not change, and no correlation pattern can see the step; the [staircase flight design](STAIRCASE_NULLTEST_HARDWARE_PREDICTION.md) holds this at the level of one cell. The verifier shows on the Heisenberg chain that W's commutation is not automatic: that chain is not quadratic in the fermions and so lies outside F125's spectator intertwiner (Theorem B of [PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY](../docs/proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md)): W fails there already at η = 0.
- S⁺ commutes for every diagonal a and for no correlated one. Conjugating [W, L] = 0 by Π_Z and using the price identity gives [S⁺, L] = −[S⁺, {K, ·}] (checked), and since S⁺ commutes with the rest of L, [S⁺, L] = [S⁺, L_off], L_off the dissipator of the off-diagonal part of a. It fills the ket and empties the bra at one site, so it keeps the disagreement set, which is all local noise reads, and flips the sign of one entry of u, which any nonzero covariance reads: on a coherence that disagrees at l (ket empty, bra full) and at k and agrees elsewhere, the S⁺ step at l lands on a matrix unit no other step reaches, and its rate changes by 8|a_lk| ≠ 0 (the commutator's nonvanishing checked on random a, the shared field and a single small covariance between the two end sites). Under one covariance common to every pair the failure is only a shift: K is one number on each popcount, so [L, S⁺] = −8κ(d + 1)·S⁺ on the source block (p, q), and S⁺ still carries each eigenvector of L to an eigenvector or to zero, the eigenvalue shifted by −8κ(d + 1), the chains of S⁺ being those of the local generator at the rates γ_k − κ (checked at N = 3..5); for a random covariance pattern [L, S⁺] is no such shift (checked).

In the SO(4) proof's Hubbard reading (Medvedyeva, Essler and Prosen's η-pairing in dissipative fermions, in the repository's literature review), with ket and bra as the two species and the local spin s^z_k = ½(n_k^ket − n_k^bra) (not the S_z above), u_k = −4s^z_k and the dissipator is −8·Σ_kl a_kl s^z_k s^z_l. Its diagonal part is −8·Σ_k a_kk (s^z_k)², and (s^z_k)² is a third of the local spin's square, untouched by either SU(2); the off-diagonal part is an Ising exchange between the sites' spins, which the spin SU(2) does not leave invariant (so S⁺ fails to commute) and which commutes with the η-pairing ladder (so W survives): the split read in the symmetry's own terms. For one common covariance it is local terms plus −8κ(Σ_k s^z_k)², with Σ_k s^z_k = d/2, a function of the spin Cartan alone, which lowers the spin SU(2) to its U(1): the multiplets' eigenvalues split while S⁺ still steps along them, the shift above. So the parenthesis in `SidewaysSpinLadderClaim`, "the ladder never moves the disagreement set", is exactly the scope of S⁺'s immunity: the set, not its signs.

One consequence reaches back into the arc. Π_Z is a permutation of matrix units, so it preserves norms, and at local rates on the open XY chain it carries each eigenvector of L to an eigenvector of L (at −λ − 2σ); since Π_Z⁻¹ S⁺ Π_Z = W, Π_Z⁻¹ therefore carries every chain of eigenvectors that S⁺ generates onto a chain of W with the same transport norms (the norms ‖S⁺v‖ of the steps along the chain), ‖W·Π_Z⁻¹x‖ = ‖S⁺x‖, and the inverse quarter turn moves the lattice's spin lines m = ±1 onto its η lines d = ±1. `SidewaysSpinLadderClaim` records the S⁺ chains as measured and gated at N = 4, 5, 7 and 9, their transport norms the Clebsch-Gordan coefficients √(ℓ(ℓ + 1) − μ(μ + 1)) (ℓ = (N − 3)/2 the chains' spin, μ the weight), and the η chains' norms as untested. By this identity every S⁺ chain on m = ±1 has an image, a W chain on d = ±1 at the mirrored eigenvalue, with the same norms; whether that image is the arc's η chain on its line turns on a multiplicity, below.

The arc's two η chains lie in the band half of the F125 orbit at odd N (the arc's band and fold blocks, whose band half is the blocks with |d| = 1), one multiplet on each line d = ±1; the measured S⁺ chains are its fold half (its blocks on m = ±1). The records place the two halves at mirrored values: the claim names the F125 fold family "exactly the interiors of the two S⁺ chains", [PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY](../docs/proofs/PROOF_CODIM1_BY_ADDITIVITY.md) Theorem B and §7 carry the band sectors at λ and the fold at its partner, and the arc records band and fold as one orbit under λ ↦ −λ − 2N, the holomorphic fold that PROOF_CODIM1 §7 derives (in its units γ = 1, which is Π_Z's −λ − 2σ; Theorem B's partner is −λ̄ − 2N, the same on the real axis). Those records identify blocks and eigenvalues, not eigenvectors. Where the η multiplet is L's only multiplet on its line d = ±1 at that eigenvalue, Π_Z⁻¹ of a measured S⁺ chain is the arc's η chain, and the η norms are the measured Clebsch-Gordan values at the odd N where the S⁺ chains were measured (5, 7 and 9). That multiplicity we did not check, so the η chains on d = ±1 stay unmeasured: once it holds, the identity reduces their norms to the S⁺ ones. The F89 Path-k walk measured the η-side norms on the d = −2 chain.

## What this does and does not say

It says that, for jumps built linearly from the Z_k with a real Kossakowski matrix and an H commuting with X^⊗N (real, for the similarity), the palindrome's price is the anticommutator with the decay operator Σ c†c, a number exactly when the matrix is diagonal, and that for a field shared alike by all pairs and a popcount-conserving H commuting with X^⊗N the mirror survives between each block and its image about centres set by the block's distance from the half-filling point, with a uniform longitudinal field adding an imaginary part. The identities are proved above for every N. So is the global break, for a ⪰ 0, in four forms, the first three for the rates as well: for a positive summed covariance and an H keeping the all-0 and all-1 configurations as eigenstates; wherever K takes one value above σ on a popcount, for a popcount-conserving H commuting with X^⊗N (under one common covariance every allowed κ ≠ 0); at weak enough coupling, for every pattern with a covariance and every H; and, for the complex eigenvalues, through the third moment's closed form, for a covariance and a traceless H commuting with X^⊗N whose squared two-site ZZ content, weighted by the covariances, sums to zero or less. So is which popcounts see one value of K for every covariance pattern. The reading of the third and fifth moments for the shared field at uniform rates on the open XY and Heisenberg chains is exact computer algebra at N = 2..7, a second route to the verdict of the far-end modes for the complex eigenvalues. The exact checks run at N = 2..5 for the identities and the block law at uniform rates, N = 3..5 for the block law at site-dependent rates and for the ladders, N = 3 and 4 for the characteristic polynomials of blocks, N = 2..4 for the third moment with a Hamiltonian (N = 2..5 at H = 0, N = 2..8 for its noise term, and the shared field's closed forms to N = 7), N = 2..5 for the far-end modes, and N = 2 and 3 for the whole spectrum.

It covers Z dephasing only. A shared field along a single other axis is the z case in a turned basis, so the block statements hold for an H that conserves the popcount and commutes with the global flip in that basis (the Heisenberg chain for every axis), and not for the z-anisotropic XXZ chain under a shared x or y noise field, which breaks that grading; a field shared along several axes at once, the singlet page's three-axis case, breaks that grading in any case, and none of the block statements apply there. The block law is written for one covariance common to every pair; for a general covariance pattern the identity still holds, the global palindrome breaks, for a ⪰ 0, wherever one of the four routes above reaches, and its complex pairing, for a traceless H commuting with X^⊗N, breaks at all but at most two nonzero strengths on each line a = Λ + τC (no moment beyond the third was read there for a general pattern, nor, where no rate route reaches, whether the rates alone pair), and a block pairs with its image where K takes one value on each of its two popcounts, which the four shapes settle for every pattern and which in the thirteen patterns checked are exactly the blocks that pair. It does not say how large the covariances are on any device. The price-pair campaign read them in three of its four runs: run 1 flagged one at 2.2 standard errors, run 2 found c₀₂ at 5.1 standard errors on the outer pair, which did not recur and stays unexplained, and run 3 read all three local; the price-pair page's verdict reads "No *persistent* correlated bath is required by the clean run-3 session". Nor can the [F81 hardware bridge](F81_VIOLATION_HARDWARE_BRIDGE.md)'s scalar f81_violation see the covariances: it reads the Π²-odd part of the noise, which correlated Z dephasing does not have. The conversion c_ij = 2ηγ is the singlet page's: it is Cov = 2a of the price-pair book (above) for the shared field. The [rung-ladder page](ABSORPTION_RUNG_LADDER_HARDWARE_PREDICTION.md)'s c_ij, twice its Lindblad coefficient, is the same book. The conversion is a change of book, not a measurement of η on any device. On the ladders this page reduces the η chains' transport norms on d = ±1 to the measured S⁺ ones only where the η multiplet is the only one on its line at that eigenvalue, which was not checked. And it does not touch F158's criterion, whose class needs jumps that square to a multiple of the identity: a jump in the span of the Z_k (real coefficients) does so only when it is a multiple of a single Z_k, so no jump set of a non-diagonal Kossakowski matrix is in it.
