## What this is about

A quantum state can meet itself again after a while: S(t) = Tr(ρ·e^{tL}ρ), the overlap of what it is with what it was. The mirror of this repository (Π, [F1](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f1); its factor R of F118, ρ ↦ ρ·X^⊗N, used here after the field has been turned to X, is ρ ↦ ρ·(cos φ·X + sin φ·Y)^⊗N for the field at φ) turns a state into a counterpart that meets itself the way the state would run backwards, at a price ([Π as a Centered Spectral Mirror](PI_AS_TIME_REVERSAL.md)). Some states meet themselves the same way in both directions; the parent page asks which. For the cat state, |0…0⟩ and |1…1⟩ with a plus sign in equal amounts, under a field in the equator plane at one angle φ from X on every bit, the answer is one sentence: it does so exactly when Nφ is a multiple of π. This page holds the statement, its proof on the letters, the closed form of the first term that breaks it, and what it does not yet answer.

## Abstract

N bits, each watched along Z at a rate γ_l ≥ 0 (σ = Σγ_l > 0), each under a field h_l·(cos φ·X + sin φ·Y) at one common angle φ and strengths h_l ≠ 0, and any Hermitian coupling H_c that commutes with X^⊗N and with Σ_l Z_l, on any graph (XXZ bonds of any strength and any anisotropy among them). For the cat with phase θ, ρ = |c_θ⟩⟨c_θ|, |c_θ⟩ = (|0…0⟩ + e^{iθ}|1…1⟩)/√2, the centred odd moments m_k = Tr(ρ·(L + σ)^k ρ) satisfy

    m_k = 0                                                    for odd k < 2N + 1,
    m_{2N+1} = (σ/3)·(2N + 1)!·∏_l h_l²·sin²(Nφ − θ),
    m_k = sin²(Nφ − θ)·G_k   with G_k free of φ and θ,        for every odd k,

so e^{σt}S(t) is even in t, which is S(t) = e^{−2σt}S(−t), the state meeting itself the same way forwards and, paying the price, backwards, **exactly when sin(Nφ − θ) = 0**. The first odd term of e^{σt}S(t) is (σ/3)·∏h_l²·sin²(Nφ − θ)·t^{2N+1}; it contains nothing of the coupling. With one field strength, one rate and one angle on every bit an isotropic coupling never enters the self-encounter at all, on any graph and at any order, and at order 2N + 3 the coupling enters only through Δ_b − J_b, bond by bond (step 7); with unequal rates or strengths the isotropic coupling enters, with unequal strengths already at order 2N + 3.

A hopping may carry a twist, a Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya part, and each bit may hold a field angle of its own. Where the angles follow the twist the law above holds with Nφ replaced by Σφ_l; on a ring they can follow only when the flux is a multiple of π. Where they do not, the twisted current that the fields drive through the bonds enters at order 7: m_7 = 96·Σ_l γ_l·(div j)_l² from N = 3 on where sin(Σφ_l − θ) = 0 and at every phase from N = 6 on (N = 2 has a form of its own), so at this order the look notices where that current piles up at a watched bit; a current that circulates around a ring without a source, or piles up only at unwatched bits, escapes the law (section "Under a twisted coupling").

## The setting

Bits l = 1 … N with the Pauli letters I, X, Y, Z at each; words (Pauli strings) are written P, and a state ρ = Σ_P c_P·P. The generator is

    L(ρ) = −i[H, ρ] + Σ_l γ_l (Z_l ρ Z_l − ρ),
    H = Σ_l h_l (cos φ·X_l + sin φ·Y_l) + H_c,

with H_c Hermitian, [H_c, X^⊗N] = 0 (every Pauli term of H_c carries an even count of Y and Z letters taken together) and [H_c, Σ_l Z_l] = 0 (H_c keeps the number of excitations). The XXZ bond J(XX + YY) + Δ·ZZ on any pair of bits is such a term, at any J and Δ and on any graph. The price is 2σ, σ = Σ_l γ_l. For any operator A, S_A(t) = Tr(A†·e^{tL}A); for a state this is the self-encounter. The property asked about is S(t) = e^{−2σt}S(−t), equivalently that f(t) = e^{σt}S(t) is even, equivalently that every odd centred moment m_k = Tr(ρ·(L + σ)^k ρ) vanishes (f(t) = Σ_k m_k t^k/k!). For operators ⟨A, B⟩ = Tr(A†B). The height of a word is its count of X and Y letters; the quiet edge is height 0, the far edge height N. The first move is R(A) = A·X^⊗N, a factor of the mirror ([F118](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f118-the-mirror-group-π--rd-the-dihedral-d₄-and-the-cube-of-characters-tier-1-derived-signed-permutation-identities-exact)), and "the look" is the self-encounter S. The cat as an operator is ρ_ϑ = |c_ϑ⟩⟨c_ϑ|.

## The proof, in letters

Seven steps, each read on the letters; the seventh concerns the coupling ([THE_ONE_SQUARE](../docs/THE_ONE_SQUARE.md) §3 and §7 to §9).

1. **The turn.** U = ⊗_l diag(1, e^{iφ}) carries X onto cos φ·X + sin φ·Y at every bit and leaves Z and H_c alone (H_c keeps the excitation number). So Ad_U carries the generator with the field along X onto the generator with the field at φ, and the self-encounter of ρ with the field at φ equals that of U†ρU with the field along X. U†|c_θ⟩ is the cat with phase θ − Nφ: the far corner |0…0⟩⟨1…1| picks up e^{iNφ} under ρ ↦ U†ρU. Through step 5 the field lies along X and the cat's phase is ϑ = θ − Nφ.
2. **The cat in words.** ρ_ϑ carries 1/2^N on every quiet word (I and Z letters only) with an even count of Z, nothing on the quiet words with an odd count of Z, ±cos ϑ/2^N on the far words (X and Y letters only) with an even count of Y, ±sin ϑ/2^N on those with an odd count, nothing elsewhere.
3. **The parity** ([F63](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f63-w_yz-parity-symmetry-l-pi2--0-tier-1-proven-analytically-verified-n2-5), "The three turns"). The letters that anticommute with X are Y and Z. A step of a term T carries a word P onto T·P up to a phase, or onto nothing where the two commute, and the count of Y and Z letters of a product is the sum of the two counts modulo 2; every term of the field along X and of H_c has an even count, so no step changes a word's parity ε(P) = (n_Y + n_Z) mod 2, and the watching moves no word. Equivalently X^⊗N·P·X^⊗N = (−1)^{ε(P)}·P and Ad_{X⊗N} commutes with L. The even words (the cat's quiet words and its cosine words) and the odd words (its sine words) never mix, and the self-encounter, bilinear in the amounts, has no cross terms between them: each moment is A_k + B_k·cos ϑ + C_k·cos²ϑ + F_k·sin²ϑ with A_k, …, F_k free of ϑ.
4. **The two points.** The first move R keeps ε, carries each sine word onto a quiet word with an odd count of Z, which the cat does not carry, and returns ρ_0 and ρ_π up to the sign. The whole H, the field along X and H_c, commutes with X^⊗N, so a state that R returns up to the sign has the property ([The Pairing Condition](THE_PAIRING_CONDITION.md), its trace form at one state). So every odd moment vanishes at ϑ = 0 and ϑ = π: B_k = 0 and A_k = −C_k, and m_k = (F_k − C_k)·sin²ϑ. In [F99](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md#f99)'s terms sin²ϑ = 2α of the turned cat, its Π²-odd content, and the two points are α = 0.
5. **The order.** Write sin²ϑ = (1 − cos 2ϑ)/2: the factor F_k − C_k is fixed by the part of m_k that goes with e^{±2iϑ}, which by step 4 is a pure cos 2ϑ. Writing ρ_ϑ = Q + (e^{−iϑ}E + e^{iϑ}E†)/2 with Q its quiet part and E = |0…0⟩⟨1…1|, that part is ¼·(e^{2iϑ}⟨E, (L + σ)^k E†⟩ + its conjugate), the amplitude from one corner of the far edge to the other. Let d be the count of bits at the upper level |1⟩ in the ket less that in the bra (THE_ONE_SQUARE §3's d = p − q): E has d = −N, E† has d = +N. The field along X is σ⁺ + σ⁻ and changes d by one per step; H_c and the watching keep d. So that amplitude needs at least 2N steps of the field. Without the watching the look is even in t for every state (the steps of any Hermitian H, as a table on the words, are minus their own transpose, so ⟨A, L_H^k A⟩ = 0 for odd k and every Hermitian A; the rate-free part of m_k vanishes at every ϑ, so its e^{±2iϑ} part does too), so every surviving monomial of an odd moment, in the parameters h_l, γ_l and the couplings, carries at least one rate. Hence m_k = 0 for odd k < 2N + 1, and the cos 2ϑ part of m_{2N+1} is made of exactly 2N field steps and one factor of a rate: a further field step would change d an odd number of times, and a step of H_c in place of the rate carries no rate and belongs to the look without the watching. m_{2N+1} contains nothing of H_c.
6. **The value.** Back with the field at φ and the cat with phase θ, and with H_c gone, every bit runs alone and the corner amplitude is a product over the bits of one bit's: e^{γ_l t}·⟨|0⟩⟨1|, e^{tL_l}|1⟩⟨0|⟩ = h_l² t² e^{−2iφ}(1 − γ_l t/3) + O(t⁴). The factor −γ/3 is read on the cube: one bit's round trip runs from height 1 to Z at height 0 and back, its centred charges are −γ, +γ, −γ, so the centred moments of the pair are 0, 2h²e^{−2iφ}, −2γh²e^{−2iφ}, and against the t² term the t³ term is (−γ)·2!/3! = −γ/3. The t^{2N+1} coefficient of the product is −(σ/3)·∏_l h_l²·e^{−2iNφ}, so the cos 2ϑ part of f's t^{2N+1} coefficient is ¼·[e^{2iθ}·(−σ/3)∏h_l² e^{−2iNφ} + conjugate] = −(σ/6)·∏h_l²·cos 2ϑ, and with step 4 m_{2N+1} = (σ/3)·(2N + 1)!·∏_l h_l²·sin²ϑ.
7. **The coupling at identical bits.** With one strength, one rate and one angle on every bit, write each XXZ bond as J_b(2·SWAP_b − 1) + (Δ_b − J_b)·Z_aZ_b. The cat's amount on a word is the same on every reordering of the word's letters across the bits, and identical bits keep every such reordering, so the state stays invariant under every permutation of the bits. Conjugation by a swap exchanges two bits' letters, so a swap commutes with such a state, and the isotropic part J_b(2·SWAP_b − 1) moves nothing. An isotropic coupling, of any strength on any bond of any graph, therefore never enters the self-encounter. A swap contributes only after a step of a Z_aZ_b term has broken the symmetry on its right, and, since the swap's step is minus its own adjoint and the bits' own steps keep the symmetry from both sides, also on its left; so J_b alone needs at least three coupling factors. At order 2N + 3, with its 2N field steps and its rate, at most two remain, and m_{2N+3} sees each bond only through Δ_b − J_b. With unequal rates or strengths the bits are not identical and the isotropic coupling enters, with unequal strengths already at order 2N + 3.

So m_{2N+1} ≠ 0 whenever σ > 0, every h_l ≠ 0 and sin ϑ ≠ 0, and the property fails; where sin ϑ = 0 step 4 gives it. This holds at every N. The letters give the structure and the one-bit value; the exact matrix computation below is the second route, and the two meet on the law.

## Under a twisted coupling

A hopping may carry a twist. On a bond e joining bits a and b, oriented from a to b, write it as J_e cos δ_e·(X_aX_b + Y_aY_b) + J_e sin δ_e·(X_aY_b − Y_aX_b) = 2J_e·(e^{iδ_e}|01⟩⟨10| + h.c.) on the two bits; its second part is a Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya term. It keeps the excitation number and breaks the commutation with X^⊗N, since each of its words carries one Y. ZZ terms may stand beside it as before, every bit l may hold a field h_l·(cos φ_l·X + sin φ_l·Y) at an angle of its own (h_l ≠ 0), and the watching is the page's. Turn every bit by its own angle, U = ⊗_l diag(1, e^{iφ_l}): every field comes to lie along X, the cat's phase becomes ϑ = θ − Σ_l φ_l, and each bond keeps a residual twist β_e = δ_e − (φ_b − φ_a). In that frame H = H_f + H_e + T: H_f the fields along X; H_e the untwisted part J_e cos β_e·(XX + YY) plus the ZZ terms, which commutes with X^⊗N; and T = Σ_e ξ_e·(X_aY_b − Y_aX_b), ξ_e = J_e sin β_e, which anticommutes with it. Call j_e = h_a·h_b·ξ_e the current on bond e, and (div j)_l the sum of j_e over the bonds that leave bit l less the sum over the bonds that arrive there.

**Where the angles follow the twist** (every β_e a multiple of π, so T = 0), the turned frame is the page's setting with couplings of signs ±J_e, and the three-line law holds as stated with Nφ replaced by Σ_l φ_l (step 7 then reads the turned couplings: a bond with β_e = π carries −J_e, and its isotropic point is Δ_e = −J_e): the cat has the property exactly when sin(Σ_l φ_l − θ) = 0. In the original frame the first move is then the turned one, A ↦ A·⊗_l(cos φ_l·X + sin φ_l·Y), which keeps H and, where sin(Σ_l φ_l − θ) = 0, returns the cat up to the sign. On an open chain, and on any tree, the angles can always follow, φ_b = φ_a + δ_e bond by bond. On a ring with every bond oriented around it the residual twists add up to the flux, Σ_e β_e = Φ = Σ_e δ_e, whatever the angles, so they can all be multiples of π only when Φ is. Under Z watching this is the spectral condition of [the palindrome as a colouring](THE_PALINDROME_AS_A_COLOURING.md)'s frame theorem (the palindrome holds exactly when some diagonal phase gauge makes H commute with X^⊗N) and of the holonomy in Theorem 2 of [the complement connection](../docs/proofs/PROOF_PALINDROME_COMPLEMENT_CONNECTION.md), read here at one state; on the letter cube it is the spiral gauge of [the cube site by site](THE_CUBE_SITE_BY_SITE.md), whose finding 3 reflects Heisenberg + DM by Π turned about Z by arctan(D/J) from site to site. In the letters it is F119's reversed flux: X^⊗N carries every hopping phase to its reverse (X^⊗N·H·X^⊗N = Hᵀ for these terms), so the first move, turned bit by bit, can keep the system only where reversing the flux changes nothing that a turn about Z cannot undo, Φ ≡ −Φ (mod 2π).

**Where they do not**, the look sees every source of the current at a watched bit at order 7:

    m_7 = 96·Σ_l γ_l·(div j)_l²,    at every N ≥ 3 where sin ϑ = 0, at every ϑ from N = 6 on,
                                    and at N = 4, 5 at the other phases computed.

The proof, in four steps; steps 1 to 4 prove the law where sin ϑ = 0, and the end of step 4 says what carries it to other phases.

1. **The twist must act on both sides.** Write Λ(A) = −iT·A for the twist acting on the ket and Ω(A) = −iA·T for the twist acting on the bra, so that its step is Λ − Ω. Let ϑ be 0 or π, so the first move R returns ρ up to the sign. R keeps the steps of H_f and H_e, reflects the watching at the price, and turns the twist's commutator into its anticommutator, Λ + Ω, since X^⊗N·T·X^⊗N = −T. Write the centred generator as L + σ = W + (Λ − Ω), where the even part W satisfies R·W·R = −W†, and note that Λ + Ω equals minus its own adjoint. Then m_k = ⟨Rρ, (L + σ)^k Rρ⟩ = ⟨ρ, (−W† + Λ + Ω)^k ρ⟩; m_k is real, so it equals the conjugate, ⟨ρ, (−W − Λ − Ω)^k ρ⟩, which for odd k is −⟨ρ, (W + Λ + Ω)^k ρ⟩. Averaging with m_k = ⟨ρ, (W + Λ − Ω)^k ρ⟩ cancels every term with an even number of bra steps Ω. The terms odd in T vanish as well, since at ϑ = 0 or π the cat holds only words of even parity and a single twist step changes the parity; so the part of m_k of second order in T, which at k = 7 is all of it (step 2), keeps only one twist step on each side:

        m_k = −Σ_{a+b+c = k−2} ⟨ρ, W^a (Λ W^b Ω + Ω W^b Λ) W^c ρ⟩.

   The look notices the twist only when it runs once on the ket and once on the bra.
2. **The order.** The twist keeps the number and moves nothing on |0…0⟩ or |1…1⟩, so on each side a field step must make an excitation before the twist can move it, and a second must take it away again, back to the corner it started from (from N = 3 on, two steps cannot reach the other corner). Without a rate the look is even in t (step 5 of the proof above). That is 2 twist steps, 4 field steps and 1 rate: order 7 at every N ≥ 3, and nothing of H_e fits.
3. **Where the rate falls.** Ket and bra each return to the corner they started from (step 2), so the cat's four corners |α⟩⟨β| are read one at a time; take |0…0⟩⟨0…0|. Let η = Σ_l h_l|l⟩ be the field's one-excitation pattern and T₁ the twist on one excitation, imaginary and antisymmetric; (T₁η)_l = 2i·(div j)_l/h_l up to one sign common to all bits. The ket's loop through bit u (made at some bit, moved by the twist to u, taken away at u) has the amplitude κ_u = h_u·(T₁η)_u = 2i·(div j)_u, and the sum Σ_u κ_u = ⟨η, T₁η⟩ vanishes because T₁ is antisymmetric. A rate step charges σ less twice the rates of the bits where ket and bra disagree (the cube's height, weighted by the rates): with the ket's excitation at u and the bra's at v it is σ − 2γ_u − 2γ_v + 4γ_u·[u = v]. A rate that falls while one side's loop is closed multiplies Σ κ = 0. Inside both loops, Σ_{u,v} κ_u κ_v·(σ − 2γ_u − 2γ_v + 4γ_u [u = v]) = 4·Σ_u γ_u κ_u² = −16·Σ_u γ_u (div j)_u², because σ and the single charges each meet a free sum Σ κ = 0. Only the bits where ket and bra hold their excitation together survive, weighted by 4γ_u, the charge they escape by agreeing there.
4. **The count.** On the ket the field, the twist and the field act in this order, on the bra likewise, and the one rate falls between. A ket step multiplies by −i; a bra step multiplies by +i for the field and, for the twist, by −i, since step 1 took the twist's own sign on the bra (−Ω) out in front of the sum. So with the rate after both twist steps the ket contributes (−i)³·κ_u = i·κ_u and the bra i·(−i)·i times its loop read from the other side, ⟨η|T₁ = −(T₁η)ᵀ, which is −i·κ_v; the product is κ_u κ_v. A side whose rate falls before its twist step reads its loop at the source instead of the target, which flips that side's sign by the antisymmetry of T₁. Of the 42 orderings of the ket's three steps, the bra's three and the rate with the rate inside both loops, 12 have it after both twist steps, 12 before both, and 9 each one of the two mixed ways, so the weight is 12 + 12 − 9 − 9 = 6, and with the minus sign of step 1, m_7 = −6·(−16)·Σγ(div j)² = 96·Σγ(div j)². The other three corners of the cat give the same value: about |1…1⟩ the hole moves under −T₁, which flips the sign of that side's loop, and on |0…0⟩⟨1…1| and |1…1⟩⟨0…0|, where exactly one side holds a hole, the charge changes sign as well, so the two flips cancel; each corner carries the value of |0…0⟩⟨0…0|, and with weight ¼ each m_7 is that value. Other phases: the cat's phase enters only through its two far corners, e^{±iϑ}, so m_7(ϑ) = a + Re(e^{iϑ}K_1) + Re(e^{2iϑ}K_2). K_2 joins one far corner to the other, 2N field steps, so it is absent from N = 4 on. K_1 joins the quiet part to a far corner, N field steps on one side, and with the twist only on that side it needs an odd number of twist steps and a rate: N + 2 steps, absent from N = 6 on. Equal values at ϑ = 0 and π give Re K_1 = 0, which at N = 4 and 5 leaves Im K_1·sin ϑ; it vanished at every phase gated, no argument for that is known, and the law at other phases rests there on the gates.

With an untwisted hopping and the fields at their own angles (every δ_e = 0) the divergence is a torque: (div j)_l = Σ_m J_lm h_l h_m sin(φ_m − φ_l), up to one sign for all bits, the component along Z of h_l·n_l × Σ_m J_lm h_m·n_m, where n_l is the direction of the field on bit l. At order 7 the look asks whether every watched bit's field lies on the line of the field its neighbours' fields add up to through the coupling.

**What follows.** The property needs m_7 = 0. On an open chain, and on any tree, with every bit watched and every J_e ≠ 0, the law then asks (div j)_l = 0 at every bit; a leaf has one bond, so its current vanishes; removing the leaf, the same holds for the next one, bond by bond: the angles must follow the twist, and then the law above decides the phase. So on such a graph from N = 6 on the cat has the property exactly when every β_e is a multiple of π and sin(Σφ_l − θ) = 0; at N = 4 and 5 the same follows wherever the law holds at the cat's phase, proven at ϑ = 0 and π and gated at the other phases computed; at N = 3 it is proven where sin ϑ = 0 and open elsewhere. At N = 2 the far corner is two field steps away and m_7 has a form of its own; in the frame of the fields, at ϑ = 0,

    m_7 = 16·J²·sin²β·(h_1 + h_2)²·[γ_1(5h_1² − 16h_1h_2 + 17h_2²) + γ_2(17h_1² − 16h_1h_2 + 5h_2²)],

at ϑ = π the same with h_2 → −h_2, both quadratic forms positive definite; where h_2 = −h_1 at ϑ = 0, m_7 vanishes and m_9 = 384·J²·h_1⁴·sin²β·σ³ does not (gated; at ϑ = π with h_2 = h_1 the same follows by turning bit 2 by π, which flips h_2, J and the cat's phase). The phase is already fixed by m_5 = 40·σ·h_1²h_2²·sin²ϑ, which the twist does not enter, so at N = 2 too the cat has the property exactly when the twist is followed and sin ϑ = 0. On a ring the current can circulate without a source, h_a h_b ξ_e the same on every bond; that is the one direction of currents the law cannot see, and on a ring whose flux is no multiple of π the residual twists cannot all be multiples of π, so with every bit watched, wherever the law holds, order 7 leaves exactly this circulation. On the rings where it was computed (N = 4 in the gates, N = 3 and 4 pinned and N = 5 read by the live witness) the circulation breaks at m_9. A bit without watching (γ_l = 0) can hold a source the law does not see.

## Scope and what it is not

- The field lies in the equator plane. A component along the watching does not stop the turn, which commutes with Z; it breaks step 3, since a Z field does not commute with X^⊗N, and with it the first move.
- The proof needs the angle common to all bits once an untwisted coupling hops excitations between them; the strengths may differ, none zero. Angles that differ from bit to bit along a hopping are a twist in the turned frame (section "Under a twisted coupling"). For a coupling that commutes with every Z_l (Ising, ZZ only) the turn may differ from bit to bit, ⊗diag(1, e^{iφ_l}), and the same six steps give the law with Nφ replaced by Σ_l φ_l; with no hopping there is no twist to follow, and the section "Under a twisted coupling" holds this case.
- The coupling must keep the excitation number. Of the couplings that do not commute with X^⊗N only the twisted hopping, a Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya part on a bond, is treated (section "Under a twisted coupling"); others are outside.
- The watching is along Z, at rates γ_l ≥ 0 with σ > 0. At σ = 0 every odd moment vanishes at every angle (step 5).
- Only the cat with a phase is classified. Other states that keep the property under the turned field are named on the parent page (the states the turned first move returns); the whole set is not known.
- "Backwards" is a reading of the identity S_{Rρ}(t) = e^{−2σt}S_ρ(−t), with S_A(t) = Tr(A†e^{tL}A), for the operator Rρ = ρ·X^⊗N with the field along X, which is Rρ = ρ·(cos φ·X + sin φ·Y)^⊗N with the field at φ; it is not a time-reversed run.

## Questions about the formula

- **How the coupling enters beyond step 7.** At identical bits step 7 lets the isotropic part of an anisotropic bond enter from order 2N + 5 on, three coupling factors with a swap between two Z_aZ_b steps; does it, at N ≥ 3, and with which closed form? With unequal rates the isotropic coupling enters: at which order, and is that order set by the differences of the rates?
- **The whole function.** With H_c gone or isotropic (step 7), one rate and one strength on every bit and θ = 0, e^{σt}S(t) = ¼·Σ_{a,b}(e^{γt}·g_{ab}(t))^N, with a and b running over the four cells |i⟩⟨j| of one bit and g_{ab}(t) = ⟨a, e^{tL_1}b⟩, sixteen terms; its odd part is then in closed form for every N. Does the factor G_k of the abstract at J ≠ 0 grow from that product order by order?
- **The order 2N + 1.** The larger the cat, the higher the order in t at which its look can first tell forwards from backwards. Is that the same fact, under another name, as the cat's J-blindness without a field, now with the field doing the 2N steps the coupling cannot do?
- **The cousin.** F120's first firing rung bounds a spectral moment by 2k + 1 from one factor of the dissipator's diagonal and 2k steps of H; here one state, one rate and 2N steps of the field. Is there one statement of which both are faces, a state-resolved moment tower?
- **The circulation on a ring.** A current that circulates without a source, h_a h_b ξ_e the same on every bond, is invisible at order 7, and on a ring whose flux is no multiple of π, with every bit watched, it is all that order 7 leaves. It broke at m_9 where computed (N = 4, one point). Does it break at m_9 always, and with which closed form? Until then the ring is decided in one direction: flux 0 or π with the angles following is enough.
- **Bits without watching.** At γ_l = 0 bit l may hold a source of the current; which twists does the look then miss, and at which order does it see them?
- **N = 3 off ϑ = 0 and π.** There order 7 is also F168's order 2N + 1, and a term linear in the twist joins them; whether the twist must follow at every phase is not shown.
- **N = 4 and 5 off ϑ = 0 and π.** The part Im K_1·sin ϑ, a single twist step from the quiet part to a far corner, vanished at every phase computed; why is not known.
- **The steps one by one.** The gates and the live witness meet the result of the four steps, not the steps.
- **A flight.** The price pair flew GHZ₃ on ibm_marrakesh (fw.Confirmations, price_pair_locality_marrakesh_july2026). A device cannot run t backwards, so the odd part is read only through a fit of S(t) at t ≥ 0 to seventh order, or through the partner Rρ, which is not a state. Is the t⁷ term of GHZ₃ under a transverse field at φ = π/6 (sin 3φ = 1) against φ = 0 within reach of either route at a device's own rates?
