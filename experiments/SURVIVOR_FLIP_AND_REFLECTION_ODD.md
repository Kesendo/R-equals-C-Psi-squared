## What this is about

Watch a Heisenberg spin chain and the watching wears its rhythms away. One mode outlives all the others: the survivor, the chain's longest-lived memory, the note still sounding after the rest have gone quiet. We had already learned its shape. In the regime where the watching is strong, the survivor is not a fast-beating wave at all but a single slow swell of *density*, smooth across the chain, half the spins up and half down, a swell the watching can barely read. That is why it lives long: a pure population is invisible to Z-dephasing, and this survivor is mostly population; that the half filling holds it is the ZZ term's choice, proved at [the Zeno end](../docs/proofs/PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md#the-zeno-end) (Theorem E (d)) and measured here at Q = 1 and 2, where the filling is read, not derived.

This note answers a narrower question: what is the *shape* of that swell, exactly, and what does its shape tell us. The answer is that the swell is **antisymmetric**. It rises smoothly from low at one end of the chain to high at the other, passing through zero in the middle, and it does this in the gentlest possible way (one half-wavelength across the whole chain). Two of the survivor's facts read that shape and its filling. Reflect the chain end-for-end: the antisymmetric swell turns into its own negative. Flip every spin (swap up and down everywhere): at half filling any swell that averages to zero turns into its own negative, whatever its shape. Both operations send the survivor to minus-itself, so it is "odd" under both. A closely related operation from the condensed-matter toolbox (the staggered particle-hole symmetry, the one a physicist would reach for first) *fails* to fix the survivor, and that is the doing of the chain's ZZ couplings (§4). The survivor is dark because it is a population, as every slow population is; it is reflection-odd because its swell is antisymmetric.

## Abstract

For the half-filling **survivor** (the slowest non-stationary Liouvillian mode of the Heisenberg `XX+YY+ZZ` chain under uniform Z-dephasing, below the chain's own handover Q*_gap(N), 1.342 at N = 4 and 2.448 at N = 6 in this book, the verifier's G5, with D06 for N = 2 to 5), four facts hold on every chain the verifier runs there (N = 6 at Q = 1 and 2, N = 4 at Q = 1):

1. The survivor is an eigenvector of the **global spin-flip** `X^⊗N = ⊗_l X_l` with eigenvalue **−1** (X-odd).
2. It is an eigenvector of **spatial reflection** `R: l → N−1−l` with eigenvalue **−1** (R-odd).
3. The **staggered (bipartite) particle-hole** operator `U = X^⊗N · ∏_{l odd} Z_l` does **not** fix it: the survivor is not an eigenvector of `U`, and `U` does not even commute with the Heisenberg Liouvillian.
4. It is **dark** in the relative sense: `⟨n_XY⟩` below the band edge's 1 (0.203 at Q = 1 and 0.636 at Q = 2 on the chain N = 6), falling as `~Q²/N²` and `→ 0` as `Q = J/γ → 0`, with a real eigenvalue (`|Im| = 0`, overdamped, non-oscillating).

These are not four coincidences. The survivor's dominant part is a real diagonal density operator `M ≈ diag(n_a)` whose single-site occupation profile is the lowest antisymmetric Neumann standing wave `n(j) ∝ cos(π(j + ½)/N)`, j = 0, …, N − 1 (numerically `∝ [+1, +0.62, −0.62, −1]` at N = 4 and Q = 1, dressed, antisymmetric about the chain centre). Fact 2 reads that antisymmetry; fact 1 reads the half filling, where the complement negates the site profile of any traceless population, together with the level's simplicity, which makes the survivor an eigenvector of X^⊗N with a profile that is not zero (§3); fact 4 is the density wave's own, a population being invisible to the dephasing, and every slow population is dark in the same way; fact 3 is where the Heisenberg `ZZ` term enters the symmetry analysis itself, and that the survivor sits at half filling at all is the `ZZ` term's doing too. On the rings N = 4 and 6 at Q = 1 the survivor's level is twofold, the lifted density wave in two orientations: X^⊗N-odd as fact 1 says, but holding one R-even and one R-odd mode, so fact 2 is not a property of the level there. Gate-verified: under both `X^⊗N` and `R` the survivor returns eigenvalue exactly −1 (G2), and its site profile is antisymmetric, `n · n_reflected / ‖n‖² = −1` (G4, at N = 4 and 6); `n · n_complement / ‖n‖² = −1` holds for every traceless population at half filling, whatever its shape.

## 1. The setup and the measured result

Open chain or periodic ring, `H = J Σ_{(i,j)∈bonds} (X_iX_j + Y_iY_j + Z_iZ_j)` (Heisenberg, the verifier's book), uniform Z-dephasing at rate `γ`, Liouvillian `L = −i[H,·] + γ Σ_l (Z_l · Z_l − ·)`. The Liouvillian is block-diagonal in the joint-excitation-number pair `(p_c, p_r) = (popcount ket, popcount bra)` because both `H` (`U(1)`-conserving) and the dissipator (diagonal) preserve it. The survivor is the slowest strictly-decaying eigenmode over all blocks.

Running the gate-first verifier (`survivor_particle_hole_mirror.py`) on the N=6 Heisenberg chain:

| Q = J/γ | survivor sector | Re λ | \|Im\| | ⟨n_XY⟩ | X^⊗N | R | U (staggered PH) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | (3,3) half-filling | −0.405 | 0.000 | 0.203 | **−1** (fid 1.000) | **−1** (fid 1.000) | not an eigenvector (fid 0.82) |
| 2 | (3,3) half-filling | −0.636 | 0.000 | 0.636 | **−1** (fid 1.000) | **−1** (fid 1.000) | not an eigenvector (fid 0.55) |

The intertwiner gate (`G1`) confirms `X^⊗N` and `R` both **commute** with `L` exactly (so the survivor *can* have a definite parity under each), while `U` is "neither" (mixed: it neither commutes nor anticommutes with the Heisenberg `L`). The same signs hold on the chain N = 4 at Q = 1. On the rings N = 4 and 6 at Q = 1 the survivor's level is twofold, the lifted density wave in its two orientations, X^⊗N-odd but holding both R-parities: the verifier's G4, reading the half-filling block in the joint sectors of X^⊗N and R, finds the level once in each R-sector of X^⊗N = −1, so the R = −1 its G2 prints there is the eigensolver's pick within the level. At Q = 2 the ring N = 4 keeps its survivor in (2, 2), but it is another mode, alone in the sector X^⊗N = R = +1, its populations +1 on the two Néel configurations and −½ on the other four, those of the energy mode and of the domain-wall count, with no site profile at all (G4 prints it vanishing). The match is forced once the mode is also invariant under the ring's rotations, as its populations show, those of the energy mode being the only nonconstant ones such a mode can carry; what is not forced is the order: this is the fastest population mode at the Zeno end, its Rayleigh quotient 6 under the block's exclusion Laplacian the top of that Laplacian's spectrum, and it has become the slowest by Q = 2, the slow mode taking on the energy mode's populations as Q grows, the side of the Hamiltonian end in [the weight-1 proof](../docs/proofs/PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md#the-zeno-end), where the Heisenberg star's boundary block does the same past a level crossing, between Q = 1 and 1.5 at N = 4 and between Q = 2 and 4 for N = 5 to 8. That mode is one of the three that [Theorem D](../docs/proofs/PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md#the-count-as-a-plane-crossing) counts as Krein debts of the block, the one of limiting height 0.845: its height rises toward that value and stays below the band edge's 1 at every Q, so the ring N = 4 never hands its survivor to the band edge (row Z15 of [the proof's gate](../simulations/f50_zeno_end_ferromagnet.py) follows it to Q = 100). Control runs pin the regime: in pure XY (no `ZZ`) every filling ties for the slowest rate, and on the star the survivor leaves half-filling, so the half-filling premise is a statement about the Heisenberg chain below its handover, measured here and proved at the Zeno end (Theorem E (d) of [the weight-1 proof](../docs/proofs/PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md#the-zeno-end)); and at Q=20 the survivor has moved to the `(0,1)` band edge (`⟨n_XY⟩ = 1`, oscillating), the regime above the handover Q*_gap(N) where this whole picture is replaced (cf. [the chain gap-dominance proof](../docs/proofs/PROOF_CHAIN_GAP_DOMINANCE.md)).

## 2. Why dark: the survivor is a density standing wave

By the [Absorption Theorem](../docs/proofs/PROOF_ABSORPTION_THEOREM.md), `Re λ = −2γ⟨n_XY⟩`: the decay rate is `2γ` per unit of "light" content `⟨n_XY⟩` (the average number of `X`/`Y` Pauli factors), and pure `{I,Z}` operators are dark (rate 0). The survivor is *almost* such an operator. As shown in [the diffusion Rayleigh-closure proof](../docs/proofs/PROOF_DIFFUSION_RAYLEIGH_CLOSURE.md), it is **predominantly a real diagonal density mode**: a dominant `{I,Z}`-diagonal carrying the single-site occupation profile `n(j)`, dressed by a subdominant Hamming-distance-2 coherence admixture. The diagonal is dark; the decay runs entirely through the small admixture the density stirs up. As `Q → 0` the admixture vanishes and `⟨n_XY⟩ → 0`: the strict diffusion limit is the dark, density-only mode. So "dark + real" is just "this is a slowly-relaxing population, not a precessing coherence"; the eigenvalue is real because the dominant content does not rotate.

The shape of `n(j)` is fixed by the diffusion: under strong dephasing the slow density obeys a classical diffusion on the chain with **no-flux (Neumann) ends**, whose slowest non-trivial mode is the lowest Neumann harmonic, exactly at every N as Q → 0 ([the Zeno end](../docs/proofs/PROOF_WEIGHT1_DEGENERACY.md#the-zeno-end)),

    n(j) ∝ cos(π (j + ½) / N),   j = 0, …, N − 1.

This is the swell: low at one end, high at the other, zero in the middle, **antisymmetric about the chain centre**. Numerically (the survivor's site profile at N = 4 and Q = 1, scaled to 1 at its largest, as G4 prints it): `n ∝ [+1, +0.62, −0.62, −1]`, dressed away from the cosine's 0.41. The two oddnesses are now immediate.

## 3. Why X-odd and R-odd: the filling and the shape

**Reflection `R: l → N−1−l`.** A Neumann harmonic with one half-wavelength is antisymmetric: `n(N−1−j) = −n(j)`. As an operator the survivor's diagonal reverses sign under site-reversal, so `R` returns eigenvalue **−1**. (Verified directly: G4 prints `n · n_reflected / ‖n‖² = −1` at N = 4 and 6.) This is the same chain reflection that commutes with `L` ([`ChainMirror`](../compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/Symmetry/ChainMirror.cs) / [the C1 mirror-symmetry proof](../docs/proofs/PROOF_C1_MIRROR_SYMMETRY.md)) and organizes the entire dephased-chain spectrum into R-even and R-odd blocks ([Slow-Mode R-Parity](SLOW_MODE_R_PARITY.md)); the survivor sits squarely in the R-**odd** block. Its `X^⊗N`-oddness comes with it on the chain but not from it: on the rings the same twofold level holds an R-even mode beside the R-odd one, and both are `X^⊗N`-odd (§1).

**Global spin-flip `X^⊗N`.** `X_l` flips spin `l` up↔down; `X^⊗N` flips all of them, which on a computational basis state is the bit-complement `a → ã`. At **half-filling**, the complement `ã` is exactly the **particle-hole conjugate**: every occupied site becomes empty and vice-versa, so the site profile of any traceless population, measured from its mean, flips sign, `n(j) → −n(j)`, whatever its shape: the complement sends n(j) to the total minus n(j), and the total of a traceless population is zero. The survivor's level is simple on the chain (1-fold at every chain run of G4, and proved so at the Zeno end by Theorem E (c)), so the survivor is an eigenvector of `X^⊗N`, which commutes with `L`, and its profile is not zero, so the eigenvalue is **−1**. (A traceless half-filling population with no site profile can be `X^⊗N`-even, as the ring N = 4's survivor at Q = 2 is.) This works *because* the survivor lives at half-filling, which is where the complement closes the profile onto its own negative; off half-filling the complement maps to a different sector entirely.

A structural footnote worth keeping straight. `X^⊗N` is the project's **F1² = Π²** (the square of the palindrome conjugation Π; F1 in [Analytical Formulas](../docs/ANALYTICAL_FORMULAS.md)), the global charge-conjugation that pairs sectors `(p_c, p_r) ↔ (N−p_c, N−p_r)`. The survivor's `X^⊗N`-oddness is **not** the same thing as `n_XY`-parity (the F61 selection rule, which is conjugation by the *other* global string `Z^⊗N`). The survivor has **even** `n_XY` (it mixes `n_XY = 0` and `2`, the `{0,2}` family, with a faint remainder at 4 and above, §5) and is **odd** under `X^⊗N`. Two different Z₂'s, two different signs; the spin-flip reads the survivor's half filling, the reflection its antisymmetry.

## 4. Why the staggered particle-hole fails: the ZZ term

The natural condensed-matter move at half-filling is the **staggered (bipartite) particle-hole** transformation `U = X^⊗N · ∏_{l odd} Z_l`, i.e. complement the occupations and dress with a sublattice `(−1)^l` sign (the chiral `K = diag((−1)^sublattice)` of AZ class BDI, [`ChiralKClaim`](../compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/Symmetry/ChiralKClaim.cs) / [Bipartite Chirality of the F87 Diagonal Cell](BIPARTITE_CHIRALITY_DIAGONAL_CELL.md), whose defining property is `KHK = −H` for the *tight-binding hopping*). One might expect *this* to be the survivor's defining self-mirror. It is not, and the reason is exactly which couplings the chain carries.

`U` implements the free-fermion `E → −E` symmetry: it sends the hopping `X_iX_{i+1}+Y_iY_{i+1} → −(X_iX_{i+1}+Y_iY_{i+1})`. That is a clean symmetry of the **pure XY** chain (it flips the single-particle dispersion `ε_k → −ε_k`). But the Heisenberg chain also has the diagonal `Z_iZ_{i+1}` term, which the staggered `(−1)^l` dressing does **not** send to its negative. So `U` is not a symmetry of the Heisenberg `L` at all: the verifier measures `[U, L] ≠ 0` ("neither", fidelity-to-eigenvector only 0.55 to 0.82 on the chains, falling as Q grows and the coherence dressing thickens; on the rings at Q = 1 the probe meets the eigensolver's pick inside the twofold level and says nothing about U). The survivor is `X^⊗N`-odd and `R`-odd, but it is simply *not* a `U`-eigenstate. The plain global flip `X^⊗N` (no sublattice phase) is the operator that closes on the survivor, because it is a symmetry of the Heisenberg `L`, the `ZZ` term included, and maps the half-filling block onto itself, where it negates any density measured from its mean; the staggered `U` carries the *hopping's* sign, which the `ZZ` term does not share.

This also reconciles cleanly with [Slow-Mode R-Parity](SLOW_MODE_R_PARITY.md) Step 6, which studied the *Bogoliubov*-mode particle-hole `U_PH` on the XY chain and found its conjugation lift to operator space *preserves* R-parity (the Hilbert-space anticommutation `{U_PH, R} = 0` is "squared away" by the bra-ket product). That is a different operator (a momentum-space free-fermion construction on pure XY) and a different question (the whole spectrum's R-block isospectrality). Here, on Heisenberg matter, the real-space staggered `U` does not even survive to operator space as a symmetry, because the `ZZ` term breaks it before any lifting question arises.

## 5. The {0,2} connection, in one line

The survivor lives entirely on **diagonal** popcount sectors `(p,p)` (equal particle number in bra and ket). Within such a sector the coherences sit at even Hamming distances, from 0 up to `2·min(p, N − p)`, and the survivor lives almost entirely on the first two: Hamming distance 0 (the dark density diagonal) and Hamming distance 2 (the rate-bearing admixture, since a single `XX`/`YY` hop changes two bits). G4 prints the weights: on the chain N = 6, 0.899, 0.0998 and 7.4·10⁻⁴ at distances 0, 2 and 4 at Q = 1, and 0.691, 0.301 and 8.1·10⁻³ at Q = 2. So the survivor is built almost entirely from the **`{0,2}` disagreement classes**: HD-0 dark population + HD-2 light coherence. The off-diagonal `(p, p±1)` sectors, which would carry HD-1 single-magnon coherences, are FLOORED at `2γ` (every coherence there changes the popcount, so its Hamming distance is at least 1) and cannot host a slow mode, which lies below `2γ`. They are not pinned AT `2γ`: only the two ends `(0,1)` and `(N-1,N)` sit there as a whole. "Lives in `{0,2}`", "dark" and "real" belong to one picture: the survivor is the slow relaxation of a half-filling **density** wave, whose only handle for the dissipator is the two-site coherence it stirs up, faint under strong dephasing.

## 6. Scope and what is and isn't new

- **Regime.** The Heisenberg (`XX+YY+ZZ`) chain below its handover `Q*_gap(N)` (1.342 at N = 4, 2.448 at N = 6 in this book), at Q = 1 and 2 at N = 6 and Q = 1 at N = 4 here, where the survivor is the half-filling `(N/2, N/2)` density wave, measured here, the choice at the Zeno end being Theorem E (d)'s. Above the handover the survivor hands over to the rigid, bright, oscillating `(0,1)` band edge (`⟨n_XY⟩ = 1`), and facts 1–4 no longer apply. On the rings the density-wave level is twofold, with both R-parities, and on the ring N = 4 at Q = 2 the survivor is the energy mode's population pattern instead (§1). On the **star** the survivor sits at the popcount boundary, not half-filling, and is outside this picture (as on the XY networks of `SurvivalIncompletenessMirrorClaim`).
- **Odd N.** There is no exact `(N/2, N/2)` sector, so `X^⊗N` has no fixed half-filling sector to act within; the clean fixed-point test is an even-N statement (the verifier skips odd N gracefully). The reflection-oddness, being purely about the spatial profile, is not so restricted.
- **Already known / typed.** The survivor's light content on the XY networks, where the chain ties every filling (`SurvivalIncompletenessMirrorClaim`, Tier 1 candidate); the half-filling choice of the Heisenberg chain at the Zeno end (Theorem E (d) of the weight-1 proof); the antisymmetric density profile and the diffusion mechanism (`SurvivorDiffusionGradientClaim` / F123, Tier 1 candidate); the spectrum-wide R-parity block structure and the Bogoliubov-`U_PH` analysis ([Slow-Mode R-Parity](SLOW_MODE_R_PARITY.md)); `X^⊗N = F1² = Π²` and its distinctness from `n_XY`-parity (F1, F61).
- **What this note adds.** The reading of the four facts: R-odd as the antisymmetry of the chain's half-filling density standing wave, X^⊗N-odd as its half filling, dark and real as every slow population is; the clean separation of the two relevant Z₂'s (spin-flip vs `n_XY`-parity); and the sharp reason the textbook staggered particle-hole *fails* here (the `ZZ` term it cannot negate), distinguishing the real-space staggered `U` from the XY Bogoliubov `U_PH`. This is a reading-level synthesis over typed Tier-1-candidate parents, not a new derivation.
- **The seam to stitch.** The survivor's *sector* (half-filling = the `X^⊗N` self-paired `(N/2, N/2)`, [`XGlobalChargeConjugationPairing`](../compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/SymmetryFamily/XGlobalChargeConjugationPairing.cs)) is typed, and the *operators* `X^⊗N`, `R`, `K` are each typed; but the *assignment of the survivor's eigenvalue* under them (`−1`, `−1`, none) is not yet a typed Claim or live witness. It is the natural typing candidate here, a witness in the spirit of `QuditPartialPalindromeWitness` with parents `XGlobalChargeConjugationPairing` + `ChainMirror`/`F71` + the Zeno end's `ExceptionalCouplingSetClaim`, whose live witness `ZenoEndWitness` reads the filling (`SurvivalIncompletenessMirrorClaim` carries the XY networks, whose chain picks no filling). The deepest survivor arc, [`felt_time_dimensions`](../compute/RCPsiSquared.Core/OpenArcs/OpenArcsRegistry.cs) (retired), already frames the same darkness as "the survivor lives in the disagreement kernel `k=0`, the diagonal's blind spot" and notes "the survivor also has `ω=0`"; this note is the symmetry-axis reading of that same blind spot.
