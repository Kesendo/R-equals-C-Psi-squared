## The claim under test, and the fence

Under local noise of any shape (Markovian Z-dephasing, quasi-static Gaussian dephasing, local relaxation), a coherence whose bra and ket differ on the site set D and agree in |0⟩ everywhere else pays, at every instant, the product of what its sites pay, each normalized to its own preparation:

    E_D(t)/E_D(0) = Π_{j∈D} E_j(t)/E_j(0)          (pointwise, shape-free)
    Γ(D)          = Σ_{j∈D} Γ_j                      (as fitted rates)

The rate form is Theorem 2 of the proof read at J = 0, with the relaxation half carried by F82 to F84 (amplitude damping on a flipped site j multiplies the coherence by e^{−t/(2T1_j)}). The pointwise form is stronger than the theorem's eigenmode statement and follows from factorization: a product of single-site channels acts on |bra⟩⟨ket| as a product of single-site factors. The clause "agree in |0⟩ everywhere else" is load-bearing and is why every carrier here puts its spectators in the ground state: a site outside D that is excited on both sides pays its full relaxation e^{−t/T1} on top (the Z letter of amplitude damping charges γ while adding nothing to n_XY; at T1 = 281 µs and t = 80 µs that is 0.285 in ln E), and a spectator that is only PARTLY excited, even by 4 %, passes a ⟨Z⟩ ≥ 0.9 guard and still adds a time-dependent ZZ mask (−0.053 in ln E at 80 µs on the July parameters). No ideal carrier in this ladder is of that kind; the real preparations are qualified against it (below). F70 (the rate belongs to the pattern, not the carrier) is why an entangled Bell pair and a product-state pair correlator are the same object here.

What this flight answers is the site-resolved sum on a line whose three T2* differ; the proof's sentence asks in the uniform form (twice the rate). The flight answers the generalization the sentence is a special case of, and the sentence will be rewritten as that, not as an answer to the uniform question. A HOLDS is compatibility with the reference model at the qualified sensitivity; a VIOLATED is a measured departure from it whose cause (covariance, a populated spectator, drift, a calibration defect) is a diagnostic question, never an automatic label.

**The fence.** J = 0 throughout: no coupling is switched on, so no fractional ⟨n_XY⟩ is measured. That is stage C, a separate design that is not registered anywhere; it needs its own future design and explicit arc decision after the concentrator's 0.73× magnitude compression is understood from below.

**Predecessors.** The price pair established the observable, the readout mitigation, the exp·Gauss shape rule, the fringe guard, the ZZ discriminator and the boundary rule. The staircase established the day-of T2* rule and the in-situ relaxation block, and showed twice that endpoint checks are not enough. The fold shadow established that a gate-heavy ladder drowns its fast rung.

## Model and estimand

The reference model: no programmed exchange (J = 0); diagonal ZZ on all three bonds including the next-nearest (0,2); independent local Markovian Z-dephasing; independent quasi-static Gaussian detunings; local relaxation without heating; ideal ground-state spectators. For D ∈ {01, 12, 012} the envelope E_D is the magnitude of the selected complex coherence, scaled to one for the ideal carrier. Then r_D(t) = ln E_D(t) − Σ_{j∈D} ln E_j(t) is constant under the reference; a constant preparation amplitude changes its intercept; the fitted slope b_D is predicted zero, and a departure can also carry curvature. Mixed or excited spectators change the time dependence under ZZ and relaxation, and preparation errors are not in general constant amplitude losses; both are qualified through the full preparation and readout path, not assumed away.

The bare ZZ parameter ζ_H,b (kHz) enters H as +(2π·ζ_H,b·10⁻³/4)·Z_aZ_b (rad/µs). The conditional-Ramsey instrument fits arg(⟨X⟩ + i⟨Y⟩) and reports the excited-spectator minus ground-spectator frequency; in the no-relaxation limit that measured shift equals −ζ_H,b (the ρ₀₁ phase has the opposite sign, which is where an earlier review fixture went wrong). The measured shift and the bare parameter are stored as distinct signed fields; the cosine mask is even in ζ and cannot detect this sign, so the translation is qualified by direct state evolution before any inversion.

## Committed estimator (to be qualified, then frozen)

1. **Counts to observables.** Raw counts and the temporal metadata are persisted before any reduction. Readout confusion per qubit from both CAL brackets (the stationary reference is their mean); tensor-product inversion is a model assumption, qualified rather than assumed. For observed probabilities p, confusion C and a parity vector s, the observable is read through w = C^{−T}s and its variance, conditional on C, is [Σ p w² − (Σ p w)²]/shots (an earlier draft wrote (1 − v²)/shots on the MITIGATED v, which is zero at v = 1 where the observed variance is 5.2·10⁻⁶ at 8192 shots, and negative above it). Calibration uncertainty is propagated with the actual derivatives ((1+v)/a and (v−1)/a for one qubit, a = 1 − ε01 − ε10) and the shared covariance the two brackets induce across arms and delays.
2. **Envelopes, fringe-free.** For a single, E_j = |⟨X_j⟩ + i⟨Y_j⟩|; for a Bell pair, 2|c| from the four settings; for the GHZ, the two quadratures. Debiasing uses the observation-model covariance; σ_E and σ_lnE follow, and their behaviour near the keep floor is measured through the actual readout path, not assumed Gaussian. The statistics are written in BAND UNITS (b/σ̂_b), never called sigmas; the gate prints the null spread over the nominal σ̂ for every statistic.
3. **The keep rule.** A delay enters a fit only where every envelope in the expression exceeds 0.04 (at 0.02 the debiased estimator was biased +0.34 in ln E by its own truncation, with a δ-method σ twice too large). t = 0 is excluded from the pointwise fits (a rate fit may use its amplitude). Fewer than 5 kept delays withholds the affected comparison.
4. **R1, two statistics per D.** r_D(t) = a + b·t by weighted least squares: the LEVEL |b/σ̂_b|, explicitly two-sided, and the SHAPE χ²_line (the lack of fit to the line; correlated quasi-static dephasing enters as −β·t², the shape the July campaign measured at β ≈ 4·10⁻⁴/µs² and preferred over a line at 40:1). The signed slope and the intercept are reported.
5. **The rate reading, descriptive.** ln E = ln A − Γt − B·t² with the quadratic term always present and B unconstrained and signed (no data-dependent choice between linear and quadratic; the July AIC rule is a cliff on the unknown K); K = √B only for B ≥ 0, otherwise K is not real; a negative finite B is a diagnostic, not a failed fit. Γ(D) − ΣΓ_j, its uncertainty and the fit diagnostics are reported, never gated.
6. **R2, one statistic per masked single.** r_q(t) = ln E_q^M − ln E_q^S against the exact finite-T1 three-qubit curve built from the session's inverted ZZ, T1 and T2*; one constant offset per site (banded and reported); the SHAPE is the reduced χ² of the residual. The curve has no parameter adjusted to the R2 data; it does carry the session's calibrated inputs with their uncertainty, sampled in the null through the same calibration fit (never as independent normal draws of quantities that share a fit). The closed form Σ_{b at q} ln|cos(π ζ_b t)| over every bond the qubit sits on is the infinite-T1 limit used as a check, not the predictor.
7. **Side quantities**, reported, never gated: the uniform ratios ⟨Γ_pair⟩/⟨Γ_single⟩ and Γ_GHZ/⟨Γ_single⟩; T2*_j against the calibration T2; the session's ZZ against July's if the line is the July line; the fitted K per site against the ZZ block's; the B-block T1 against calibration.

## The ZZ inverse, and the preparation families

Bare ZZ is inferred through the COMPLETE forward measurement (simultaneous ZZ and relaxation dynamics on the actual n0/n1/f1 preparations, the readout, the phase detrending, the free-intercept slope fit and the envelope selection the analysis uses), inverted on an explicit, qualified, identifiable domain with refusal on ambiguity, inadequate retained points or excessive uncertainty; the multiplicative attenuation depends on the bare ZZ itself (0.908 at 3.9 kHz, 0.913 at 4.66, 0.945 at 8 kHz on the July grid at T1 = 281 µs in the two-site fixture; the July record's single 0.84 is a comparison value, not a target). The local fixture the gate pins: a measured −3.918 kHz inverts to a bare parameter of +4.301 kHz in that two-site model; the full three-site inverse including (0,2) is qualified from synthetic counts. T1 is read by the bracket, and its fitted uncertainty (about 0.8 % on the planning inputs) enters the inversion through its Jacobian; the qualification draws a measured T1 per replica and inverts at the drawn value, and the null draws the same T1 once per replica for the inversion and for the main-batch prediction, so the two errors are correlated rather than added.

Preparation errors are qualified in two families: an amplitude-only loss with ground spectators preserved must leave the R1 level and shape null (the statistical null control); a populated spectator (0.96|0⟩⟨0| + 0.04|1⟩⟨1|) passes the ⟨Z⟩ ≥ 0.9 guard and adds a time-dependent mask, so the measured spectator populations and their uncertainty are used to BOUND that contribution, and a covariance reading is withheld when the bound is inadequate. A doubled Bell depolarization (0.08 → 0.16) reads ⟨Z⟩ = 0.84 on its spectator and must WITHHOLD through the guard; it is a refusal control, not a must-not-fire case. The candidate depolarizing losses (S 0.02, P 0.08, G 0.15; the May Dicke preparation reached 88 % of its ceiling with one CX) are planning models.

Temporal inconsistency withholds the whole main verdict.
- **Sensitivity** is a POWER: the smallest common-mode and the smallest single-bond dose giving at least 90 % probability of a valid VIOLATED, with Monte Carlo uncertainty, withheld cases counted separately. In this gate's book a covariance c multiplies a coherence that flips both ends of a bond by e^{−c·t}, so c here = 2·c_ij in the July price-pair book. On the July clean-session covariances (c₀₁ = −0.0010 ± 0.0006, c₀₂ = +0.0002, c₁₂ = +0.0025 ± 0.0005 /µs in July's book, the last at 2.2σ and never confirmed) this instrument would read c₁₂ near its VIOLATED level: R1 at this sensitivity is a covariance tomograph at least as much as an additivity test, and a clean HOLDS on all three D is a finding about the device, not the expected default.

**Diagnostic table for an R1 departure** (hypotheses to be separated by the controls, never a mechanism read off a three-number pattern):

| Signature | Hypothesis |
|---|---|
| all three levels shifted together, same sign, the GHZ largest (about 3:1:1), shapes clean | Markovian common-mode dephasing (all-to-all covariance, linear in t) |

**What would falsify what.** R1 HOLDS on every D is site-resolved additivity landing as a registered number with its error bar, the proof's sentence then rewritten to what is true. R1 VIOLATED does not falsify the theorem: it is a departure from the qualified reference model, decoded by the table into a device property only where the controls exclude the alternatives, exactly as the July campaign did. R2 CONFIRMED is the boundary rule read with no parameter fitted to the curve; R2 OFF is a device reading, never folded into R1. Two arms, two verdicts, each decoded on its own.

## Honesty notes

- Not modelled: leakage to |2⟩, heating (the July heating leg measured γ↑ ≤ 2.2·10⁻⁵/µs; a heating spectator's mask is 5·10⁻³ in ln at 80 µs, a tenth of a point σ), correlated amplitude damping. The first would show as structure in the fit residuals, the last in the B-block asymptote. None is a licence to move a band after the fact.
- The δ-method σ near the floor runs the wrong way (the keep rule truncates the low tail, biasing kept values upward and overstating their σ), which is why the bands are empirical quantiles of the counts-level null and not χ² theory, and why the statistics are written in band units.
- The R2 curve is not parameter-free in knowledge: the session's ZZ (σ ≈ 0.1 kHz) shifts it by 0.03 to 0.06 in ln at 80 µs on the July line, the inverse's own error by more; both are inside the bands because the null samples them through the same calibration fit.
- The K vector and the T2* prior are provenance-bearing planning inputs; the ZZ block's Ramsey is the session measurement that replaces them before the main go, and an input outside the qualified domain withholds rather than being re-gated after the fact.
- The number of gated comparisons is nine, correlated; the bands are calibrated on the family, validated on separate replicas, and the withheld fraction is reported unconditionally.
- Cost is not established until the July job records are read; the concentrator's 0.283 ms per shot is a measurement of another workload.
