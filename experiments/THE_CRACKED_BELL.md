## The three closed forms (first order in δ, gates A-C; the split is spectral and block-independent, the reversal and the wall read on the (0,1) block, the circulation clock on the (1,1) one)

Degenerate perturbation theory on the pair {ψ_m, ψ_{−m}} with the perturbation −δJ(|N−1⟩⟨0| + |0⟩⟨N−1|) gives the 2×2 matrix −δJ·(2/N)·[[cos θ_m, e^(iθ_m)], [e^(−iθ_m), cos θ_m]], θ_m = 2πm/N, and everything follows from its eigensystem:

1. **The flat split.** Every degenerate pair splits by the same ΔE = 4δJ/N, independent of m (m = 0 and, at even N, m = N/2 are unpaired and shift instead; the gate excludes them for that reason), because |ψ_m(j)|² = 1/N: a point defect is flat in mode space, every partial of the bell hears the crack equally. The partners are the crack-adapted standing waves cos/sin(θ_m(j + ½)), antinode respectively node centred on the cracked bond, shifted by −(2δJ/N)(cos θ_m ± 1) in opposite directions. Gated by the decade-ratio law (the error e(δ) on ΔE/δ shrinks linearly, decade ratios inside [0.098, 0.102] across δ = 1e-5..1e-3, over the seven N 6, 7, 8, 12, 13, 16 and 32, odd and even; that is a grid and not a sweep, and the difference matters, because the band is a statement about the leading *correction* and must fail where that correction vanishes, at N ≈ 19.03 for m = 1, which the gate beside it checks on purpose). 2. **The reversal.** Launch the *perfect* ring's traveling wave on the cracked ring and the split partners beat. Two clocks come out of that beat and they sit on different blocks: the circulation's zero crossing at T_zero = πN/(8δJ) is a (1,1) reading (gate D), while the counter-wave overlap that gives T_rev is a (0,1) one (gate B). 3. **The visibility wall.** With dephasing, the reversed amplitude carries the exact envelope e^(−2γ·T_rev) = exp(−πN/(2Qδ)), Q = J/γ. Exact in the γ-factorization (the scalar block), exponent accurate to O(δ) relative. That document is *"Tier: 2/3"* and the section cited is its Tier 2 one, whose measured law is FI ≈ c·Q for a bond defect on an N = 4 XY chain, worst residual ~15%, so it is a frame here rather than support, and the defect half of the composition is already its own: the split resolves when Q·δ clears ~πN/(2·ln(1/ε)).

**What this buys the proof.** PROOF_RING_GAP_DOMINANCE §Scope shows the exact-floor observable dropping the N = 4 exception at δ = 1e-4 and says the underlying coherence "has simply left that set". The *reversal* read of the same crack is exponentially blind below δ ≈ πN/(2Q·ln(1/ε)) (≈ 0.07 at N = 4, Q = 20, ε = 1%); the early beat deficit is *polynomially* blind instead. Three different bars on three different quantities; the near-coincidence of the first two is not a relation between them. ## The reborn inherit the clock: F126's ladder on the ring (gate: `ring_renewal_current.py`)

**The law.** Write E = 2J·cos k and u = J′/J = 1 − δ. Matching the plane-wave ansatz across the cracked bond, ψ_(−1) = u·ψ_(N−1) and ψ_N = u·ψ_0, and setting the 2×2 determinant to zero gives one function of k whose zero set on the open interval (0, π) is the whole spectrum, at every δ (G vanishes at k = 0 and π for free, which is why the interiority argument below is needed):

    G(k) = (1 − u²)·sin(Nk)·cos k + [(1 + u²)·cos(Nk) − 2u]·sin k = 0
         = sin((N+1)k) − u²·sin((N−1)k) − 2u·sin k

Gate E1a puts every level on that curve: one Newton step along G moves each eigenvalue by at most 1.8e-14, over 48 sampled (N, δ) points spanning N = 4 to 40 and δ = 0.001 to 0.999.
